EXACT CONDITIONAL THEOREM

Assume s=e+pi is algebraic. If E-functions F,G and nonzero algebraic
x,y satisfy F(x)=e and G(y)=pi, then there are finite Borel
singularities rho_F in S(F), rho_G in S(G) such that

    rho_F/x = rho_G/y.

In particular, for F(z)=exp(z), x=1, every E-function G with G(y)=pi
satisfies y in S(G). This follows by rescaling to the common point 1
and applying the contrapositive of Delaygue's Theorem 2.1.

EXACT LOGARITHM THEOREM

If an E-function F satisfies F(x)=exp(eta) for nonzero algebraic
x,eta, then x/eta is in S(F). Hence every exact algebraic logarithm
construction is automatically in the exceptional branch of the
Fischler--Rivoal logarithm theorem.

MIXED VALUE-RING REDUCTION

Under s=e+pi algebraic, e and pi are transcendental elements of both
the E-value ring and the G-value ring. Therefore any of

    E-values intersect G-values only in algebraic numbers,
    e is not a G-value,
    pi is not an E-value

would refute the hypothesis. None is proved by the audited primary
theorems. Functional E/G independence and functional class
intersections do not specialize to this complex value statement.

BEUKERS REGULAR-INTERPOLATION NO-GO

The 2026 E-function interpolation theorem can supply separate minimal
homogeneous equations regular at the evaluation point; their companion
systems form a common regular direct sum. Under s=e+pi algebraic,
Beukers' specialization theorem then lifts the value relation and
forces a functional linear relation among the components of every such
direct-sum system. These components generally include derivatives, so
this does not by itself prove that 1 and the two interpolants are
functionally dependent. The missing input is componentwise
independence of a closed common first-order vector, not regularity.

CURRENT MEASURE/PERIOD CHECK

The Adamczewski--Faverjon E-value measure is an exact alternative:
P(values)=0 or a quantitative lower bound. Under the hypothesis,
the integer polynomial m_s(X+Y) is exactly zero at (e,pi), so the
measure stays in its allowed zero branch. Snodgrass's August 2026 v2
E-period theorem constructs cyclotomic rational structures and period
pairings, but gives no injectivity, nonvanishing, or transcendence
theorem for the relevant matrix coefficients.

VERDICT

No audited existing theorem, with all hypotheses verified, proves or
disproves that e+pi is algebraic. The precise survivors are:

1. Borel-support separation for an exact E/E representation;
2. an archimedean mixed E/G value-specialization theorem;
3. the E-value/G-value intersection conjecture; or
4. a genuinely joint algebraic-independence theorem for logarithms of
   E-values.

No main-problem proof is claimed.
