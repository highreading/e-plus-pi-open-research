# Start here

Read [the current state](docs/PROJECT_STATE.md), [the irrationality criterion](docs/IRRATIONALITY_CRITERION.md), and [the errata](reviews/ERRATA_AND_SCOPE.md) first. Then choose one route from [results and routes](docs/RESULTS_AND_ROUTES.md). Loading every archived report into one AI context is unnecessary and makes source checking harder.

For a first contribution, select a bounded audit task in [open tasks](docs/OPEN_TASKS.md). Follow its context links and reconstruct the exact definitions before evaluating the claim. State whether you are checking a finite identity, an infinite-family theorem, a normalization, or a missing implication. The output should make that distinction visible.

If you are considering a route already known to fail, read its [full exclusion proof](no_go/README.md) and check that your proposed change actually leaves its hypotheses. A renamed notation or a different integer clearer of the same ratios may not change the construction.

The repository has two reading levels. The top-level guides and task board are the small orientation packet. `sources/`, `work/`, `research/`, `reviews/` and `results/` preserve the larger research and evidence corpus. The [file index](docs/FILE_INDEX.md) and [offline catalog](CATALOG.html) make that corpus searchable.

Historical reports retain their original claims and checkpoint dates. They are accompanied by a notice pointing to current status and errata. An old ACTIVE label is historical; no client is running as part of this release. An old PASS label certifies only its recorded scope, if supported by its review.

Read [contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md), create a bounded issue, and submit a complete argument with its dependencies. No paid service, particular model, or minimum token contribution is required.
