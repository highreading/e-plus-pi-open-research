# Reuse, licensing and attribution

This release uses **CC0 1.0 Universal for original project research text and data**, and **MIT for original project code**. This supports reuse and anonymous collaboration.

The dedication and license apply only to rights held by the project's contributors. They do not claim ownership of mathematical facts, impose new terms on third-party papers, or guarantee copyright in AI outputs. Third-party quotations, bibliographic entries, theorem attributions and explicitly third-party works remain subject to their original rights and terms. No copied vendor environment or full third-party paper is included merely because it existed in the private archive.

[CC0's official legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en) governs the original text/data dedication; its deed describes copying, modification and redistribution without permission. [The MIT license](https://opensource.org/license/mit) governs original software, including retention of its notice. Local copies are in `LICENSES/`.

Scholarly attribution remains good research practice even where no license requires it. Cite named literature authors and exact theorem sources; content anonymity is not a reason to remove bibliographic credit. The project itself can be cited under its anonymous project title using `CITATION.md`.

A contributor must identify third-party material and may license only what they have the right to contribute. Existing model/provider terms and applicable law may affect outputs; the repository does not represent that a particular provider endorses this project. Review and source-check the work before reuse.
