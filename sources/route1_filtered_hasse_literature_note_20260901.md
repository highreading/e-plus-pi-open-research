# Route 1 filtered-Hasse literature checkpoint

Checked: 2026-09-01 (Beijing time)

## Question screened

Do current crystalline, prismatic, or Dwork frameworks supply a theorem that
turns the actual moving fixed-$j=1$ Hasse--transverse gate into a
prime-independent bounded-complexity compatible family with a weighted
chosen-prime zero-density estimate?

## Primary sources checked

1. Christian Hokaj, *Fontaine--Laffaille Theory over Power Series Rings*,
   arXiv:2407.21327, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21327>.
2. Michel Gros, Bernard Le Stum, and Adolfo Quirós, *Twisted differential
   operators of negative level and prismatic crystals*, arXiv:2010.04433,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.04433>.
3. Alan Adolphson and Steven Sperber, *Dwork cohomology, de Rham cohomology,
   and hypergeometric functions*, arXiv:math/9910009,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9910009>.
4. Mohammadreza Mohajer, *A Tate-Type Theorem for Crystalline Classes in the
   1-Motivic Category*, arXiv:2603.12407,
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12407>.

## What these sources support

- Hokaj supplies a genuine Fontaine--Laffaille framework over Witt power
  series and certain Tate-algebra bases.  This is adjacent to the formal
  filtered module used in Items 374 and 377.
- Gros--Le Stum--Quirós give a Frobenius-descent/Cartier framework for
  topologically quasi-nilpotent twisted connections.  This supports treating
  the choice of Frobenius lift and connection as structural data rather than
  as a scalar determinant identity.
- Adolphson--Sperber provide the classical bridge among Dwork cohomology,
  de Rham cohomology, and hypergeometric functions.  This is relevant to the
  proposed hypergeometric realization of the surviving periods.
- Mohajer's 2026 result gives full faithfulness and algebraicity statements
  for Frobenius-compatible classes in a 1-motivic range.  It is a realization
  theorem in a fixed geometric category, not a distribution theorem for the
  moving selected primes in this archive.

## Negative applicability result

None of the checked sources, as stated, supplies all of the inputs needed
here:

1. one prime-independent bounded-complexity geometric family whose local
   coordinate is the actual tied finite-log moment $Q_0(h,s)$;
2. compatibility across the changing characteristics selected by
   $p=4h+6s+3$; and
3. an estimate of the form
   $\sum_{p\in\mathcal Z_M}\log p=o(M)$ for the simultaneous actual
   Hasse--transverse zero set.

This is a scoped literature conclusion, not a claim that no such theorem can
exist.  It means that Items 374--383 cannot cite any of these papers as the
missing weighted zero-density bridge.  The live fixed-$j=1$ task remains:
construct the actual global family first, then prove chosen-prime
nonconcentration for its codimension-two splitting locus.
