# Item 194 — a coupled-gate obstruction on the PNT-side rank-zero cell

Checked: 2026-08-30 (Beijing time)

## 1. Scope and outcome

Work in the complete $\kappa=2$ rank-zero cell



$$
4m+1=(2j+1)p-2s,
 \qquad 1\le s\le\frac{p-3}{6},
 \qquad 4\mid(2j+1)p-2s-1,
\tag{1.1}
$$



and restrict to its prime-number-theorem side



$$
3j+1<p.                       \tag{1.2}
$$



Thus this item does **not** reuse the $p\le\sqrt{6m}$ exact tail of
Items 168 and 173.  Put



$$
r=\frac{p-6s-3}{2},\qquad
 P_0=u^rQ^{2s},\qquad P_1=u^rQ^{2s-1},                \tag{1.3}
$$



where $u=x(1-x)$, $Q=(1+x)(1+x^2)$, and let $T_\nu$ be the
zero-constant primitive of $P_\nu$ in $\mathbb F_p[x]$.

> **PROVED — fixed rational-map and Hasse representation.**  For fixed
> $p$, the entire moving $s$-family is generated by
> 

$$
> P_\nu(s)=u^{(p-3)/2}
>       \left(\frac{Q^2}{u^3}\right)^sQ^{-\nu}.        \tag{1.4}
>
$$


> This rational expression is a polynomial for every admissible $s$.
> The local factorization (3.2) and exact recurrence (3.3) retain the next
> Hasse digit and the determinant carry; no equidistribution assumption is
> made.

> **PROVED — exact coupled-gate equivalence.**  Let
> $\ell_{\nu,0},\rho_{\nu,0},\varepsilon_{\nu,0}$ be the leading
> endpoint coordinates after the first primitive, as in (3.5).  On every
> row satisfying (1.1)--(1.2),
> 

$$
> \boxed{A_0=B_0=0
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> \ell_{0,0}=\ell_{1,0}=0.}                            \tag{1.5}
>
$$


> The proof does not divide by a logarithmic coordinate.  It treats the
> exceptional zero-log pair separately and rules out the only other linear
> algebra branch by a uniform polynomial elimination.

> **PROVED — exact reduction of the cubic gate.**  The Item 163
> normalization therefore becomes
> 

$$
> p^3\mid c_m
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> \ell_{0,0}=\ell_{1,0}=0\quad\hbox{and}\quad A_1=0.   \tag{1.6}
>
$$


> Here $A_1$ includes the signed determinant carry in (3.7); it is not a
> raw coefficientwise congruence.

> **EXPERIMENTAL EXACT FINITE.**  The deterministic second-Cartier replay
> covers all 6,058 admissible rows through $p\le151$, with every
> admissible $j$.  It finds 56 separate $A_0$-zeros, 55 separate
> $B_0$-zeros, no common logarithmic zero, and no simultaneous
> $A_0=B_0=0$.  A contained full $p$-adic replay through
> $p\le101$, $j\le6$, covers 502 rows: nine have $A_0=0$, eight
> have $A_1=0$, ten have $B_0=0$, and none has
> $A_0=A_1=0$ or passes the cubic gate.  These are finite diagnostics,
> not density claims.

> **OPEN — the exceptional common-root problem.**  No sublinear bound is
> proved for the number or weighted mass of rows satisfying
> $\ell_{0,0}=\ell_{1,0}=0$.  Consequently (1.5) is a sharp algebraic
> obstruction, but it does not by itself prove zero PNT-scale cubic mass.
> No new content exponent and no conclusion about $e+\pi$ is claimed.

## 2. Rank-zero primitive and the moving rational map

Write



$$
a=3j+1,\qquad c=2j+1,
 \qquad F_j=\frac{u^a}{Q^c}.                           \tag{2.1}
$$



The floor identities are



$$
6m=ap+r,qquad
 4m+1+\nu=cp-2s+\nu.                                  \tag{2.2}
$$



Hence the two original differentials factor as



$$
\omega_\nu=F_j^pP_\nu\,dx.                           \tag{2.3}
$$



The degrees



$$
\deg P_0=p-3,\qquad\deg P_1=p-6   \tag{2.4}
$$



show that neither $P_\nu$ contains $x^{p-1}$.  Their first Cartier
images vanish separately, and both zero-constant primitives $T_\nu$
are $p$-integral.  Integration by parts gives



$$
\omega_\nu=d(F_j^pT_\nu)-p\Psi_\nu,
 \qquad \Psi_\nu=F_j^{p-1}F_j'T_\nu\,dx.              \tag{2.5}
$$



The endpoint term vanishes because $F_j(0)=F_j(1)=0$.

Since $r=(p-3)/2-3s$, (1.4) follows immediately.  Thus the moving
parameter is the fixed rational map



$$
K(x)=\frac{Q(x)^2}{u(x)^3}.\tag{2.6}
$$



This is a reformulation, not a bounded-degree collapse: the primitives
$T_\nu=\int_0^xP_\nu(t)\,dt$ still have degrees $p-2$ and $p-5$.

## 3. Local Hasse form and the missing digit

For a pole $\xi\in\{-1,i,-i\}$, put



$$
U_\xi(t)=u(\xi+t),\qquad V_\xi(t)=\frac{Q(\xi+t)}t,
 \qquad G_{j,\xi}(t)=\frac{U_\xi(t)^a}{V_\xi(t)^c}.    \tag{3.1}
$$



If $k_\nu=4m+1+\nu$, the exact moving Hasse factorization is



$$
\frac{U_\xi(t)^{6m}}{V_\xi(t)^{k_\nu}}
 =G_{j,\xi}(t)^pU_\xi(t)^rV_\xi(t)^{2s-\nu}.          \tag{3.2}
$$



It is valid over the integers before reduction modulo $p$.  If the
left side is $C(t)$, logarithmic differentiation gives the exact
coefficient recurrence



$$
(U_\xi V_\xi)C'
 =\{6m\,U_\xi'V_\xi-k_\nu U_\xi V_\xi'\}C.           \tag{3.3}
$$



At the next Hasse level, the integral Frobenius defect



$$
\mathfrak q_{p,j,\xi}(t)
 =\frac{G_{j,\xi}(t)^p-G_{j,\xi}(t^p)}p               \tag{3.4}
$$



isolates the data invisible modulo $p$.  Equations (3.2)--(3.4), with
the exact factorial-valuation reserve at resonant indices, give a
deterministic representation of the missing digit; they do not make it a
bounded-degree function of $s$.

Define integral coordinates after (2.5) by



$$
\ell_\nu=\frac{L_\nu}{p},\qquad
 \rho_\nu=\frac{X_\nu}{p},\qquad
 \varepsilon_\nu=\frac{E_\nu}{p},                    \tag{3.5}
$$



and write, for example,
$\ell_\nu=\ell_{\nu,0}+p\ell_{\nu,1}+O(p^2)$.
Then



$$
\frac{\mathscr A}{p^2}=\ell_1\rho_0-\ell_0\rho_1,
 \qquad
 \frac{\mathscr B}{p^2}=\ell_1\varepsilon_0-
                          \ell_0\varepsilon_1.         \tag{3.6}
$$



Put



$$
\begin{aligned}
 S_0&=\ell_{1,0}\rho_{0,0}-\ell_{0,0}\rho_{1,0},\\
 S_1&=\ell_{1,0}\rho_{0,1}+\ell_{1,1}\rho_{0,0}
      -\ell_{0,0}\rho_{1,1}-\ell_{0,1}\rho_{1,0},\\
 A_0&\equiv S_0\pmod p,\qquad 0\le A_0<p,\\
 q_0&=\frac{S_0-A_0}{p}.
\end{aligned}                                          \tag{3.7}
$$



The exact missing digit is



$$
\boxed{A_1\equiv S_1+q_0\pmod p}.\tag{3.8}
$$



The quotient $q_0$ is an ordinary signed integer carry.  This is why
an argument about only the leading Hasse row cannot determine $A_1$.

## 4. Four sections and the endpoint kernel

Differentiate $F_j$ and put



$$
D_j=a u'Q-cQ'u
 =(3j+1)-(5j+2)(x+x^2+x^3)+x^4.                       \tag{4.1}
$$



For $\nu=0,1$, set $N_\nu=T_\nu D_j$ and



$$
H_\nu(x)=\sum_{n=0}^4h_{\nu,n}x^n,
 \qquad
 h_{\nu,n}=\sum_{z=0}^{p-1}[x^{pn-4z}]N_\nu.          \tag{4.2}
$$



Then



$$
\Phi_\nu:=\mathcal C\Psi_\nu
 =\frac{u^{3j}H_\nu}{Q^{2j+2}}\,dx,
 \qquad H_\nu(0)=0.                                  \tag{4.3}
$$



### 4.1 Exact four-point transform

For every fourth root of unity $\zeta$, the degree bounds and
$N_\nu(0)=0$ give



$$
N_\nu(\zeta)=H_\nu(\zeta^p). \tag{4.4}
$$



Indeed, $h_{\nu,n}$ is exactly the sum of the coefficients whose
indices are congruent to $pn\pmod4$.  Evaluation at the four roots is
an invertible transform on the four-dimensional space



$$
\mathcal V=x\mathbb F_p[x]_{\le4}.\tag{4.5}
$$



The values of (4.1) are



$$
D_j(1)=-4(3j+1),\qquad
 D_j(-1)=D_j(i)=D_j(-i)=4(2j+1).                       \tag{4.6}
$$



They are nonzero under (1.2).

### 4.2 The complete zero-coordinate direction

Let $\mathcal E_j(H)=(R(H),L(H),E(H))$ be the formal endpoint
coordinate map of the differential in (4.3).  Its kernel on $\mathcal V$
is exactly



$$
\ker\mathcal E_j=\mathbb F_pG_j,
 \qquad
 G_j=uQ+xD_j
 =(3j+2)x-(5j+2)(x^2+x^3+x^4).                        \tag{4.7}
$$



First, (4.7) lies in the kernel because



$$
d\!\left(\frac{x u^{3j+1}}{Q^{2j+1}}\right)
 =\frac{u^{3j}G_j}{Q^{2j+2}}\,dx                     \tag{4.8}
$$



and the primitive vanishes at both endpoints.  Conversely, zero
logarithmic and circular coordinates make all finite residues vanish.
Here every finite pole order is at most $2j+2<p$, so in characteristic
$p$ residue zero is the only local obstruction to rational integration;
there is no hidden resonant $p$-th pole.  A rational primitive therefore
exists.  Zero rational endpoint makes its endpoint values equal.  After
subtracting their common value, the primitive has
zeros of order at least $3j+1$ at both 0 and 1, poles of order at most
$2j+1$ at the roots of $Q$, and is finite at infinity.  It therefore
has the form



$$
\frac{u^{3j+1}(\alpha+\beta x)}{Q^{2j+1}}.\tag{4.9}
$$



Since $H(0)=0$ and $3j+1\ne0\pmod p$, differentiation forces
$\alpha=0$, proving (4.7).  In particular, $\mathcal E_j$ has rank
three on $\mathcal V$.

### 4.3 Gate dichotomy without division

Let $c_\nu=\mathcal E_j(H_\nu)=(R_\nu,L_\nu,E_\nu)$.  Up to harmless
common signs and the Frobenius sign on $E$, the two leading gates are



$$
A_0=L_1R_0-L_0R_1,
 \qquad B_0=L_1E_0-L_0E_1.                            \tag{4.10}
$$



If $L_0=L_1=0$, both gates vanish.  If
$(L_0,L_1)\ne(0,0)$, both gates vanish exactly when $c_0,c_1$ are
proportional.  By (4.7), this is equivalent to



$$
\operatorname{rank}(H_0,H_1,G_j)\le2.\tag{4.11}
$$



Thus, with no division by either $L_\nu$,



$$
A_0=B_0=0
 \Longleftrightarrow
 \bigl(L_0=L_1=0\bigr)
 \ \text{or}\ 
 \operatorname{rank}(H_0,H_1,G_j)\le2.               \tag{4.12}
$$



Using (4.4), (4.6), and
$G_j(\zeta^p)=\zeta^pD_j(\zeta)$, condition (4.11) is equivalent to



$$
\operatorname{rank}
 \begin{pmatrix}
 T_0(\zeta)\\ T_1(\zeta)\\ \zeta^p
 \end{pmatrix}_{\zeta^4=1}\le2.                      \tag{4.13}
$$



This is the promised moving-parameter rational-map/Hasse rank
representation.

## 5. Uniform elimination of the regular rank branch

Assume (4.13).  Then some $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$, with
$(\alpha,\beta)\ne(0,0)$, satisfy



$$
\alpha T_0(\zeta)+\beta T_1(\zeta)=\gamma\zeta^p
 \qquad(\zeta^4=1).                                   \tag{5.1}
$$



Define



$$
W(x)=\alpha T_0(x)+\beta T_1(x)-\gamma x^p.          \tag{5.2}
$$



At each root of $Q$, equation (5.1) gives $W=0$, while



$$
W'=\alpha P_0+\beta P_1
   =u^rQ^{2s-1}(\alpha Q+\beta)                        \tag{5.3}
$$



vanishes to order at least $2s-1$.  Since $2s<p$, differentiation has
no characteristic-$p$ resonance in these local orders, and hence
$Q^{2s}\mid W$.  Also $W(0)=W(1)=0$, and (5.3) has a factor $u^r$.
Because $r+1<p$, the same local argument gives
$u^{r+1}\mid W$.  The factors are coprime and



$$
\deg\bigl(u^{r+1}Q^{2s}\bigr)
                  =2(r+1)+6s=p-1.                    \tag{5.4}
$$



Since $\deg W\le p$, there are $\lambda,\mu\in\mathbb F_p$ with



$$
W=u^{r+1}Q^{2s}(\lambda x+\mu).                      \tag{5.5}
$$



Differentiate (5.5); the derivative of $x^p$ is zero.  Dividing by
the nonzero polynomial $u^rQ^{2s-1}$ gives



$$
\alpha Q+\beta
 =Q\{\lambda u+(r+1)u'(\lambda x+\mu)\}
   +2s\,uQ'(\lambda x+\mu).                           \tag{5.6}
$$



Put $R=r+1$.  The coefficients of $x$ and $x^2$ on the left are
equal.  Their difference on the right is



$$
(2R+1-2s)\lambda=(p-8s)\lambda.                     \tag{5.7}
$$



Because $1\le s<p$, equation (5.7) forces $\lambda=0$.  The
coefficient of $x^4$ in (5.6) then gives



$$
-(2R+6s)\mu=-(p-1)\mu=0,                            \tag{5.8}
$$



so $\mu=0$.  Equation (5.3) now forces
$\alpha Q+\beta=0$, hence $\alpha=\beta=0$, contradicting (5.1).
Therefore the rank branch (4.11) is empty for every row (1.1)--(1.2).
Combining this with (4.12) proves (1.5).

## 6. Entry, gate, and capacity ledger

These are three separate questions.

1. **Entry support.**  Every row (1.1) is in the first-Cartier rank-zero
   cell; its already normalized first layer is the starting support.
2. **Lift support.**  The second layer asks for $A_0=0$.  A cubic copy
   asks simultaneously for $A_0=A_1=B_0=0$.  Theorem (1.5) proves that
   the cubic entry pair $A_0=B_0=0$ occurs exactly on the exceptional
   common-log locus.
3. **Capacity.**  The total raw PNT mass of this rank-zero cell is the
   Item 168 constant
   

$$
C_0=-4\log2+\frac{\pi}{\sqrt3}+3\log3-2
      =0.3370475079987658\ldots\quad\text{per }m.       \tag{6.1}
$$


   No positive fraction of (6.1) is proved to lie on the common-log
   locus, and no sublinear bound for that locus is proved either.

Thus Item 194 supplies an exact obstruction and reduces the full cubic
problem to two common logarithmic zeros plus the genuine next digit
$A_1$.  It does not turn finite survival rates into capacity and does
not change the Route-1 constant.

backend.  Canonical and replay runs are required to be byte-identical.
