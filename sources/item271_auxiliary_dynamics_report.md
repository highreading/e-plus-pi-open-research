# Item 271 — auxiliary dynamics for the moving $j=2$ slope

Checked: 2026-08-31 (Beijing time)

## 1. Verdict and strict admission split

Item 269 proved that the actual slopes $D_\delta$ are not the graph of
a fixed algebraic correspondence over the naive cutoff line.  That does
not prevent a sequence from being generated by finite-dimensional
dynamics.  This item separates three logically different statements:

1. a nonautonomous recurrence in $\delta$;
2. an autonomous rational skew product obtained by adjoining the counter
   $\delta$; and
3. a genuine lisse/crystalline Frobenius family with uniform conductor,
   nontrivial geometric monodromy, and a fixed collision divisor.

The first two exist exactly.  The third is not constructed.

Let



$$
\Delta_\delta=D_{\delta+2}-D_\delta.                          \tag{1.1}
$$



> **PROVED — exact order-two recurrence.**  In each phase there are
> explicit degree-seven polynomials
> $P_0^{(e)},P_1^{(e)},P_2^{(e)}$, with
> 

$$
> P_1^{(e)}=-P_0^{(e)}-P_2^{(e)},
>
$$


> such that every actual odd $\delta$ satisfies
> 

$$
> \boxed{
> P_0^{(e)}(\delta)D_\delta+
> P_1^{(e)}(\delta)D_{\delta+2}+
> P_2^{(e)}(\delta)D_{\delta+4}=0.}                           \tag{1.2}
>
$$


> This is proved by the exact telescoping certificate in Section 4,
> including both moving-upper-bound defects.  It is not inferred from a
> fitted finite recurrence.

> **PROVED — rank-three autonomous rational dynamics.**  Put
> 

$$
> \rho_e(\delta)=\frac{P_0^{(e)}(\delta)}
> {P_2^{(e)}(\delta)}.
>
$$


> Then
> 

$$
> \boxed{
> \Delta_{\delta+2}=\rho_e(\delta)\Delta_\delta,}              \tag{1.3}
>
$$


> and the actual slope orbit is generated by
> 

$$
> \boxed{
> T_e(\delta,D,\Delta)
> =(\delta+2,D+\Delta,\rho_e(\delta)\Delta).}                  \tag{1.4}
>
$$


> This is an autonomous rational skew product of dimension three and
> projective degree at most eight.

> **PROVED — bounded-rank translation-difference module.**  Over
> $(\mathbb Q(\delta),\sigma)$, $\sigma(\delta)=\delta+2$, the vector
> 

$$
> y_\delta=(1,D_\delta,\Delta_\delta)^{\mathsf T}
>
$$


> satisfies
> 

$$
> \sigma(y)=
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 1&0&0\\
> 0&1&1\\
> 0&0&\rho_e(\delta)
> \end{pmatrix}y.                                             \tag{1.5}
>
$$


> Thus the moving slope has a genuine bounded-rank difference-module
> realization.

These positive statements do not meet the Frobenius-divisor admission
test.

* Translation $\delta\mapsto\delta+2$ is not arithmetic or geometric
  Frobenius.
* The forward map has pole divisor $P_2^{(e)}(\delta)=0$, and exact
  actual mod-$p$ rows can meet it even though the original
  $D_\delta$ is defined there.
* The dynamics generates only $D_\delta$.  The full collision remains
  

$$
R_{r,s}(1,H_q,h_q)^{\mathsf T}=0                            \tag{1.6}
$$


  with both rows of the external matrix $R_{r,s}$ from Item 269.
* No compatible lisse or crystalline family, uniform conductor,
  nontrivial geometric monodromy, or local-density theorem is proved.

Consequently



$$
\boxed{
\text{new unconditional Route-1 rate}=0,\qquad
\text{new ordinary-\(j=2\) capacity reduction}=0.}            \tag{1.7}
$$



The raw $2/35$ ordinary-$j=2$ mass is unchanged.  Every bounded
sequence replay is **EXACT FINITE ONLY**.  The telescoping grid is a
degree-bounded symbolic certificate, not a finite-pattern inference.

## 2. The two slope phases

For $e=5$, actual rows have odd $\delta\ge1$.  Define



$$
\begin{aligned}
c_0&=1,&
\frac{c_{j+1}}{c_j}&=\frac{6j+5}{3j+4},&
K_\delta&=\sum_{j=0}^{\delta-1}c_j,\\
a_\delta&=-\delta-\frac13,&
L_\delta&=3\delta,&
\Pi_\delta&=\sum_{k=1}^{L_\delta}
2^{-k}\frac{(a_\delta)_k}{(1/2)_k},\\
D_\delta&=K_\delta+c_\delta\Pi_\delta.
\end{aligned}                                                \tag{2.1}
$$



For $e=1$, actual rows have odd $\delta\ge3$, and



$$
\begin{aligned}
c_0&=1,&
\frac{c_{j+1}}{c_j}&=\frac{6j+1}{3j+2},&
K_\delta&=\sum_{j=0}^{\delta-1}c_j,\\
a_\delta&=\frac13-\delta,&
L_\delta&=3\delta-2,&
\Pi_\delta&=\sum_{k=1}^{L_\delta}
2^{-k}\frac{(a_\delta)_k}{(1/2)_k},\\
D_\delta&=K_\delta+c_\delta\Pi_\delta.
\end{aligned}                                                \tag{2.2}
$$



These are exactly the slopes in



$$
A_s=(-1)^m\{\epsilon(H_q-D_\delta h_q)-1\}.                   \tag{2.3}
$$

## 4. Exact telescoping proof

Define the hypergeometric summand



$$
F_\delta(k)=c_\delta
2^{-k}\frac{(a_\delta)_k}{(1/2)_k}.                           \tag{4.1}
$$



Then



$$
D_\delta=K_\delta+\sum_{k=1}^{L_\delta}F_\delta(k).            \tag{4.2}
$$



For $s=2,4$, put



$$
A_s(\delta,k)=
\frac{(a_\delta-s)_k}{(a_\delta)_k}.                          \tag{4.3}
$$



For $s=1,2,3,4$, put



$$
\gamma_s(\delta)=\frac{c_{\delta+s}}{c_\delta},
$$



then



$$
\frac{F_{\delta+s}(k)}{F_\delta(k)}
=\gamma_s(\delta)A_s(\delta,k).                              \tag{4.4}
$$



Write



$$
\mathcal Q_e(\delta,k)=
P_0^{(e)}+
P_1^{(e)}\gamma_2A_2+
P_2^{(e)}\gamma_4A_4.                                       \tag{4.5}
$$



The portable certificate gives a rational function



$$
\mathcal R_e(\delta,k)=
\frac{\mathcal S_e(\delta,k)}
{\prod_{u\in U_e}(3\delta-3k+u)},                            \tag{4.6}
$$



where



$$
U_5=\{4,7,10,13\},\qquad U_1=\{2,5,8,11\}.                   \tag{4.7}
$$



Its numerator has the factored form



$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal S_5
&=-9(6\delta+5)(6\delta+11)(2k-1)\,\mathcal T_5(\delta,k),\\
\mathcal S_1
&=-9(6\delta+1)(6\delta+7)(2k-1)\,\mathcal T_1(\delta,k),
\end{aligned}                                                \tag{4.8}
$$



where the thirty exact coefficients of each $\mathcal T_e$ are frozen
in the standard-library checker.  Direct clearing of denominators gives



$$
\boxed{
\frac{a_\delta+k}{2k+1}\mathcal R_e(\delta,k+1)
-\mathcal R_e(\delta,k)
=\mathcal Q_e(\delta,k).}                                   \tag{4.9}
$$



Since



$$
\frac{F_\delta(k+1)}{F_\delta(k)}
=\frac{a_\delta+k}{2k+1},
$$



equation (4.9) is the WZ identity



$$
\begin{aligned}
&P_0^{(e)}F_\delta(k)
+P_1^{(e)}F_{\delta+2}(k)
+P_2^{(e)}F_{\delta+4}(k)\\
&\qquad =
G_\delta(k+1)-G_\delta(k),
\qquad
G_\delta(k)=F_\delta(k)\mathcal R_e(\delta,k).                \tag{4.10}
\end{aligned}
$$



The summation limits move:



$$
L_{\delta+2}=L_\delta+6,\qquad
L_{\delta+4}=L_\delta+12.                                   \tag{4.11}
$$



The certificate therefore checks, in addition to (4.9), the exact
terminal identity



$$
\begin{aligned}
0={}&\mathcal R_e(\delta,L_\delta+1)\\
&+P_1^{(e)}\gamma_2
\sum_{t=0}^{5}
\frac{2^{-(L_\delta+1+t)}
(a_\delta-2)_{L_\delta+1+t}/(1/2)_{L_\delta+1+t}}
{2^{-(L_\delta+1)}
(a_\delta)_{L_\delta+1}/(1/2)_{L_\delta+1}}\\
&+P_2^{(e)}\gamma_4
\sum_{t=0}^{11}
\frac{2^{-(L_\delta+1+t)}
(a_\delta-4)_{L_\delta+1+t}/(1/2)_{L_\delta+1+t}}
{2^{-(L_\delta+1)}
(a_\delta)_{L_\delta+1}/(1/2)_{L_\delta+1}},
\end{aligned}                                                \tag{4.12}
$$



and the lower-boundary/prefix identity



$$
-a_\delta\mathcal R_e(\delta,1)
-P_0^{(e)}(1+\gamma_1)
+P_2^{(e)}(\gamma_2+\gamma_3)=0.                             \tag{4.13}
$$



Summing (4.10), then using (4.11)–(4.13), proves (1.2).

### Why the portable grid is a proof

After the displayed denominators in (4.9) are cleared, the numerator has
degree at most $32$ in $\delta$ and at most $16$ in $k$.
The checker evaluates it exactly on a $33\times17$ rational grid.
A bivariate polynomial with those degree bounds and that many roots in
each variable is identically zero.

After clearing denominators in (4.12)–(4.13), each numerator has degree
at most $64$ in $\delta$.  The checker evaluates each on 65 exact
values.  This is polynomial identity certification by the root bound,
not a conjectural finite scan.

The separate replay of slope values through $\delta\le31$ is only
**EXACT FINITE ONLY** corroboration.

## 5. Singular divisor and actual mod-$p$ rows

Over $\mathbb Q$, every denominator factor of $\rho_e(\delta)$ is
positive on the actual positive $\delta$-orbit; $Q_5(\delta)>0$ for
$\delta\ge1$ and $Q_1(\delta)>0$ for $\delta\ge3$.  Thus (1.4)
generates the rational sequence without a characteristic-zero pole.

The forward rational map has pole divisor



$$
P_2^{(e)}(\delta)=0.                                        \tag{5.1}
$$



The invertible difference module has singular support



$$
P_0^{(e)}(\delta)P_2^{(e)}(\delta)=0
\quad\text{together with infinity}.                         \tag{5.2}
$$



Reduction modulo the row prime is different.  Exact actual witnesses
are



$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c|c}
e&p&q&\delta&P_0^{(e)}(\delta)\bmod p&
P_2^{(e)}(\delta)\bmod p\\ \hline
5&167&27&7&157&0\\
1&241&40&11&222&0.
\end{array}                                                  \tag{5.3}
$$



In both rows, the reduced denominator of $D_\delta$ and that of
$D_{\delta+2}$ are $p$-units, while



$$
\Delta_\delta=0\pmod p.                                     \tag{5.4}
$$



Thus the cleared recurrence remains true, but the quotient
$\rho_e(\delta)\Delta_\delta$ is not a defined forward update.  The
original slope family is regular at a row where the chosen rational
dynamical chart has a pole.

The table is **EXACT FINITE ONLY**.  It proves that singular actual rows
exist; it does not estimate their density.  A lisse/crystalline
realization would have to resolve these rows or prove that the omitted
part has negligible weighted capacity.  Neither theorem is available.

## 6. Direct iterates have growing coordinate complexity

The third coordinate of the $n$-th iterate contains



$$
\rho_{e,n}(\delta)=
\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}\rho_e(\delta+2j).                           \tag{6.1}
$$



The cubic factor telescopes:



$$
\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}
\frac{Q_e(\delta+2j+2)}{Q_e(\delta+2j)}
=\frac{Q_e(\delta+2n)}{Q_e(\delta)}.                         \tag{6.2}
$$



The four numerator and four denominator linear root classes are
disjoint modulo translation by $2$.  Also $Q_5$ and $Q_1$ are
irreducible over $\mathbb Q$: modulo $31$ each remains cubic and has
no root.  Hence no further rational cancellation occurs, and



$$
\deg\operatorname{num}\rho_{e,n}
=\deg\operatorname{den}\rho_{e,n}
=4n+3.                                                       \tag{6.3}
$$



This proves only that direct unrolling in the same rational coordinates
has growing degree.  It does not contradict the existence of the fixed
one-step map (1.4), and it does not rule out a different bounded-rank
lisse realization.  Degree growth is not used as an impossibility claim.

## 7. Difference dynamics is not yet Frobenius dynamics

Equation (1.5) is a rank-three module for the translation automorphism
$\sigma(\delta)=\delta+2$.  This is a legitimate algebraic-difference
object with fixed singular divisor and a generally nonconstant
multiplicative coordinate.

A lisse $\ell$-adic sheaf or overconvergent $F$-isocrystal requires
arithmetic/geometric Frobenius, a compatible integral structure, and a
conductor/monodromy theorem.  None follows formally from a translation
difference equation.  In particular, this item does not prove:

* that $\Delta_\delta\bmod p$ is a trace or matrix coefficient of a
  fixed compatible system;
* that the pole rows (5.3) admit a uniform regular extension;
* that the resulting geometric monodromy is nontrivial or large; or
* that Frobenius hits a proper collision divisor with density below the
  raw capacity.

These are open realization problems, not proved obstructions.

## 8. Why the full two-gate collision still moves

If the state is augmented by the fixed-motive coordinates
$(H_q,h_q)$, the period equation becomes the single fixed divisor



$$
\epsilon(H-Dh)-1=0.                                        \tag{8.1}
$$



This is useful for the Item-251 period $A_s$, but it is not the full
collision.  Item 269 proved the exact complete condition



$$
R_{r,s}(1,H_q,h_q)^{\mathsf T}=0,                            \tag{8.2}
$$



where $R_{r,s}$ has two rows and includes



$$
f_0,f_1,\ U_0,U_1,\ B_s,\ \kappa_r,\ \tau_{r,s},
\quad\text{as well as }D_\delta.                             \tag{8.3}
$$



The new skew product updates only $D_\delta$ and
$\Delta_\delta$.  No bounded-state update for all entries in (8.3) is
constructed.  Adjoining $R_{r,s}$ as an unconstrained coordinate gives
the tautological universal incidence from Item 269, not a Frobenius
family.

The sealed Item-268 adjacent-fiber result reinforces this distinction:
one transported line does not propagate the common two-coordinate zero.
Therefore (8.1) cannot replace (8.2).

The smallest exact open lemma is now:

> Construct a bounded-rank, uniform-conductor lisse or crystalline
> realization of the complete moving row matrix $R_{r,s}$, compatible
> with the fixed motive coordinates $(H_q,h_q)$, regular on the actual
> singular rows, and having nontrivial geometric monodromy for which
> (8.2) is one fixed proper conjugacy-stable collision condition.

Without that lemma and a uniform local-density estimate, no large-sieve
capacity theorem follows.
