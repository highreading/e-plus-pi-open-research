# Item 163 literature-status recheck

Checked: 2026-08-29 00:25 Beijing time.

## VERIFIED SOURCE STATEMENTS

- Runlong Yu, *Tail Criteria, No-Go Audits, and Apéry-Type Certificate
  Obstructions for the Irrationality of e+pi*, arXiv:2606.17303v1, explicitly
  states that the irrationality of $e+\pi$ remains open and that the paper
  supplies bounded no-go audits rather than a proof:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17303
- L. Lerner, *The Irrationality of e and pi*, arXiv:2607.19418, concerns
  separate proofs for $e$ and $\pi$; it does not claim a result about
  their sum:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19418
- Ben Snodgrass, *Periods of E-operators*, arXiv:2608.06005, supplies a
  common period framework but no injectivity or arithmetic-independence
  theorem separating $e$ from $\pi$:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06005
