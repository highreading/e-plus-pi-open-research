## Independent derivation

With



$$
\mathscr M_q(C;z)=
 \sum_{k=0}^m\binom mk {C^k\over a+q+k-z},
$$



termwise cancellation after reindexing the $k$-weighted part gives



$$
(a+q-z)\mathscr M_q+
 C(b+q-z)\mathscr M_{q+1}=(1+C)^{m+1}.
$$



Iteration gives



$$
\mathscr M_L(C;z)=A_C(z)\mathscr M_0(C;z)+E_C(z),\qquad
 A_C(z)=(-1/C)^L{(a-z)_L\over(b-z)_L}.
$$



Reversing $k$ and using $b+m-k=p-(a+k)$ gives exactly



$$
\mathscr M_L(C;z)=-C^m\mathscr M_0(C^{-1};p-z).
$$



The two complementary factorial identities give



$$
A_C(z)A_{C^{-1}}(p-z)=1.
$$



Thus the second reciprocal coefficient row is the first multiplied by
the unit $C^{-m}A_C(z)^{-1}$.  Substitution of the explicit resolvents
then gives the affine identity



$$
E_{C^{-1}}(p-z)=C^{-m}A_C(z)^{-1}E_C(z).
$$



## Topology and truncation

Let $S\subset\mathbf Z_{(p)}[z]$ consist of polynomials with $p$-unit
constant term, and set



$$
\mathcal R=\mathbf Z_{(p)}[C,C^{-1},z,S^{-1}],\qquad
 \mathfrak m=(p,z).
$$



The map $(C,z)\mapsto(C^{-1},p-z)$ preserves $S$ and
$\mathfrak m$, hence every $\mathfrak m^N$.  For a truncated series
$\sum_{j<N}f_jz^j$, zero in $\mathcal R/\mathfrak m^N$ means
$f_j=0\pmod {p^{N-j}}$.  Expanding the exact reflection therefore gives



$$
J_{L,j}(C)\equiv(-1)^{j+1}C^m
 \sum_{h=0}^{N-j-1}\binom{j+h}{h}p^hJ_{0,j+h}(C^{-1})
 \pmod {p^{N-j}}.
$$



There is no missing tail: every omitted term has total
$(p,z)$-degree at least $N$.  The coordinate transform
$F(z)\mapsto F(p-z)$ is triangular on jets with diagonal
$(-1)^j$, so it is invertible at every finite level and loses no digit.

Because $C^m$ is a unit, the first functional row uniquely solves
$Y=-C^{-m}(A_CX+E_C)$ for every $X$-class.  The second row is its
unit multiple, including the affine term.  Hence the elimination ideal
in the $X$-coordinate is zero for this relation system.
