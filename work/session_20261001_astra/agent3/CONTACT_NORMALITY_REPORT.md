Agent 3: contact-normality research report

A new uniform nonvanishing theorem is proved in CONTACT_NORMALITY_RESEARCH.md:

    n>=16, 2<=b<=n, n>=512 b^4 log n
      implies det J_(n,b)>0.

In particular, for every n>=2^18 this covers

    2<=b<=floor((n/(512 log n))^(1/4)),

including the explicit unbounded allocation given by the upper endpoint. There is no parity restriction. The initial proportional allocation b=floor(n/2) remains open in this work.

The exact new reduction holds throughout 2<=b<=n. Put Q=1-z+z^2/2 and g_m=[z^m]exp(z)Q(z)^n. Then

    Delta_(n,b)=det[g_(n+i-j)]_(i,j=0,...,b-1),

    det J_(n,b)
      =(-1)^(nb) [2^n (product_(j=0)^(n-1)j!)^3
                    /product_(m=n+b)^(2n+b-1)m!]
                  Delta_(n,b).

The logarithmic-block transformation underlying this formula is an isomorphism:

    C -> Q^n D^n(CF), degree C<n,

with determinant 2^n(product_(j=0)^(n-1)j!)^2. Its proof retains both poles 1+i and 1-i, diagonalizes in a conjugate Möbius basis, and accounts for every sign. The exponential block is transformed by the unit-triangular operator (D+1)^n.

An alternative exact rational-function determinant is generated by

    P_(r+1)=Q P_r'-(n+r)Q'P_r-QP_r,

starting with the monomials of degree less than n. This realizes the proposed annihilator (D-1)^b D^n. The smaller Toeplitz determinant also has an explicit scalar coefficient recurrence, recorded in the research note.

For nonvanishing, the circle |z|=sqrt(2) makes Q(z)/z real. A determinant integration identity expresses Delta as a complete complex integral. After centering angles at the negative real axis, the main region |u_j|<=1/(2b) has positive real phase at least 3/4. An explicit separated-box lower bound and a bound for the entire complement prove

    (-1)^(nb) Delta_(n,b)>=L_(n,b)/(2b!)>0

under the stated range, with L_(n,b) given explicitly in the research note. Odd-n signs outside the main region are included in the tail estimate. No sign is inferred from entries or transformed minors.

Through the supplied endpoint bridge, this proves that the relaxed matched space with caps (n,b,n) and contact O(z^(2n+b)) has dimension two, and its endpoint map (A(1),B(1)) is an isomorphism onto Q^2, on the same explicit growing-b set.

The limitation is quantitative and explicit: this concentration estimate does not cover b proportional to n. It makes no claim that the proportional determinant vanishes. Stronger-contact uniqueness, full-remainder nonvanishing, and actual reduced-denominator control remain separate questions.
