# Short-stack simultaneous dyadic polynomial basis: fresh gate

2026-10-02, original lower-block-dependent M24 Smith component. The precise target is a monic INTEGER basis which prices the shifted Gamma, ordinary factorial Gamma, and complete compact reciprocal term simultaneously, then gives a divisor of the ACTUAL two rectangular contents. It is not the weighted q_n Boolean/Frobenius endpoint problem assigned to Agent 1 and does not construct Root's reserved higher-pole families.

Fresh archive query covered `y(y-1)`, `two.center`, `Boolean.*Newton`, `Newton.*lattice`, and `3k^2.*content`. Read the old `sources/bessel_two_center_evaluation_lattice_cross_determinant.md`, Sections1--2: its two parameter-center evaluation index and Bessel cross determinant are prior work, but neither is a moment-lattice divisor for this fixed short stack. Our earlier one-center Gamma basis and two-rectangle full-gcd interface are direct internal overlap. Agent 1's current weighted family uses a finite-field Boolean monomial pairing; we retain that separate scope and claim no general p-ordering basis as new.

Fresh primary queries were `integer valued polynomials p ordering Bhargava generalized factorial bases determinant divisibility primary pdf` and `Hankel determinants binomial polynomial basis factorial divisibility Smith normal form`. Opened Miller--Stanton, *Orthogonal polynomials and Smith normal form*, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.03539, for the classical unimodular Gram/Smith basis principle (the older author PDF returned an error on this fresh access, so the arXiv full text was used). Also opened Brian Diaz, *Bhargava Gamma functions for determinant-admissible sets*, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.26321, working draft v0.5 July2026, especially Section6.2's quadratic-image factorial fibers and its explicit distinction between known factorial formulas and proposed determinant completions. The latter framework is background only: no proposed completion, unproved uniqueness, or quadratic-image factorial theorem is imported into our moment measures.

Classical ingredients credited: monic triangular integer basis changes, factorial valuations, beta integrals, and rank-one determinant expansion. The new specific divisor uses Q_j(y)=y^epsilon[y(y-1)]^m for j=2m+epsilon, retains the odd least common multiple and BOTH full coefficient entries, and predicts 3k^2+O(k log k) dyadic content for the actual rectangular matrices. It supplies a guaranteed factor rather than an equality for their Smith contents or a denominator lower bound.
