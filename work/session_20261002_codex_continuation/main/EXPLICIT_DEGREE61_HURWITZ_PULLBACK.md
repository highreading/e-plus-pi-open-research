# A new explicit polynomial composition with Taylor radius greater than 1.98

Root original construction and exact certificate,2026-10-02. Not independently reviewed; main research active.

## Result

Let K1,...,K60 be the frozen integer vector in EXPLICIT_DEGREE61_RADIUS198_CERTIFICATE.json, and set

    P(z)=z+sum_(k=1)^60 K_k z^k(1-z)/k!,
    G(z)=4 arctan(P(z)/(2-P(z))),

with the analytic branch at0. Then

    P(0)=0, P(1)=1, P^(j)(0) in Z for all j,
    G(1)=pi, G^(j)(0) in Z for all j,
    TaylorRadius(G)>99/50=1.98.

This is an explicit finite polynomial construction, improving the archive's strongest located explicit certified1.7679119 example. The separate infinite-Schur theorem establishes polynomial existence at every radius below2.65 but does not specify one finite degree reaching2.65.

## Exact proof

The endpoint basis makes the endpoints exact. Each term contributes K_k and-(k+1)K_k at derivative orders k and k+1, so all polynomial derivative jets are integers and vanish beyond degree61.

For q(z)=P(z)-(1+i), form the reversed degree61 polynomial w^61 q((99/50)/w), clear denominators, and apply the exact Gaussian-integer Schur-Cohn recursion. All61 Schur gaps are strictly positive; the final constant is nonzero. Therefore every zero of q is strictly outside the closed disk radius99/50. Real coefficients imply the same assertion for P-(1-i). This proves analytic continuation of the composition over a neighborhood of the closed disk.

The certificate retains the complete integer vector, polynomial jets, every state hash, positive gap hash, removed content and final constant. The new driver uses only definition-level Gaussian integer primitives from the archive's nonpolynomial certificate; it never evaluates that older candidate. The largest integer norm encountered has132599 bits, and no floating-point root is used in a proof decision.

All-order G jet integrality follows from the integer recurrence for F^(j)(0) and the Bell-polynomial composition formula, as proved in INFINITE_INTEGRAL_JET_PULLBACK_EXISTENCE.md. The real endpoint path fixes G(1)=pi.

## Generation and limits

The integer vector was generated by a new finite Schur interpolant with initial jets(1,0,0,-1,6,0,-51,29), numerical series inversion, and rounding in the endpoint basis. That numerical procedure only proposes the frozen integers. The exact certificate establishes their properties independently. The diagnostic nearest root modulus1.989128... is not the certified bound.

Adding exp(z) gives integral jets and endpoint e+pi, but Taylor truncations still have a factorial arithmetic ledger. A larger fixed analytic radius alone does not prove irrationality; the primitive-denominator question is being investigated separately.
