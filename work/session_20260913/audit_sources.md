## Existing coverage and research state

The archive has already checked Lindemann–Weierstrass, Baker, Gelfond–Schneider, Nesterenko, six-exponential arguments, Schanuel, algebraic-translation irrationality measures, mixed Hermite–Padé constructions, and a very large collection of finite-field/gcd/endpoint obstructions. The principal mixed E/G and period notes go beyond generic warnings: they calculate forced logarithm exceptional points, division failure for the E+G function class, the split rank-three exponential/arctangent connection, rational-gauge and tensor obstructions, and an explicit nonsplit extension whose new period does not cancel.

Thus repeating the elementary claim “functional independence does not imply independence of values” would add little. The useful continuation is to test genuinely new hypotheses in the recent arithmetic-holonomy and geometric-period literature, and to distinguish an accessible coefficient problem from an unresolved numerical period-map theorem.

Existing source notes agree that the low-order mixed module does not depend on the putative algebraic value of e+pi. The numerical specialization relation lies in a connection constant, not in the differential module alone. Their exact E+G division obstruction is sound in its stated scope: if H_a=a-exp(z)-4 arctan(z) and Q_a=H_a/(z-1), a decomposition Q_a=E+G would force the difference of its two decompositions into the polynomial intersection of E- and G-function classes; evaluation at 1 would then make a-e algebraic. This does not prove the still-open intersection conjecture for rings of **values**.

## Independent denominator obstruction for the direct holonomy attempt

Let a be rational and put H_a(z)=a-exp(z)-4 arctan(z). Its coefficient of z^(2m), m>=1, is exactly -1/(2m)!. Consequently any positive integer clearing the first N coefficients must be divisible by (2 floor(N/2))!, so its logarithm is N log N+O(N).

By contrast a denominator type of the form n^d product_j lcm(1,...,floor(b_j n)+c_j), with fixed finite d,b_j,c_j, has logarithm O(n). One elementary bound is log lcm(1,...,N)=O(N), so neither such types nor a fixed rational rescaling can clear the factorial denominators of H_a. The direct Theorem-2.1 germ is therefore inadmissible before the analytic inequality is considered.

There is also a coordinate-free class argument for the quotient Q_a=H_a/(z-1). If Q_a were a G-function, polynomial multiplication and addition would make exp(z)=a-4 arctan(z)-(z-1)Q_a a G-function. An entire G-function is a polynomial, which exp(z) is not. Thus Q_a is never a G-function, regardless of whether its apparent pole at 1 happens to cancel.

These conclusions concern these exact germs, not all possible recurrence or Apéry generating functions for e+pi. The most useful next step is to construct such a different pair A(z),B(z), with controlled exponential denominators and rationality-forced meromorphic continuation of B-(e+pi)A. Merely forming the already-audited quotient does not meet the arithmetic hypotheses.

## Yu June-2026 source authenticity and theorem audit

Runlong Yu, *Tail Criteria, No-Go Audits, and Apéry-Type Certificate Obstructions for the Irrationality of e+pi*, [arXiv:2606.17303v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17303), submitted 15 June 2026, category math.GM. The arXiv record and [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17303) are real and consistent with the archive; no journal reference appears in the opened record. Theorem 3.1 gives an eventual ceiling recurrence, Theorem 3.3 an eventual factorial-digit condition, Theorem 3.4 an eventual divisibility condition, and Proposition 4.2 a mixed integral identity. Their algebra is elementary and checks independently. The paper expressly leaves irrationality open and restricts computational findings to its searched families. The 145-to-133 finite dataset was not rerun here.

The following verification is independent. For an integer polynomial P, S=sum_j(-1)^j P^(j) is an integer polynomial with S+S'=P. Therefore (e^x S)'=e^x P, so integration gives S(1)e-S(0). Adding 4S(1) integral_0^1 dx/(1+x^2) yields S(1)(e+pi)-S(0). This identity supplies integer forms but does not force them to be small or nonzero.

For the tail criterion, write B_N=sum_(k=0)^N N!/k! and r_N=N!e-B_N, where 0<r_N<1 for N>=1 and r_(N+1)=(N+1)r_N-1. If e+pi=r is rational, N!r is eventually integral, hence A_N=ceil(N!pi)=N!r-B_N and A_(N+1)=(N+1)A_N-1. Conversely that eventual recurrence makes (A_N+B_N)/N! eventually constant, whose limit is e+pi. The digit and divisibility formulations follow by floors and the range 0<=d_N<=N-1. Thus the missing lemma remains an infinitely-often violation, not an additional finite computation.

**Qualification to the paper’s methodology:** its “continued-fraction shadow” exclusion is not a necessary condition for a rigorous irrationality proof. If an independent argument proves integer A_n,B_n with 0<|A_n(e+pi)-B_n| tending to zero, the contradiction to rationality is valid even when B_n/A_n are convergents or intermediate convergents. Requiring a “non-CF” family is a search preference, not a theorem. Dividing both coefficients by their gcd also decreases the absolute error; it cannot destroy established absolute smallness. Finite approximations are inadequate because they do not prove an unbounded nonzero sequence, not because they resemble continued fractions. This distinction should be retained in future route ranking.

## Ranked continuation within the delegated literature domain

1. Arithmetic holonomy applied to a genuinely new Apéry generating pair: concrete coefficient/continuation inequalities, but the required pair remains missing. The raw mixed germ is excluded by the factorial obstruction above.
2. Nonhypergeometric G/period representations: potentially useful for generating new pi approximants beyond previous hypergeometric families; demand explicit endpoint identities and normalized coefficient bounds before doing large searches.
3. Mixed E/G special-value separation: mathematically exact target, but current theorems stop at conjectural intersection or numerical period injectivity. Pursue only a sharply delimited new lemma.
4. Functional Ax–Schanuel, geometric period conjectures, abstract nonsplit extensions and period-ring membership: useful structural context, low immediate priority because they do not bridge numerical specialization.

No proof of rationality or irrationality was obtained in this audit. This is a research checkpoint; the parent session's termination conditions remain separate.
