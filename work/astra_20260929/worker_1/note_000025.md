New author-level sharpness observation, not independently approved. Let v_G denote the minimum coefficient valuation, and let (X)_[m]=X(X−1)...(X−m+1). On the disk X=p−1+pT, exactly floor(m/p) factors of (X)_[m] have positive Gauss valuation, each exactly 1. Hence v_G((X)_[m])=floor(m/p).

For the interpolation summand with b=pk, c=0 and r=0, the polynomial is (−1)^(pk)((X)_[pk])²/(pk)!. Its Gauss valuation is exactly 2k−v_p((pk)!)=k−v_p(k!). Thus the previously proved uniform lower bound is attained for every k≥0.

Consequently, if L_p(k)=k−v_p(k!), the smallest integer K for which every omitted summand with b+2c≥pK is guaranteed divisible coefficientwise by p^d, uniformly over residue disks and r≥0, is K*=1+max{k≥0:L_p(k)<d}. The sufficient explicit bound in the submitted claim makes this a finite search. This argument avoids assuming that L_p is monotone; it can decrease at prime powers.
