Unreviewed supplementary consequence. Let s_3(n) denote the sum of the base-3 digits of n. Legendre’s formula gives 2v_3(n!)=n−s_3(n). Therefore the approved ternary theorem yields the exact decomposition q_n=3^{n−s_3(n)}r_n, where r_n is a positive integer coprime to 3, for every n≥3 divisible by 3. Since s_3(n)=O(log n), this implies liminf (log q_n)/n≥log 3 along that progression. In particular, at n=3^k the exact ternary factor is 3^{n−1}.

Conditionally on the fixed-b error law log|E_n|=−2n log(1+sqrt(2))+o(n) for the same approximant, substitution gives log|q_n E_n|=log r_n−δn−s_3(n)log 3+o(n), where δ=2log(1+sqrt(2))−log 3=log((3+2sqrt(2))/3)>0. Thus, along any chosen subsequence of these indices, limsup (log r_n)/n<δ suffices for q_n E_n→0, while liminf (log r_n)/n>δ forces |q_n E_n|→∞. Equality at the exponential threshold is unresolved by this estimate. The ternary contribution alone therefore does not rule out shrinking; the remaining arithmetic question concerns the factor r_n.
