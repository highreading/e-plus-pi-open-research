UNVERIFIED CONDITIONAL SYNTHESIS: obstruction on two of the three progressions divisible by five.

Let lambda=1+sqrt(2), and let p_n/q_n be the actual reduced approximant in the fixed b=2 family, with q_n>0. Assume that it is defined for all sufficiently large indices considered and that its complete error satisfies
|e+pi-p_n/q_n| >= c lambda^(-2n)
for some c>0. The established fixed-degree asymptotic is the intended analytic input, with its exact endpoint identification retained as a dependency.

Assume additionally the following arithmetic bounds for the SAME reduced denominator:
(A) v_3(q_n)>=2v_3(n!) when n is congruent to 0 or 1 modulo 3;
(B) v_5(q_n)>=2v_5(n!) when 5 divides n.
The divisible-by-three part of (A) is recorded as published. Its residue-one part is presently worker_2's unreviewed derivation, conditional on D!=0. Bound (B) follows from the five-adic candidate if independently approved. These statuses are not upgraded here.

For n congruent to 0 or 10 modulo 15, both hypotheses apply. Writing s_p(n) for the sum of the base-p digits of n, Legendre's formula gives
q_n >= 3^(n-s_3(n)) 5^((n-s_5(n))/2).
Consequently,
q_n |e+pi-p_n/q_n| >= c rho^n / (3^s_3(n) 5^(s_5(n)/2)),
where rho=3sqrt(5)/(3+2sqrt(2)).

This base exceeds one exactly: both quantities are positive, and
(3sqrt(5))^2-(3+2sqrt(2))^2=28-12sqrt(2)>0.
For n>=1, the elementary digit bounds give
3^s_3(n)<=9n^2,
5^(s_5(n)/2)<=25n^2.
Thus the complete primitive form has magnitude at least
(c/225) rho^n/n^4,
which tends to infinity along either progression.

Therefore, under these hypotheses, any unbounded subsequence of indices divisible by five on which the primitive forms tend to zero must eventually lie in n congruent to 5 modulo 15. This identifies the unresolved n congruent to 2 modulo 3 class as necessary for that restricted search. It does not establish that this remaining class succeeds, exclude other indices, or prove anything about rationality. In particular, the vanishing of the complete normalized numerator modulo three on the remaining class cannot be replaced by the residue-one unit argument.
