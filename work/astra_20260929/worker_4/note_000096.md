First establish the bounds without assuming any endpoint congruences. For m∈{n,n+1}, j∈{0,1,2}, the terms of T_j(L_m)/f are
2^(−n) [(n!)²/(m!d!)] [(m+d)!/(n+d−j)!] u_{m,m+d} e_{n+d−j},
where 0≤d≤m, u_{m,h}=[t^h](1−2t+2t²)^m, and e_h=h!Σ_{a=0}^h1/a! is integral. The second factorial quotient is integral. The first bracket is five-adically integral: for m=n it equals n!/d!; for m=n+1 and d≤n it equals (n!/d!)/(n+1); and at d=n+1 it equals 1/(n+1)². Hence every T_j(L_m)/f is five-adically integral.

Applying ℓ_j(s^e)=1/(n+e+1−j)! to a term of ΔL_m, then multiplying by (n!)², produces
