Let z=2sqrt(2)-3, so z²+6z+1=0 and |z|=(1+sqrt(2))^(-2). Let P(x)=sum_{j=0}^J w_j x^j belong to Q[x], with P(1)=1. For approximants a_n to a target t, the normalized filter sum_j w_j a_{n+j} has error sum_j w_j E_{n+j}, where E_n=t-a_n.

Suppose z is a root of P of exact multiplicity m≥1. Its minimal polynomial f(x)=x²+6x+1 is irreducible over Q, so f^m divides P. Consequently J≥2m. If Q is the least positive common denominator of the weights, QP belongs to Z[x]. Division by the monic polynomial f^m shows QP=f^m H with H in Z[x]. Evaluation at 1 gives Q=8^m H(1), hence 8^m divides Q. The unique normalized filter of degree at most 2m with multiplicity at least m is P_m=f^m/8^m. Its actual common denominator is 8^m because its constant coefficient is 1/8^m. This extends the previously submitted m=1 result without asserting anything about the reduced denominator of the resulting approximant.

Now impose the additional analytic hypothesis
E_n=C z^n[1+a_1/n+a_2/n²+...+a_{m+1}/n^(m+1)+o(n^(-m-1))],
where C≠0 and a_1≠0. These coefficients and this expansion are assumptions, not newly established facts about the matched construction.

Write M_k=sum_j w_j z^j j^k. Since M_k=[(x d/dx)^k P(x)] evaluated at z, root multiplicity gives M_k=0 for k<m and M_m=z^m P^(m)(z)≠0. Expanding each fixed shift (n+j)^(-r), the first surviving contribution comes from r=1 and expansion order m. Every r≥2 contributes only at smaller order, and the finite sum of shifted remainders remains o(n^(-m-1)). Therefore
sum_j w_j E_{n+j}=C a_1 (-1)^m z^m P^(m)(z) z^n n^(-m-1)(1+o(1)).
Its absolute nth-root limit is |z|. Thus finite multiplicity cancellation gives a polynomial improvement under these hypotheses, while preserving the exponential rate.

For P_m, P_m^(m)(z)=m!(2z+6)^m/8^m. The leading constant simplifies to
(-1)^m z^m P_m^(m)(z)=m![(3sqrt(2)-4)/2]^m>0.

The requested computation independently forms the exact rational function sum_j w_j z^j/(n+j). After multiplication by product_{j=0}^{2m}(n+j), its numerator must have degree m and the displayed leading coefficient. Arithmetic takes place in Q(sqrt(2)), without floating-point evidence.
