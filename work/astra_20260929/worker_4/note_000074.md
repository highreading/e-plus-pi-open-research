2. Complete exponential contractions, with their boundary terms.

Let u_{m,j}=[t^j](1−2t+2t²)^m and e_j=j!Σ_{h=0}^j1/h!. The exact identities are
T_0(P)/f=2^(−n)Σ_{d=0}^n(n!/d!)u_{n,n+d}e_{n+d},

All summands are five-adically integral: the sole factorial quotient with a denominator occurs at d=n+1, where it is 1/(n+1), a unit. For d≤n−2, n!/d! contains n−1 and is divisible by five. Thus only two terms survive in the first sum and three in the second.

The required top coefficients are

u_{m,2m−1}=−m2^m,
u_{m,2m−2}=m²2^(m−1),
where these left-hand symbols denote u with the indicated subscripts. The recurrence e_j=je_{j−1}+1 gives residues e_{5h+d}=(1,2,0,1,0) for d=0,…,4. Hence
(e_{2n−1},e_{2n},e_{2n+1})≡(2,0,1).

The surviving P terms are exactly
−n²e_{2n−1}+e_{2n}≡3.
The surviving U terms are exactly

Their respective residues are 3,0,2, so T_0(U)/f≡0. In particular, omitting the d=n+1 boundary would change the conclusion.
