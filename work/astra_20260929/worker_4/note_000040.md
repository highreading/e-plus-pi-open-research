1. The denominator scalar is a unit.

The published coefficientwise derivative residues give, whenever 3|n,
H_n(1)≡1, J_n≡0, η≡0, J_{n+1}≡1, K_{n+1}≡2 (mod 3).
Hence S≡1, C≡2, W≡1, and k≡1.

Let A_m=L_m(1). Its integral generating series F(z) satisfies (1−4z−4z²)F(z)²=1. In characteristic 3, F(z)=(1+2z+2z²)F(z³). Thus
A_{3m}=A_m, A_{3m+1}=2A_m, A_{3m+2}=2A_m modulo 3.
Starting from A_0=1 shows every A_m is a unit. For 3|n, b≡2a, so D≡2b−2a≡2a≠0. In particular D and Y_raw are nonzero for every index in the stated progression, without an eventual-normality assumption.

2. Exact partial-exponential normalization.

Write (2t²−2t+1)^m=Σ_j u_{m,j}t^j. Rodrigues' identity gives
L_m(t)=(1/m!)(d/dt)^m(2t²−2t+1)^m,
[t^d]L_m(t)=(m+d)!u_{m,m+d}/(m!d!).
Set e_j=j!E_j, where E_j=Σ_{v=0}^j1/v!. Then e_j=j e_{j−1}+1, so e_{3j}≡1 and e_{3j+1}≡e_{3j+2}≡2 modulo 3.

For τ_P=T_P/f one obtains the exact expression
τ_P=2^(−n) Σ_{d=0}^n (n!/d!)u_{n,n+d}e_{n+d}.
Every d<n term is divisible by 3 because n!/d! contains n. The d=n term is e_{2n}, since u_{n,2n}=2^n. Therefore τ_P∈Z_3 and τ_P≡1.

Similarly,
τ_U=T_U/f=[2^n(n+1)]^(−1) Σ_{d=0}^{n+1}(n!/d!)(n+1+d)u_{n+1,n+1+d}e_{n+d}.
The possible denominator n+1 is a unit. Terms d<n vanish modulo 3. Since u_{n+1,2n+1}=−(n+1)2^{n+1} and u_{n+1,2n+2}=2^{n+1}, the remaining terms give
τ_U≡−2(2n+1)e_{2n}+[4/(n+1)]e_{2n+1}≡−2+8≡0.

A monomial moment μ_j=ℒ(t^j) has denominator dividing 2^j(j+1). The quotients defining w_P,w_U have integral coefficients and degree at most n. For j+1≤n, v_3(j+1)≤r; j+1=n+1 is a unit. Thus v_3(w_P),v_3(w_U)≥−r. Since v_3(1/f)=2r and r≥1, both w_P/f and w_U/f vanish modulo 3.

Consequently P*/f≡1 and U*/f≡0. The COMPLETE normalized numerator is

It is a unit. Therefore v_3(X)=−2r and v_3(q_n)=max(0,v_3(D)−v_3(X))=2r. The exact rational scaling also gives v_3(Y_raw)=−4r and v_3(X_raw)=−6r.

3. Primitive coefficient content and its cancellation.

Let ν be the minimum 3-adic valuation of every coefficient in the specified nonzero raw triple (A_raw,B_raw,C_raw). Multiplication to a primitive integer triple changes valuations by −ν.

First, every raw B coefficient has valuation at least −4r. Indeed the same Rodrigues factorial argument gives T_j(P)/f,T_j(U)/f∈Z_3 for j=0,1,2. In the published reconstruction, t_j=(aT_j(U)−bT_j(P))/G, where G=(−1)^n2^{2n+3}/(n+1) is a unit. The other cross-product row is g(η,J_{n+1},K_{n+1}), with v_3(g)=−2r. Hence the cross product defining B has coefficient valuations at least −4r. Its endpoint has valuation exactly −4r, so this is also the exact minimum for B.

Next every raw C coefficient has valuation at least −6r. To see the additional factorial loss explicitly, use
K_n(t,s)=[U(t)P(s)−P(t)U(s)]/[G(t−s)]
=[P(t)ΔU(t,s)−U(t)ΔP(t,s)]/G,
where ΔQ=(Q(t)−Q(s))/(t−s). A term of ΔL_m, for m=n or n+1, is [t^d]L_m times t^(d−1−e)s^e, with 0≤e<d. Applying ℓ_j(s^e)=1/(n+e+1−j)! and multiplying by (n!)² gives
[(n!)²/(m!d!)]·[(m+d)!/(n+e+1−j)!]·u_{m,m+d}.
The second quotient is integral; the first is 3-integral because m is n or n+1 and n+1 is a unit. Therefore (n!)²ℓ_j^sK_n(t,s) has 3-integral coefficients. The exact projection C_raw*=−Σ_j B_jℓ_jK_n proves the asserted −6r bound.

Finally A_raw is the negative Taylor polynomial through degree n of B_raw e^z+C_raw F(z). The exponential coefficients cost at most r additional valuation units. The coefficients of F(z)=Σ_{j≥1}μ_{j−1}z^j through degree n have valuation at least −L, where L=floor(log_3 n). Thus every A coefficient has valuation at least −6r−L. Together with the bounds for B,C this yields ν≥−6r−L. Since A_raw(1) has valuation −6r, necessarily ν≤−6r.

Put c=−6r−ν. Then 0≤c≤L, and the primitive endpoints have valuations c and 2r+c. Their gcd has valuation c, leaving exactly 2r in the reduced denominator. This tracks the primitive-content loss without identifying its precise value or equating it with Ω_n.
