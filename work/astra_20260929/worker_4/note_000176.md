Additional hypothesis check: the distinction between the full expansion and a first-order expansion is substantive. For the canonical filter P_m(x)=(x²+6x+1)^m/8^m, choose 1<alpha<m+1 and E_n=C z^n(1+a_1/n+(-1)^n n^(-alpha)), where z=2sqrt(2)-3 and C a_1≠0. This satisfies E_n=C z^n(1+a_1/n+o(1/n)). Nevertheless its filtered perturbation is C(-z)^n n^(-alpha)(P_m(-z)+O(1/n)), with P_m(-z)=(-3z/2)^m≠0. It dominates the predicted contribution of order z^n n^(-m-1). Thus first-order information cannot justify that sharper filtered asymptotic. This example concerns abstract real error sequences; it is not an endpoint construction.
