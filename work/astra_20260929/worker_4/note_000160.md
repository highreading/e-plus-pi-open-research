Unreviewed rational-target countermodel. Let λ=1+√2 and fix any C>0, including the audited prefactor C=4π/λ³. Put q_n=15^n, T_n=Cq_nλ^(−2n), m_n=1+15⌊T_n/15⌋, and p_n=q_n+(−1)^(n+1)m_n. Since m_n≡1 mod15, gcd(p_n,q_n)=1; thus q_n is the actual positive reduced denominator.

The estimate |m_n−T_n|≤14 gives
1−p_n/q_n=(−1)^n Cλ^(−2n)[1+O((λ²/15)^n)].
Because λ²<15, this has exactly the stipulated alternating asymptotic form, despite the target 1 being rational. Moreover q_n≥(3√5)^n/(1125n⁴) for every n≥1, and |q_n−p_n|=m_n→∞.

For Δ_n=p_(n+1)q_n−p_nq_(n+1), direct subtraction gives Δ_n=(−1)^n15^n(m_(n+1)+15m_n). Consequently
|Δ_n|∼15C(1+λ^(−2))(225/λ²)^n→∞.

This construction verifies compatibility of the abstract error, denominator-growth, and adjacent-determinant conclusions with a rational target. It does not satisfy or challenge the defining matched endpoint equations, and supplies no conclusion about the actual value e+π.
