The first erroneous formula in worker_4/note_000002.md is H_2(x)=x²−4x+6. This was a local constant-coefficient error, not a change in source normalization. In the multinomial formula the four admissible contributions are x², −4x, 2, 2. Thus H_2(x)=x²−4x+4. The original note remains part of the correction history and must not be cited without this correction.

Exact normalization: H_n(x)=n![z^n]e^{xz}(1−z+z²/2)^n. Let J_n, K_n, M_n be the first, second, and third derivatives of x^nH_n(x), respectively, evaluated at x=1. Define B_n with rows (H_n(1),J_n), (J_{n+1},K_{n+1}), (K_{n+2},M_{n+2}). Its ordered minors are I_01, I_02, I_12, each taken with increasing row order and the displayed column order. Ω_n is the nonnegative integer gcd of these three actual minors, without row division, factorial rescaling, or removal of prefactors.

For falling factorials (X)_j, define D_r(X)=Σ_{b,c≥0}(−1)^b(X)_{b+2c+r}(X)_{b+c}/(2^c b!c!). Direct multinomial expansion gives D_r(n)=H_n^{(r)}(1) for every nonnegative integer n. Terms beyond the derivative degree vanish. The H_n have integer coefficients: each term can be written as an integer multinomial coefficient times (n)_{b+2c}/2^c, and a product of b+2c consecutive integers contains at least c even factors.

Coefficientwise tail proof: on X=a+pY for an odd prime p, put R=b+2c, s=b+c, k=floor(R/p), and q=floor(s/p). Every falling factorial of length L has coefficientwise valuation at least floor(L/p). Moreover v_p(b!c!)≤v_p(s!)=q+v_p(q!). Therefore the summand has coefficientwise valuation at least floor((R+r)/p)−v_p(q!)≥k−v_p(k!). This is nonnegative and tends to infinity. Only finitely many pairs have bounded R, so the series converges in Z_p⟨Y⟩. For precision d, deleting R≥p ceil(d(p−1)/(p−2)) gives a tail in p^dZ_p⟨Y⟩, using v_p(k!)≤k/(p−1).

At p=3 the sharper precision-one cutoff R<3 suffices: for k≥1, the integer k−v_3(k!) is at least 1. In the retained terms all denominators are 3-adic units. Hence D_r(a+3Y) reduces coefficientwise to the constant D_r(a), for a=0,1,2. For these a, the retained sum equals the terminating integer-index sum. The derivative tuples for r=0,1,2,3 are (1,0,0,0), (0,1,0,0), and (1,−2,2,0).

The interpolated derivative combinations are J(X)=XD_0+D_1; K(X)=X(X−1)D_0+2XD_1+D_2; M(X)=X(X−1)(X−2)D_0+3X(X−1)D_1+3XD_2+D_3. These operations preserve coefficientwise congruences. On X=3Y the matrix therefore has constant reduction [[1,0],[1,2],[2,0]]. Before reduction, the corresponding matrix at n=0 is [[1,0],[1,2],[−4,0]], whose signed minors are (2,0,8). Thus the analytic minors reduce to (2,0,2), and I_01 is a unit throughout 3Z_3. In particular the analytic minors have no common zero there and v_3(Ω_n)=0 for every nonnegative integer n divisible by 3 under the displayed definition.
