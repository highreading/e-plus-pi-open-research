Unverified derivation. Write Q(z)=1−z+z²/2 and c_{a,t}=[z^t]Q(z)^a. Put m=n+1, h=h_m, j=J_m/m, k=K_m/m. Exact formulas with only powers of two in their denominators are
h=Σ_t(m)_t c_{m,t},
j=h+Σ_t(m−1)_t c_{m,t},
k=(m−1)h+2H'_m(1)+Σ_t(m−1)_{t+1}c_{m,t}.
Here falling factorials terminate automatically. Thus division by m need not be performed on a truncated residue.

Set A=T_0(P)/f and B=mT_0(U)/f. Coefficient reversal gives
A=Σ_{t=0}^n(n)_t c_{n,t}e_{2n−t},
B=4e_{2m−1}+2Σ_{t=1}^m(m−1)_{t−1}c_{m,t}(2m−t)e_{2m−1−t}.
The first term of B is precisely the singular boundary contribution, retained without approximation.

With C=(mj−h)J_n−m(k−j)h_n, the exponential part of Z is exactly
2A(mj²−hk)−BC−2h(jJ_n−kh_n).
For n≥14 in this progression, this expression equals Z modulo25 because the omitted moment contributions are 2(w_P/f)(S/m)−(mw_U/f)C, each of valuation at least two. For floor(log_5(n+1))=1, v_5(n!)≥2; for larger logarithmic index L, the published r≥L implies 2r−L≥2.
