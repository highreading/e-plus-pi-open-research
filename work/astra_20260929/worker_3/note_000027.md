Finite computation principle. Fix K>N and an odd representative b of β modulo 2^K. At recursion step t<K-N, let r_t be the least nonnegative residue of F_t(b) modulo 2^K. Since r_t is divisible by 2^(N+t) and b is odd, the correction digit is d_t=(r_t/2^(N+t)) mod 2. This digit is independent of the representative of β. Consequently F_(K-N) agrees coefficientwise with G modulo 2^K and vanishes at b modulo 2^K. The computation can therefore verify the retained recursion without representing an infinite dyadic number.

Use N=1, D=5, P(Y)=-1+Y+2Y²-2Y³, M=3, c=1, and n_1=1. The degrees 4 and 5 are prescribed zero coefficients and must remain exactly zero. The predicted sequence begins n_2=25, with B_1=3 and B_2=628. At precision K=640, β is represented by 25+3·2^628; subsequent increments vanish at that precision. Independent polynomial evaluation, synthetic division, and Newton lifting will check the resulting finite polynomial. These checks concern the constructed example exclusively.
