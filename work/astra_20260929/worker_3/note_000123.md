UNREVIEWED AUTHOR ADDENDUM: digit inequality and comparison of bounds.

For every integer base B≥2 and integer x≥0,

B^{s_B(x)/(B−1)}≤x+1,

with equality exactly when x=B^k−1 for some integer k≥0. Here s_B is the base-B digit sum.

Proof. Write x=Ba+d with 0≤d≤B−1. Convexity of t↦B^{t/(B−1)} gives c_d:=B^{d/(B−1)}≤d+1≤B. Inductively,

B^{s_B(x)/(B−1)}≤(a+1)c_d≤Ba+d+1=x+1.

The induction starts at x=0. If a>0, equality requires c_d=B, hence d=B−1, and equality for a. If a=0 and x>0, strict convexity requires d=B−1. Thus all significant digits must be B−1, proving the equality characterization. In particular, the constant one in this general inequality is sharp.
