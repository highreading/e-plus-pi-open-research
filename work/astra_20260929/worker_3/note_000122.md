Let s_B(m) denote the sum of the base-B digits of the nonnegative integer m. I claim
B^{s_B(m)/(B−1)}≤m+1.
The case m=0 is equality. Write m=Bu+r, with 0≤r≤B−1, and put a_r=B^{r/(B−1)}. Convexity of B^x on [0,1] gives a_r≤r+1, and also a_r≤B. Assuming the inequality for u, we obtain
B^{s_B(m)/(B−1)}=a_r B^{s_B(u)/(B−1)}≤a_r(u+1)≤Bu+r+1=m+1,
since (B−a_r)u+(r+1−a_r)≥0. Induction proves the claim. Equality holds at m=B^k−1, so the constant one in this general inequality is sharp.

For the endpoint application, retain the published normalization p_n/q_n=−X_n/D_n in lowest terms, q_n>0. The following application uses the previously audited arithmetic premises
v_3(q_n)≥2v_3(n!)+η_n,
v_5(q_n)=2v_5(n!),
for n≥6, n≡1 modulo five. The five-adic premise also supplies D_n≠0 throughout this arithmetic range. Here η_n is one on n≡1 modulo three and zero otherwise.

Legendre's formula yields
q_n≥3^{η_n}(3√5)^n/[3^{s_3(n)}5^{s_5(n)/2}].
Write n=5m+1. The elementary inequality above gives
3^{s_3(n)}≤(n+1)²,
5^{s_5(n)/2}=√5·5^{s_5(m)/2}≤√5(m+1)²=√5(n+4)²/25.
Consequently
q_n≥5√5·3^{η_n}(3√5)^n/[(n+1)²(n+4)²].
Relative to the previously submitted bound with denominator 9√5·n²(n−1)², the improvement factor is
225 n²(n−1)²/[(n+1)²(n+4)²],
which tends to 225. This comparison concerns lower bounds only.

Using separately the published fixed-b=2 error asymptotic, with ρ=1+√2, gives for all sufficiently large indices on this progression
|q_n(e+π)−p_n|≥[10π√5·3^{η_n}/ρ³]·[(3√5)/ρ²]^n/[(n+1)²(n+4)²].
The analytic threshold is unspecified and must not be identified with n=6. This supplement is an author derivation awaiting independent review; it establishes no denominator upper bound or rationality conclusion.
