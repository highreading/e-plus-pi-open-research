UNREVIEWED SUPPLEMENT: sharp transfer of finite precision through a simple-root normalization.

Let F,G belong to the restricted power-series ring Z₂⟨Y⟩, suppose F≡G modulo 2^M coefficientwise, with M≥1, and assume their common reduction modulo 2 is a+bY with b odd. Then each series has a unique root in Z₂, denoted α and β, and α≡β modulo 2^M.

Proof. Restrictedness ensures that reduction modulo 2 is a polynomial. The assumed affine reduction has exactly one root modulo 2 and derivative 1 there, so Hensel lifting gives existence and uniqueness. For x,y∈Z₂, the convergent divided difference satisfies
F(x)−F(y)=(x−y)U(x,y),
where U(x,y)≡b modulo 2: the linear coefficient is odd and every higher coefficient is even. Thus v₂(F(x)−F(y))=v₂(x−y). Since G(β)=0 and F(β)∈2^M Z₂, taking y=α proves v₂(β−α)≥M. This bound is sharp: F(Y)=Y and G(Y)=Y+2^M have roots differing by exactly 2^M.

Suppose an exact disk normalization is C(r+2^hY)=2^s F(Y), where s≥0. Knowing C(r+2^hY) coefficientwise modulo 2^N, with N>s, determines F only modulo 2^(N−s). Under the affine-reduction hypothesis, it therefore determines the corresponding index root ξ=r+2^hα modulo 2^(N−s+h). For the slope-eight disks and N=10, normalization by 4 permits index-root precision modulo 2^11, while normalization by 8 permits precision modulo 2^10. These are conditional generic bounds; a particular disk may have another factorization or no root.

For n=r+2^h m with m∈Z₂, the same divided-difference identity gives the exact scalar valuation
v₂(C(n))=s+v₂(m−α)=s−h+v₂(n−ξ),
with v₂(0)=+∞. If P is a finite approximation to F modulo 2^M, then v₂(P(m))<M determines v₂(F(m)) exactly; if P(m)≡0 modulo 2^M, the finite data provide only v₂(F(m))≥M. Accordingly, finite precision cannot resolve arbitrarily deep exceptional cancellation.

Finally, division by Y causes no additional dyadic precision loss when the exact constant coefficient is zero: it merely shifts coefficients. A constant coefficient known only to vanish modulo 2^N does not justify exact division in Z₂⟨Y⟩. This distinguishes the exact C(1)=0 identity checked in the assigned audit from its finite congruence certificate.

Scope: these are general analytic statements. They neither identify the germs with actual endpoint quantities nor bound approximation depth at the particular exceptional root. No independent publication approval is claimed.
