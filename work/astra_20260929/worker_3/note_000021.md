UNVERIFIED DERIVATION: PRESERVING A FINITE POLYNOMIAL PART WHILE PRESCRIBING A DEEPLY APPROXIMABLE ROOT

Let N≥1, let P∈Z[Y] have degree at most D, and suppose P(Y)≡Y−1 coefficientwise modulo 2. Choose an odd residue a modulo 2^N satisfying P(a)≡0 modulo 2^N. Such a residue exists uniquely by elementary Hensel lifting. Fix also any positive odd integer M and any residue c modulo M.

Choose a positive integer n_1 satisfying n_1≡a modulo 2^N and n_1≡c modulo M. Define

B_j=n_j²+j+N,
n_{j+1}=n_j+M·2^{B_j}.

Then n_j converges dyadically to an odd β≡a modulo 2^N. The exponents B_j increase strictly, and factoring the first term of each remaining tail gives

v_2(β−n_j)=B_j.

Every n_j remains congruent to c modulo M, and P(β)∈2^N Z_2.

We now construct an integer-coefficient restricted series G with exactly the same coefficients as P through degree D. Put F_0=P. Inductively, assuming F_t(β)∈2^{N+t}Z_2, choose d_t∈{0,1} so that

F_t(β)/2^{N+t}+d_t β^{D+1+t}≡0 modulo 2,

and put

F_{t+1}(Y)=F_t(Y)+d_t2^{N+t}Y^{D+1+t}.

Oddness of β makes the digit choice unique. The limit

G(Y)=P(Y)+Σ_{t≥0}d_t2^{N+t}Y^{D+1+t}

belongs to Z_2⟨Y⟩ and satisfies G(β)=0. It agrees exactly with P through degree D and satisfies G≡P coefficientwise modulo 2^N. For L≥D, the tail beyond degree L has Gauss valuation at least N+L−D. Its ordinary integer coefficients above degree D satisfy |[Y^r]G|≤2^{N+r−D−1}, so an exponential ordinary coefficient bound also holds.

Subtract G(β) and divide termwise by Y−β. The polynomial quotient (P(Y)−P(β))/(Y−β) is congruent to 1 modulo 2, because P≡Y−1 modulo 2. The quotient associated with the t-th added monomial has Gauss valuation at least N+t. Consequently

G(Y)=(Y−β)U(Y), with U∈1+2Z_2⟨Y⟩.

Thus β is the unique root in Z_2, G′(y)≡1 modulo 2 for every y∈Z_2, and

v_2(G(n_j))=v_2(n_j−β)=n_j²+j+N.

This checks that fixing a finite polynomial part, finite coefficient precision, a simple-root certificate, quantitative convergence, and fixed odd-modulus congruences can coexist with arbitrarily large linear-depth violations in a constructed function. It does not preserve the project's full coefficient sequence, recurrence, determinant identities, or endpoint normalization. Those additional structures remain possible sources of a bound for the actual exceptional root. This derivation has not received independent registry review.
