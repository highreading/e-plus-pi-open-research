Unreviewed topological supplement. Fix c>0, an odd positive integer m, and a residue r modulo m. For M≥1 define U_M as the union, over positive integers n≥M with n≡r (mod m), of the dyadic balls n+2^{ceil(cn)}Z_2. Each U_M is open. It is dense: given a basic ball a+2^k Z_2, the Chinese remainder theorem supplies arbitrarily large positive n satisfying both n≡a (mod 2^k) and n≡r (mod m). Choose such n≥M with ceil(cn)≥k. Its ball in U_M is contained in the prescribed basic ball. Completeness of Z_2 and the Baire category theorem now imply that E=intersection_{M≥1} U_M is dense G_delta. Membership means v_2(x−n)≥ceil(cn) for infinitely many distinct admissible n. Nevertheless E has Haar measure zero: for every M its measure is at most sum_{n≥M}2^{−ceil(cn)}, which tends to zero. For every s>0 the same covers have total s-power diameter bounded by sum_{n≥M}2^{−s ceil(cn)}→0, proving Hausdorff dimension zero. Thus topological genericity and measure-theoretic rarity coexist for precisely this approximation property. Neither determines whether a specified analytic root belongs to E; an arithmetic input specific to that root remains necessary.
