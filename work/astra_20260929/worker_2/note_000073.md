1. Auxiliary residues on all three classes.

Let q(s)=1−s+s²/2. The coefficient of x^j in H_m(x) is (m!/j!)[s^(m−j)]q(s)^m. These coefficients are ternary integral. If j lies below the largest multiple of 3 not exceeding m, then m!/j! is divisible by 3. Consequently only the top m mod3 plus one coefficients survive modulo 3. Since [s]q^m=−m and [s²]q^m=m²/2, this gives
H_m(x)≡x^m                         if m≡0,
H_m(x)≡x^m−x^(m−1)                if m≡1,
H_m(x)≡x^m−x^(m−1)+x^(m−2)        if m≡2.
The last formula is used only for m≥2. Differentiating these polynomial congruences and applying the definitions of J_m,K_m yields
(h_m,J_m,K_m)≡(1,0,0), (0,1,2), (1,0,2)
for m≡0,1,2 respectively.

and therefore (S,C,W)≡(1,2,0).

2. Complete normalized numerator for n≡1.

All summands are ternary integral when n≡1, including d=n+1 because n+1 is a unit. Terms d≤n−2 vanish: n!/d! then contains n−1, which is divisible by 3.

The needed coefficients are
