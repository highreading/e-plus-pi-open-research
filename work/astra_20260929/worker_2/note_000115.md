Independent preparatory audit of the conditional five-adic loss criterion.

Assume exactly the source hypotheses: for all sufficiently large positive integers n, p_n/q_n is the specified reduced endpoint approximant, q_n>0, v_3(q_n)≥2v_3(n!), and |ξ−p_n/q_n|=Cρ^(−2n)(1+o(1)), where ξ=e+π, ρ=1+√2, and C=4π/ρ³>0. Source identification and proofs of these hypotheses are not part of this supplementary audit.

Put a=v_5(q_n), b=2v_5(n!), and L=max(0,b−a). If a≤b, then a=b−L; if a>b, then L=0 and a≥b−L. Thus v_5(q_n)≥2v_5(n!)−L in both cases. Since q_n is a positive integer and 3 and 5 are distinct primes, unique factorization gives q_n≥3^[2v_3(n!)]5^[2v_5(n!)−L]. Legendre’s formula converts this to q_n≥3^[n−s_3(n)]5^[(n−s_5(n))/2−L]. No coprimality of factorial contributions or primitive polynomial normalization is being assumed.

For any positive integer n and prime p, set k=floor(log_p n). Its base-p expansion has k+1 digits, so s_p(n)≤(p−1)(k+1). Consequently 3^s_3(n)≤3^[2(k+1)]≤9n² when p=3, and 5^[s_5(n)/2]≤5^[2(k+1)]≤25n² when p=5. These elementary estimates hold for every n≥1. Combining them with the arithmetic hypotheses gives q_n≥(3√5)^n/(225n⁴5^L). The denominator estimate applies wherever those arithmetic hypotheses hold; their assumed eventual validity does not establish it at earlier indices.

The positive error asymptotic supplies a separate threshold beyond which |ξ−p_n/q_n|≥(C/2)ρ^(−2n). Multiplication by q_n therefore yields |q_nξ−p_n|≥K n^(−4)(3√5/ρ²)^n5^(−L), with K=2π/(225ρ³)>0. There is no effective numerical threshold asserted here.

Let η=log_5(3√5/ρ²). Its positivity follows exactly from 3√5>6>3+2√2=ρ². Define S_n=L−ηn+4log_5 n. The lower bound is precisely K5^(−S_n), which confirms both signs in the source’s limiting condition.

Along an unbounded subsequence with L=o(n), one has S_n=−ηn+o(n), hence S_n→−∞ and the lower bound tends to infinity. Therefore the actual absolute linear forms tend to infinity on that subsequence. In particular, L=O(log n) suffices.

Conversely, if the actual absolute linear forms tend to zero along a subsequence, their nonnegative lower bound K5^(−S_n) must also tend to zero. Because K>0, this is equivalent to S_n→+∞. Thus shrinking necessarily requires L_5(n)−ηn+4log_5 n→+∞. It also implies liminf L_5(n)/n≥η, but does not imply a strict inequality for that liminf. The full displayed condition is stronger than this weaker slope condition.
