Use the polynomials p_k, moment functional L, norms h_k, and factorial functionals ell_j from the supplied matched-endpoint source. Put a_k=p_k(1) and r_k=L((a_k-p_k(t))/(1-t)). The quotient is a polynomial, so r_k is rational. Since L(1/(1-t))=pi, chi_k=pi*a_k-r_k. Consequently, with
V=Σ_{k=0}^n a_k p_k/h_k,
Z=Σ_{k=0}^n r_k p_k/h_k,
we have the exact identity W=1/(1-t)-pi*V+Z.

Write v_j=ell_j(V), z_j=ell_j(Z), and E_m=Σ_{k=0}^m 1/k!. For n≥1,
ell_0(1/(1-t))=e-E_n,
ell_1(1/(1-t))=e-E_{n-1}.
For b=1 the specified row and column orders therefore give
D_V=v_1-v_0,
N=v_0 ell_1(W)-v_1 ell_0(W)
  =-D_V e+C_n,
C_n=v_1 E_n-v_0 E_{n-1}+v_0 z_1-v_1 z_0 ∈ Q.
