Unreviewed independent sharpness construction.

Fix a rational target α=u/v in lowest terms, v>0, constants β>1 and C>0, and any integer m≥1. There are reduced rational approximants p_n/q_n, with q_n>0 eventually, satisfying

α−p_n/q_n = (−1)^n C β^(−n)(1+O(β^(−n)))

and

q_n/β^n → m/(vC).

Proof. Choose integers a,b with ua−vb=1 and put s_n=(−1)^n. Define Q_n=mβ^n/(vC). Choose an integer t_n in the residue class 1 modulo m nearest to (Q_n−s_nma)/v, and set

q_n=s_nma+vt_n,   p_n=s_nmb+ut_n.

Then |q_n−Q_n|≤vm/2, so q_n is eventually positive and q_n=Q_n(1+O(β^(−n))). The integer matrix with rows (a,v) and (b,u) has determinant 1. Consequently

gcd(p_n,q_n)=gcd(s_nm,t_n)=1.

Also uq_n−vp_n=s_nm exactly. Therefore

α−p_n/q_n=s_nm/(vq_n)=s_nCβ^(−n)(1+O(β^(−n))),

and the stated denominator limit follows.

For the audited fixed-b constants β=ρ², C=4π/ρ^(b+1), ρ=1+√2, this realizes every value mρ^(b+1)/(4πv) permitted by the rationality-based accumulation criterion. In particular, even an exponentially small relative remainder in the leading error asymptotic is compatible with rationality in this abstract setting. The construction does not satisfy the prescribed Padé or endpoint equations and establishes no claim about their denominator sequence. Its role is to identify exactly why family-specific arithmetic information remains necessary.
