Unreviewed supplement: an explicit test of the sufficiency gap.

Let ρ=1+√2, α=ρ², and fix any real target ξ and constant C>0. Thus ξ=e+π and C=4π/ρ³ are permitted. Write F_p(n)=v_p(n!) and define
q_n=3^{2F_3(n)}7^n.
These are positive integers with v_3(q_n)=2F_3(n), v_5(q_n)=0, five-adic loss L_n=2F_5(n), and residual denominator factor H_n=7^n in the notation of note_000116.

For any prescribed signs σ_n∈{−1,1}, put t_n=q_nξ−σ_n Cq_nα^(−n), and choose
p_n=−1+21 floor((t_n+1)/21).
Then p_n≡−1 mod21, so gcd(p_n,q_n)=1. Moreover r_n=t_n−p_n lies in [0,21), giving the exact identity
ξ−p_n/q_n=σ_n Cα^(−n)+r_n/q_n.

Legendre’s formula gives q_n=21^n/3^{s_3(n)}. Since 3^{s_3(n)}≤9n² and 21>α, we have q_nα^(−n)→∞. Consequently
ξ−p_n/q_n=σ_n Cα^(−n)(1+o(1)),
while |q_nξ−p_n|∼Cq_nα^(−n)→∞.
Thus this example has genuine reduced numerators and denominators; the errors are not independently assigned artificial data.

Nevertheless, it satisfies both necessary loss conditions. With δ=log(3√5)−log α, the digit-sensitive expression is exactly
L_n log5−δn+s_3(n)log3+[s_5(n)/2]log5
=n log(α/3)+s_3(n)log3→+∞.
Also L_n/n→1/2, whereas η=δ/log5<1/2, so L_n−ηn+4log_5 n→+∞. Subtracting the omitted term log H_n=n log7 instead gives
n log(α/21)+s_3(n)log3→−∞,
consistent with divergence of the linear forms. Hence neither necessary condition becomes sufficient under the valuation and error assumptions alone. Additional identities specific to the endpoint family could still impose restrictions absent from this construction.

The following bounded check specializes to ξ=0, C=1, and positive error. It uses α^(−n)=A_n−B_n√2, where A_n²−2B_n²=1. An integer square root determines floor(q_nα^(−n)) exactly. Choosing the next integer k_n≡1 mod21 gives p_n=−k_n and 0<k_n−q_nα^(−n)<21. The computation checks reduction, factorial and digit identities, and the rounding bound at eight fixed indices. It has not yet run; the general argument above does not depend on numerical evidence.

An unreviewed construction shows that both necessary five-adic loss conditions can hold while the corresponding linear forms diverge, even for reduced rational approximants having the prescribed target and error constant. The uncontrolled residual denominator factor causes the divergence. This construction concerns arbitrary approximants, not the matched endpoint family, and gives no estimate for its actual denominators.
