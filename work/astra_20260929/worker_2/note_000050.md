Independent source comparison and nonvanishing audit; not yet a registered or independently approved claim.

Fix integers 1≤b≤n. Use the source definitions t=(1+iu)/2, L(P)=∫_{−1}^1P(t)du, C*(t)=t^nC(1/t), and ell_j(t^q)=1/(n+q+1−j)!. Put ell_B=Σ_{j=0}^b B_j ell_j. The approximation conditions are equivalent to
L(t^q C*)=−ell_B(t^q), 0≤q≤n+b−1,
together with A=−T_n(Be^z+CF) and the endpoint equation.

The explicitly nonzero bilinear norms h_k=L(p_k²)=2(−1)^k/[(2k+1)binom(2k,k)²] therefore give exactly
C*(t)=−Σ_{k=0}^n ell_B(p_k)p_k(t)/h_k.
The remaining rows are ell_B(p_k)=0 for n+1≤k≤n+b−1, and B(1)+ell_B(K_n(t,1))=0. Thus the source reduction is an equivalence, without a rank or endpoint-nonvanishing assumption. It inverts only the displayed h_k.

Let g(t)=1/(1−t), v_k=L(p_k g), H_n(t)=Σ_{k=0}^n v_k p_k(t)/h_k, and Ψ_n=g−H_n. Taylor reconstruction gives the complete identity
R(1)=ell_B(g)+L(C*g)=ell_B(Ψ_n).
The exponential series is absolutely convergent because of its factorial denominators. The logarithmic series can be integrated termwise because |t|≤1/√2 on the integration segment. Consequently this identity retains the entire remainder; it is not an estimate using its first surviving coefficient. Endpoint matching is needed to identify R(1)/Y with A(1)/Y+e+π, but not for this complete-tail identity itself.

A useful form of the Christoffel–Darboux remainder avoids dividing by v_n:
Ψ_n(t)=[v_n p_{n+1}(t)−v_{n+1}p_n(t)]/[h_n(1−t)].
Indeed N=(1−t)Ψ_n is a polynomial of degree at most n+1 orthogonal to every polynomial of degree at most n−1. Its leading coefficient is v_n/h_n. The recurrence p_{n+1}=(t−1/2)p_n+β_n p_{n−1}, with β_n=n²/[4(4n²−1)], gives L(p_n N)=−v_{n+1}, determining the other coefficient. The n=0 case follows directly from p_0=1 and p_1=t−1/2.

All the second-kind moments are nonzero. Orthogonality applied to (p_n(1)−p_n(t))/(1−t) gives
p_n(1)v_n=L(p_n(t)²/(1−t))
=2(−1)^n/binom(2n,n)² · ∫_{−1}^1 P_n(u)²/(1+u²)du.
Since p_n(1)>0, this proves sign(v_n)=(−1)^n for every n≥0. This argument uses no positivity assertion for the complex bilinear functional.

Set b_n=p_{n+1}(1)/p_n(1)>0 and α_n=v_{n+1}/v_n<0. The endpoint recurrence gives b_0=1/2 and b_n≥1/2. Applying the second-kind recurrence at index n+1 gives
|α_n|=β_{n+1}/(1/2+|α_{n+1}|)<1/6.
Thus 1+α_n/b_n>2/3 for every n≥0.

Christoffel–Darboux and endpoint symmetry now yield
V_n(0):=K_n(0,1)=2p_n(1)p_{n+1}(1)/|h_n|>0,
Ψ_n(0)/V_n(0)=−[v_n/(2p_n(1))](1+α_n/b_n).
In particular Ψ_n(0)≠0 and has sign (−1)^{n+1} for every n≥0. Defining ε_n=|v_n|/p_n(1), the latter identity is exactly
Ψ_n(0)/V_n(0)=(−1)^{n+1}ε_n(1+α_n/b_n)/2.
This verifies the source normalization and sign without recalculating the asymptotic prefactor.

For b=2, let a_j=ell_j(p_{n+1}), t_j=ell_j(V_n), w_j=ell_j(Ψ_n), and use the raw cross product B=a×(1+t). With Δ(P)=ell_1(P)−ell_0(P), Θ(P)=ell_2(P)−ell_1(P), and S(P,Q)=Θ(P)Δ(Q)−Δ(P)Θ(Q), direct determinant expansion gives
Y=det(a,1+t,1)=−S(p_{n+1},V_n),
R(1)=det(a,1+t,w)=S(p_{n+1},Ψ_n)+det(a,t,w).
Both source signs are correct.
