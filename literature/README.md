# Literature and applicability records

Primary sources and precise hypotheses matter. This publication preserves the project's literature applicability notes and claim reviews. It does not assert exhaustive coverage or novel priority, and preparation did not perform new mathematical research.

The classical reference points include the [NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions](https://dlmf.nist.gov/) for orthogonal polynomials, Laguerre/Rodrigues formulae, kernel identities and special-function normalization. Apply the exact theorem needed rather than a general similarity of methods.

General Lindemann–Weierstrass, logarithmic-form and algebraic-independence results cannot be assumed to give the current actual denominator or mixed-value conclusion. Conditional results retain their conjectural assumptions. Local Hensel root existence does not provide an ordinary-integer approximation bound.

The archived OpenAI collection screens discuss Families 005, 017 and 022 and later method candidates. These are dated applicability investigations; an advertised collection claim is not adopted as a theorem without checking its proof and hypotheses. Full downloaded paper trees, catalogue dumps, author profiles and screenshots are omitted from this release. Follow primary links retained in the notes.

The pinned claimed-solution reviews include explicit auxiliary counterexamples for the specified Carella and Foukzon versions. Their limited scope is part of the review. Scholarly author names are retained for attribution, while personal contributor metadata is removed.
