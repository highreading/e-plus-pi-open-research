# Candidate finite perturbation support argument — not an accepted theorem

This is a coordinator-authored proposed route for checking the next complete
quadratic digit. It must be derived or rejected in the original finite objects.
Producer locality alone does not prove corrected-column locality.

On the retained progression, v3(A)=v3(D)=5 and D>=486. Keep the original LOW,
residual, HIGH spaces, functional, full cutoff, physical terminal and actual
producer. Write 3P=Qc+3^6 Rprod, with Rprod divisible by3. The established
factorial saturation gives, at precision3^20,

Rprod=(y+1)x^(A-45) B20(x), degree B20<=45, modulo3^20.

The actual eliminated inverse has at worst one displayed LOW division by3;
its unit normalized inverse is explicit. Thus a perturbation insertion can
be charged conservatively at precision3^6. At precision3^20 at most three
such insertions can survive in a finite inverse expansion. The candidate
support budget uses kappa=45 and ell<=3, not arbitrary many perturbations.

Potential invariant to PROVE: a complete corrected column after ell actual
perturbation insertions is divisible by x^(D-ell*kappa), with its remaining
y-support on integer Omega20-grid neighborhoods of controlled width. This
requires checking the ACTUAL LOW projection, lower truncation, HIGH feedback
and physical top return for each insertion. The existing core closure proves
the ell0 case only. No coordinatewise orthogonality or inverse-support claim
is assumed for ell>0.

The worst two columns plus one producer insertion have seven kappa shifts:
7*45=315<D. If the invariant is true with a width still below half the grid
gap, multiplying the producer and the two complete columns retains a factor
x^H times an integral polynomial; this may exclude every lower odd-half-grid
pole at the paid modulus. Their upper-degree gap must separately exclude the
top pole. The factorial term remains in the complete functional with its own
valuation budget. This could prove a whole actual residual divisibility
directly and hence evaluate a further complete Q digit jointly, without
evaluating independent higher LOW and HIGH source jets.

The original window gives Omega20/D>147968/81. This is only a grid separation
resource, not a proof of the proposed invariant. A valid proof needs exact
width increments and every finite boundary copy, including any derivative
or remainder factor that consumes a nonunit division. If the LOW projection
produces a missing opposite-edge term, exhibit that actual term and its paid
order; do not discard it because the previous digit vanished.

The objective remains the complete original matrix and distinguished pair.
Even a new absolute common depth is not an inverse, an all-prime gcd estimate,
or a proof of the nonzero whole same-index error tending to zero.
