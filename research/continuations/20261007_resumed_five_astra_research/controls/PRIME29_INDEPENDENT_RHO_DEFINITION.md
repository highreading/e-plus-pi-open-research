# Recovered independent prime29 normalization

The independent definition, already present in the October5 original packet
and its complete A2turn15 source, is

C_n=CT_z(z^(-1)+2+2z)^n=[z^n](1+2z+2z^2)^n,
rho_n=1/(6 C_n).

It is the actual first-force entry f0^0, not a definition from the mixed/norm
ratio. The definition source used an older original exponent window; its
algebraic definition and equality to f0^0 hold for all n. Its older window-
specific numerical residue is not transplanted to the current family.

Current A2turn0 Theorem4.1 proves that C_m is a29-adic unit for EVERY integer
m>=0: all29 one-digit values are nonzero, and Frobenius with Laurent support
gives C_(d+29r)=C_d C_r modulo29. The parent independently verifies that entire
one-digit table by exact polynomial multiplication in a separate certificate.
Thus v29(rho_n)=0 on the original family. No negative-valuation normalization
is needed at this prime for this rho definition.

There is a further exact original-index relation. For q=(69b-7)/29,
n=29^2 q+203=29^2 q+29*7. Consequently C_n=2 C_q modulo29.
The unchanged low residues also give q=29 W+24, so C_q=25 C_W modulo29,
and C_n=21 C_W modulo29. These are unit identities, not an evaluation of the
huge complete original upper word. The current sources prove C_W is nonzero
but do not yet provide a feasible original-word accepting-value calculation.

The primitive target at K=c+4+nu becomes, without a nonunit inverse,

6 C_n x^T Y^[K] -29^(K-1) eta =0 modulo29^K,
where x^T x=29^nu eta and eta is a29-adic unit.

The full-source equivalent is

6 C_n Z_w^T Y -29 Z_w^T Z_w =0 modulo29^(d+2).

Multiplication by6 C_n preserves valuation because it is a unit. It does not
evaluate either scalar or justify dropping any source, endpoint, or logarithmic
term. The precision needed for C_n in the first product depends on the proved
valuation of that complete product. Only after the requisite mixed divisibility
is established can a single C_n digit be claimed sufficient.

A natural exact renormalization is fbar=f^0/C_n, fbar0=1, and zbar=A^-1 fbar.
Both divisions are by a proved29-adic unit. The relative target can be factored
by C_n^2, leaving a complete normalized-source pairing with multiplier6 and
the original terminal. Any further head-shape or feedback identity must be
proved at the actual growing precision; a fixed flat58 approximation is not
an all-depth identity.
