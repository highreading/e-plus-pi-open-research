# Higher producer jets: a direct degree exclusion

Coordinator derivation for independent review. It concerns the exact complete
functional and A1turn3's sensitivity formula, not an arbitrary support theorem.

The retained cutoff satisfies 4n-3=4H-4D+5<9H. Its only odd denominator of
valuation h is3H=3^h, at rstar=(3H-1)/2. All other retained odd denominators
have valuation at most h-1. The factorial summand has factor3^h and its4
denominator is a unit. Hence, on integral polynomials in the stated cutoff,

M(G)=[y^rstar](G-G(-1))/(y+1) modulo3.

This uses every retained denominator before reducing, rather than deleting a
selected pole. Thus deg G<=rstar implies M(G)=0 modulo3.

The independently reconciled complete core construction gives F_i=z_i modulo3,
so deg(F_i modulo3)<=d-1. A1turn3 defines

O_i=U Lc^-1 a_i-kappa tau_i (Y_d-U Lc^-1 Xd).

Here LOW spans degrees at most D-1; all normalized inverse coefficients are
integral. Therefore deg(O_i modulo3)<=d. If P is integral of degree at most
A+2, then

deg(P(F_j O_i+F_i O_j) modulo3)<=A+2+2d-1=H+2D-1.

This is below rstar because the original window implies4D-1<H. Consequently
the ENTIRE sensitivity formula in A1turn3 vanishes modulo3 for all such P.
The actual producer has degree at most A+2, so subtracting an integral
coefficientwise lift of its lower27 jets does not violate this degree bound.

Subject to the already displayed exact sensitivity formula and core inverse
replacement, K18 is unchanged by mathscrR -> mathscrR+27P for actual-degree P.
Thus the fourth and higher divided-producer digit layers do not require an
independent source-jet evaluation for this next digit. This is stronger than
merely labeling that sensitivity unknown. It does NOT evaluate the remaining
lower-jet contribution, Vc_dd/3, full radical or distinguished cofactor.

One can also apply the same degree argument to M(P Uu Fi): its degree is at
most A+2+(D-1)+(d-1)=H+d, below rstar. Likewise M(P Yd Fi) has degree at most
H+2D-1. Their leading residues vanish separately. The possible source at the
physical HIGH terminal m is different and must remain; the degree exclusion
