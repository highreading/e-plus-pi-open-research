# Research scope for the complete abstract screen

The objective is an unconditional proof that e+pi is rational or irrational.
No such proof has been accepted. We are using five real external Astra streams;
A4 independently reviews new proofs. The three priority papers are Family005,
Family017 and Family022. Catalogue abstracts are claims to screen, not proved
lemmas. This compact scope is not a substitute for full original definitions
when a candidate is selected for actual application.

The main approximation route has complete exponential and logarithmic forcing,
finite inverse matrices, physical endpoint corrections, factorial and rational
normalizations, actual column contents, a least simultaneous clearer, and a
final gcd over ALL primes. Its analytical whole-error/nonvanishing theorems have
their original scope; the outstanding arithmetic question is the actual final
primitive denominator. A valid conclusion needs integer p_n,q_n and
0<abs(q_n(e+pi)-p_n)->0 on ONE AND THE SAME infinite original index set.
Different original families cannot share gains without a proved identity.

Ternary stream A1: the complete moment functional contains both a factorial
term -3^h(2t)!/4 and a pole term. A pole-only Jacobi replacement is invalid.
Original indices lie in a recovered modular progression with a fixed interior
degree window. Accepted precision29 work finds a growing radical but retains
complete producer corrections. Endpoint-adapted finite Schur elimination gives
T=[[a,z^T],[z,C]], with T divisible by27 and diagonal lambda of valuation -1.
For d=1-lambda*a, the relative determinant is d det(C+lambda/d zz^T).
An actual directional bound C^{-1}z in 3^{-1}Z_3 would prevent the worst
cancellation, but it is open. Leading-rank or common-determinant depth alone
does not control this relative scalar. Actual endpoint-diagonal factors and
the finite moment range must remain present.

Prime29 stream A2: b=3^(249005515+574312172u), n=2001b, u=2 mod29^9.
The complete normalized force, finite exterior factorial filtration, physical
endpoint, fixed source digits and a unit rho are accepted. One column's content
gain is at most logarithmic in n; the full norm valuation nu is NOT bounded by
that fact. The source displacement and interior log homogeneity are archived.
A new normalization-specific logarithmic charge determinant is
-2^(n+1)(n!)^2/b!, under independent review; its Legendre Casoratian is classical.
A conditional factorial-content transfer still needs the actual complete
exponential residue and the auxiliary scalar clearer's cost. Hahn polynomials
must retain the missing upper finite boundary. A rational first-order
antidifference obstruction does not exclude all higher-order constructions.

Endpoint stream A3: original n=15^r or105^r, r>=2. The fixed-seed forced
Legendre companion has complete exponential plus factorial-logarithmic source.
The actual integer frame and projected residual Xi_j are recovered; j=0 has
an extra omitted-column correction. Accepted exact denominator includes
|R_j|/gcd(R_j,C_j) times n!/gcd(n!,zeta_j), and a retained extra F payment.
Almost-everywhere Diophantine statements do not select this fixed real value
or these sparse original indices. A universal six-component rational tensor
gauge is rigorously impossible within that entire gauge class. A recent
source decomposition has a logarithmic component of exp(O(n)) height but a
complete first forced source with denominator n!, so a coordinatewise
factorial-normalization shortcut fails. Actual projected adjoint cancellation
or a subfactorial correlated-contact estimate remains open.

Binary stream A5: b=9^(18+32u), n=4002b, u>=0; contact0..b-1,
physical reconstruction0..b with z_b=0. Complete x=(1/2)R A^{-1}f,
y=(R A^{-1}(h^e+h^F)+e_0)/(4b!), x=2^a x_0. Actual a>=1 is accepted,
as are a full inverse modulo8, an exact two-binomial criterion for a=1,
complete source integrality and finite boundary completion. A claim a>=2 on
u=1 mod4 and the complete short adjoint are under review. The norm is
Q=x_0^Tx_0, and its linear parity acceptance is
S=sum_(j<b)(n+1-j)binom(n+2,j)z_j^f, with S/2^(a+1) integral.
A new unreviewed full-return theorem claims v2(S)>=1+chi,
chi=v2 binom(n+b-1,b-1). Paid excess chi-a is NOT proved positive on an
infinite original family. A sufficient endpoint upper bound is a<=v2 binom(g,d),
d=(b-1)/4,g=(n/2+1)/2. The resulting excess is
2s2(4002b)-s2(4003b-1)-s2(4001b+3). Unbounded raw chi is not paid growth.
Actual least clearer, column contents and primitive 2x2 minor saturation are
isolated, but minor saturation does not exclude scalar norm isotropy. The
original primitive denominator has known v3(q)=n-(b+15)/2, retained throughout.

Existing tools already used include creative telescoping, hypergeometric/Hahn
and Legendre recurrences, factorial prime valuations, Kummer/Lucas digit
rules, Cartier/Frobenius reductions, Chebyshev kernels, mixed determinants,
signed whole-error bounds, gcd/overlap methods and fixed-logarithm lower bounds.
Similar terminology alone is not a new route. Especially promising candidates
would supply a deterministic mixed-period integer construction, a genuinely
paid ALL-prime scalar-cofactor bound, a compatible compact replacement for the
factorial functional, or a rigorous nonvanishing/archimedean determinant bridge.
Full primary proofs and original hypotheses must be checked before adoption.
