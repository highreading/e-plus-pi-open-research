# Existing graded algebra for the candidate ternary sector ranks

Current A1turn7 establishes a candidate-sector radical lower bound242, but not
its exact rank or next actual Schur digit. Source-archive keyword searches for
Iima, Iwamatsu, Lefschetz and modular Jordan partitions found no match. This is
narrow retrieval evidence, not an exhaustive literature-absence statement.

Primary source read: Lisa Nicklasson, *The strong Lefschetz property of monomial
complete intersections in two variables*, arXiv:1703.07970, Collect. Math.69
(2018),359–375: https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.07970
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.07970
The parent inspected the introduction and §4 syzygy definition, Proposition4.7,
and Theorem4.11 with its proof. The paper relates multiplication maps in
two-variable monomial complete intersections to the degrees of two syzygy
generators. It supplies positive-characteristic criteria in terms of distance
to odd lattice points at powers of p. These are established tools; merely
classifying failure of the strong Lefschetz property does not determine the
rank of the present specified degree map or its actual endpoint observation.

Related primary search hits: Iima–Iwamatsu, arXiv:math/0612437, and modular
Jordan partitions arXiv:1403.4685. Only search abstracts have been inspected;
no full theorem from these is claimed here. An ungraded Jordan type alone
needs graded information before it answers the current question.
