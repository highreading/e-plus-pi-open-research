# Higher-radical and Jacobi reuse gate

Current A1turn12 evaluates a proposed actual second-radical operator on a
fixed original subwindow and an intermediate diagonal valuation. The parent
new sparse auxiliary certificate checks11665 divided coefficients,29524
series/recurrence coefficients and the scalar coefficient. It is not a proof
of actual corrected jets or original-index infinitude; independent review is
required for those hypotheses.

The coordinator searched the older ternary reports before choosing a next
approach. Jacobi/Christoffel formulas are already extensively developed.
October6 A1turn19 explicitly distinguishes the pole-only core from the
COMPLETE core, retaining its factorial form. It also proves that projected
Jacobi channels cannot replace primitive affine residual columns or the unit
physical endpoint. Growing polynomial content and a four-digit Christoffel
loss were already accounted for. These are closed obstacles to a direct
content-transfer shortcut and should not be rediscovered.

NIST DLMF18.3 Table18.3.1 and18.2(v) were checked as primary classical
orthogonal-polynomial references. They supply Jacobi norms/weights and the
Christoffel--Darboux machinery at their normalizations, not the missing
complete-source scalar or paid precision-growing inverse theorem.

- https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.3
- https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.2#v

The new target is a precision-growing endpoint-controlled elimination or an
evaluated higher relative cofactor on the COMPLETE original matrices. A
fixed extra digit or common determinant multiplicity is insufficient. No
exhaustive absence of literature is asserted.
