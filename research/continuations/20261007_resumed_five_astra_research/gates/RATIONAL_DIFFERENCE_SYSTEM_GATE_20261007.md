# Rational difference systems: existing primary method to reuse

Primary paper personally inspected: Mark van Hoeij, Moulay Barkatou and Johannes
Middeke, [A Family of Denominator Bounds for First Order Linear Recurrence
Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.02926), arXiv2007.02926v1 (2020).
The abstract, definitions, shift-case setting and Algorithm10 in the
[primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.02926) were read.

The paper constructs denominator/content bounds for rational solutions of
tau(Y)=M Y when tau is an automorphism of a unique factorization domain and
M is invertible over its fraction field. In the ordinary shift case the
domain is Q[n] with tau(n)=n+1. Its bounds reduce rational solution search
to polynomial solution search; global and componentwise variants are supplied.
This existing method should be applied rather than replaced by an unsupported
fixed-denominator ansatz.

Application still requires forming the exact invertible homogenized system,
computing a valid bound for its actual coefficient matrix and proving a
sufficient polynomial degree bound. The 42-variable failed class in the parent
certificate is not such a universal bound. This paper supplies no contact
resonance gcd or e+pi theorem. Archive source-title search found no saved
2007.02926 note; that limited search is not an exhaustive novelty claim.
