# All-prime factorial-transfer and representation gate

The coordinator searched the Desktop archive before continuing this topic.
The binary column-normalization bridge, full source recurrence/rank3 reduction,
factorial beta-digit bridge, and multi-output Smith obstruction already exist.
They must be reused at their individual producer scopes. A common factorial
factor in a raw column is not a new theorem about the final primitive q.

The primary paper by Zudilin, arXiv1310.1526v3, was checked at Proposition1,
Lemma7 and the concluding arithmetic discussion. It obtains coefficient
divisibility from parameter permutations of the SAME normalized linear form,
with prime exponents given by factorial floor differences. Combining identical
representations can strengthen an arithmetic divisor. Its construction targets
zeta(2); it does not furnish an identity for the present complete e+pi producer.
Its finite residual-gcd observations also illustrate the distinction between
numerical extra content and a proved asymptotic gain.

- https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.1526

The coordinator also checked the primary October2026 preprint2610.02912v1,
sections2--4. It claims an eventual identity between two zeta(2) linear-form
families by first cancelling the irrational coefficient using a terminating
hypergeometric transformation, then comparing a rational difference's size
with its paid denominator. That cancellation is an essential prerequisite.
No ancillary code was downloaded or run. The paper's numerical certificates
and global claims are not adopted as a theorem about e+pi.

- https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02912v1

Inference for continuing research: a useful adaptation would need a genuine
identity for the complete ORIGINAL norm/mixed pair or its actual linear form,
including both sources and finite returns. Matching approximate values or
combining arithmetic gains from distinct original families is insufficient.
Neither primary source supplies that identity. No exhaustive absence of
literature is asserted.
