# Existing fixed-logarithm bound for the adjacent-window radical

The parent verified the standard fixed-algebraic-number logarithm bound before
accepting the unbounded-radical implication. Primary publication metadata and
abstract: Matveev, Izvestiya64(2000),1217--1269,
https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/im314
https://doi.org/10.1070/im2000v064n06ABEH000314
The English full-text link returned the publication landing page, so no full
original-paper reading is claimed. The exact stated bound and its hypotheses
were checked in Evertse's author-hosted lecture, Chapter5 Theorem5.2 and
Corollary5.3 (PDF pages2--3):
https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~evertsejh/dio17-5.pdf

For fixed positive algebraic bases and integer exponents, a nonzero real
logarithmic form has a lower bound c B^(-M), with constants independent of
the exponents. In current A1turn8 the bases are3,4,C*=3^26-1. The original
identity gives Lambda=(h-27)log3+logC*-jlog4>0 and
Lambda<2r/(C*P), while exponent height B=O(logP). Therefore
r>=c'P/(logP)^M, excluding bounded radical dimension on any infinite original
subfamily in the adjacent window. This is an application of established
mathematics, not a new general logarithm estimate or an actual residual digit.
