# Repaired actual transport and a stronger producer bound at the next radical digit

## Abstract

The recovered coefficient identity repairs the transport premise identified by the referee. Its essential content is not a finite producer receipt: it is the exact degree reset


$$
\deg(3P_n-Q_c)\le A+1
$$


together with the exact coefficients, integral normalized producer vectors, and the signed endpoint identity.

Using these identities, I prove the required complete finite-multiplier statement and obtain


$$
\Phi_R\in3^{21}M(\mathbb Z_3).
$$


Thus, at the scope of the retained finite-core and paid-chain theorems specified below, the actual depth-$26$ operator is the turn8 Hankel operator. Its macroscopic terminal radical is unchanged.

There is also a further consequence. Using the retained mixed-observation bound


$$
T=\bigl(\mathcal M(\mathscr R\,wF_i)\bigr)\in3M
$$


and three additional factorial factors, one obtains


$$
\boxed{\Phi_{\mathscr R}\in3^{21}M,\qquad \Phi_R\in3^{22}M.}
$$


This is a new argument, not an inference that divisibility by $3^{21}$ makes the next digit zero. It shows that the producer contribution to the first radical digit actually vanishes, under the explicitly retained mixed-observation theorem.

The remaining first-radical-digit problem is therefore a core problem at precision $3^{28}$. At that precision, total corrected-column digit order two cannot be discarded. I isolate its unknown second-jet contribution and distinguish it from the finite-rank physical-terminal contribution. I do **not** obtain an evaluated rank for the resulting restricted operator on an infinite original subwindow.

No conclusion about rationality or irrationality of $e+\pi$ follows. The relative cofactor, actual primitive denominator, all-prime final gcd, and nonzero whole-error comparison remain unresolved.
