# 1. Scope and proof conventions

The two localizations concern different original families. They will not be combined into a single index set.

Throughout, a statement such as $X\in p^rM$ means entrywise divisibility over $\mathbb Z_p$, allowing negative $r$ when an inverse has a paid denominator.

Four kinds of conclusions must be distinguished:

1. **Recovered defining identity:** an exact consequence of the attached original definitions.
2. **Proved implication from retained mathematics:** a derivation whose precise source hypotheses are listed, without reconstructing their earlier proofs.
3. **Auxiliary finite certificate:** a calculation establishing only the explicitly bounded assertion it checks.
4. **Open obligation:** an unevaluated original-object quantity or an unproved infinite-family estimate.

In particular, a local congruence does not establish an accepting value unless the complete quantity on its right-hand side is evaluated.
