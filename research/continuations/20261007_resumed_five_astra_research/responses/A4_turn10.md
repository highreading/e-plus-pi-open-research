## 5. Uniform failure of the once-divided unit criterion

### 5.1 A boundary-complete extension below $1/9$

Turn10 states its nonzero rank formula on fixed interior subwindows. The elementary argument actually gives a uniform finite statement.

**Proposition.** Suppose the divided-tail formula holds and $0<N_0/P_0<1/9$. Put


$$
k=C-N_0-2-(L-1)
=\frac{P_0/3-3N_0}{2}>0.
$$


Then


$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}\mathcal W=\max(0,L-k),\qquad
\dim\ker\mathcal W=\min(L,k).
}
$$



**Proof.** The polynomial $(y+1)^2x^{N_0}$ has degree $N_0+2$ and leading coefficient $1$.

If $k\ge L$, even the smallest extraction index exceeds its degree, so the matrix is zero.

If $0<k<L$, the first $k$ rows and columns are zero. On the remaining square block, the antidiagonal $u+v=k+L-1$ extracts the leading coefficient $1$, and all entries on its degree-forbidden side vanish. Reversal of one coordinate order gives a triangular matrix with unit diagonal. ∎

This handles tuples arbitrarily close to either transition; no density or fixed-distance hypothesis is needed for singularity. Equality $N_0/P_0=1/9$ is impossible for sufficiently large original tuples because it would give $r=P/9$, contradicting $r\equiv2\pmod9$.
