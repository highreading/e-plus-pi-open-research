# Actual compact denominator prime interval: diagnostic gate

Coordinator, 8 October 2026. This permits only cheap new trial division of
the already certified actual primitive denominators at k=32 and k=33. No
determinant is recomputed and no original binary index is replaced by either

that every prime in (2k,6k-5] survives in q_k. It is not an asymptotic theorem.
