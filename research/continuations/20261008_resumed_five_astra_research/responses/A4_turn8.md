# Paid transport of the complete producer through the first radical return

## Abstract

The rationality or irrationality of $e+\pi$ remains unresolved.

This report proves a new local statement about the **complete actual producer**, not merely its complete-core substitute. The proof retains the original index family, the finite LOW/HIGH space, the physical terminal $Y_m$, the complete signed force $t+\xi v$, the degree-$30$ reciprocal, and all prefix, $J$- and rank-$b$ matrix returns.

$$
\boxed{

\in 3^6\operatorname{Mat}(\mathbb Z_3).

\tag{A}
$$


In the notation of A4 Turn 5, this includes the evaluated producer estimates


$$
\boxed{\Delta_{GG}\in9\operatorname{Mat}(\mathbb Z_3),\qquad

\tag{B}
$$


Consequently,


$$
\boxed{
\frac{T_{\mathrm{act},\mathrm{red}}}{81}
\equiv-\mathsf H_\kappa\pmod3,
}
\tag{C}
$$


and, on the exact full radical $\mathscr G$ supplied by A1 Turn 5,


$$
\boxed{
\frac{\mathscr G^T
(T_{\mathrm{act},\mathrm{red}}-T_{c,\mathrm{red}})

\equiv0\pmod3.
}
\tag{D}
$$


Thus the complete producer contributes **zero to the first divided radical digit**. This conclusion is uniform on the original amplitude space and therefore does not require shrinking the full radical.

The multiplier overflow above $m$ is proved coefficientwise divisible by $3^{26}$, before any finite-space observation is applied.

The matrix result does **not** evaluate the actual returned endpoint or the $1/9$ diagonal return. The endpoint obstruction is reduced below to an explicit three-entry test in the actual returned $K$-coordinates. The diagonal obstruction is an exact residue requirement for a specified quadratic form. Neither is replaced by an unreturned evaluation functional.

No growing primitive-denominator saving, nonzero whole-error estimate, or proof concerning the rationality of $e+\pi$ follows.
