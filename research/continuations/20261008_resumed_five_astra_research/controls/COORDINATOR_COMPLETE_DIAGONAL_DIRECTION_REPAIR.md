## Exact border and unconditional physical orders

For an exact endpoint-normalized pair with matrix

    3^k*[a,w^T;w,B], f=e0,

the endpoint-pivot determinant is -(1-3^k*lambda*a). Let

    u=1-3^k*lambda*a,
    c=3^k*lambda/u.

Whenever k+v3(lambda)>=1, u is a unit and the exact residual matrix is

    3^k*(B+c*w*w^T).

## The original3^-1 directional allowance is still preserved

In the adapted frames k>=4 and lambda_new>=-2, one has v3(c)>=2.
In the different monomial-first second-kernel frame k>=6 and lambda4>=-4,
one also has v3(c)>=2. In both specified actual frames, therefore,

    B*x=w, x in3^-1*Z3^s
       iff (B+c*w*w^T)*v=w, v in3^-1*Z3^s.

Proof: in the forward direction use

    v=x/(1+c*w^T*x).

Since w is integral, v3(c*w^T*x)>=1, so the denominator lies in1+3Z3.
Substitution gives the exact equation and preserves the allowance.
Conversely, from the perturbed equation,

    B*v=(1-c*w^T*v)*w.

Again the displayed scalar belongs to1+3Z3. Dividing v by it gives x
with B*x=w and the same allowance. This argument uses no inverse of B
and remains valid if B is singular. Every previously incurred endpoint,
matrix and diagonal return remains present in the exact definitions.
