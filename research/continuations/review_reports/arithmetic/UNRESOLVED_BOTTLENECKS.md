# Stronger arithmetic goals still unresolved: one page

Reviewer：/root/literature_map。Date：2026-10-04。

The decisive asserted propositions in the ten source manuscripts are valid under their respective conditions, with no definite error requiring changes found. The stronger route goals below are retained by the sources. They do not affect correctness of those propositions or leave gaps in the raw all-parity exclusion completed by the primary reviewer.

1. **Complete large-prime saturation and depth/size of H.** H has at most one nonunit factor and J at most two. H/ηᴴ and X/θ retain complete Smith depths, with vηᴴ≤vθ. These results are settled. Unit status of ηᴴ or an effective depth/size bound still requires separate proof. Factor counts do not estimate exponents, and unit ζ does not estimate H's final row.
2. **Endpoint restriction.** J's elimination carrier has content e+s, where s is the endpoint restriction on H's free kernel. For dual endpoints, the complete scalar is K/Θ. Do not confuse h,Θ, or matrix 𝖹 with scalar Z. Modn fixes only truncated depth; for p>3n it gives no automatic information at p. Cross-n entry recurrences have not produced a content upper bound.
3. **Actual q uses only the final gcd of Z/N.** Since ΔA=(−1)^n n!FN and ΔB=(−1)^(n+1)FZ, all of n!F cancels and q=|Z|/gcd(N,Z). Estimates for F may help E/F=Z but cannot be treated directly as gains in q. At large primes, vN and vZ still need comparison. The source does not claim this global goal solved.
4. **Distinguish settled and unsettled p-power cases.** For n=mp^ν with odd p, 3m<p, and n>p, N is a unit and vq=(n−m)/(p−1)+v(dII). Seed v_p(A_m)=0 or 1 gives exact dII depths for every ν≥1 or ν≥2 respectively. A4=136 handles 4·17^ν,ν≥2. Nonvanishing for seeds≥2 and the ν=1 exceptional correction are not asserted proved. The n=p=7 case is separately and correctly recorded with vq=0.
5. **Adjacent common-core propagation.** ζ is indeed a unit for p>3n and must not remain an unresolved target. But the two-new-column 2×2 unit pivot, lost-low-row term, and Schur/exterior correction need independent control. Lower divisibility ζ|gcd(D_n,D_(n+1)) gives no upper bound.

These follow-up questions prove neither rationality nor irrationality of e+π. The primary reviewer accepted actual |q_n(e+π)−p_n|→∞ for the fixed original family.
