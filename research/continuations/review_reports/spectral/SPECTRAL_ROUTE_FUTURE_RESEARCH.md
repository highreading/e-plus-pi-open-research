# Next step for the spectral route: fix the object before choosing the mathematical task

Attribution: `/root/proof_inventory`. October 4, 2026. Independent recommendations; the main register was not changed.

**Shrinking has been excluded for the same original raw rational-approximation family.** Document 1999 and main review 204 establish `|q_n(e+π)−p_n|→∞` for sufficiently large indices of both parities. Changing only the basis, cutoff, energy metric, or normalization while obtaining the same `p_n/q_n` does not change the actual q or error. Nonzero integer multiples and every index subsequence tending to infinity are also covered. Matrix identities and explicitly conditional theorems may remain valid; stronger conclusions that a manuscript does not claim are not treated as manuscript errors.

1. **If this spectral matrix framework is pursued, aim for a structural theorem.** The most precise remaining target is `T_D^T T_D=I−D_a^T(I−D_gD_g^T)^{-1}D_a` at the same cutoff, or an inf-sup bound in the actual data norm of document 2059. A full resolvent Gram matrix, rank, density, or a coefficient right inverse cannot substitute for that target. Decide first whether to prove nonzero rank, a specified number of controlled directions, or a concrete lower bound; these tasks have different strengths. Success would still not restore shrinking for the same raw family.

2. **Document 2254 supplies a single-channel starting point.** The present review confirms its polynomial-weight test, exact data relabeling, first-channel good-space orthogonality, and retained `√(2/n)` bound on a `D−O(√n)` data band. The object to study is the actual cross-moment map in equation (11), projected onto that data band and the other good channel. A useful result would provide an explicit boundary formula, an exact low-rank decomposition, or a controlled norm. Positivity and one lower degree of `F_b−F_a` alone do not imply such a result. Estimates must retain `Σ_ab` and the exact degree caps.

3. **List the energy and physical problems separately.** Even if the residual energy block has full column rank, the physical kernel must still be studied through `J_e=S^{-1}F_LL^{-1/2}U_e`, `M_e=J_e^TJ_e`, and the actual endpoint rows. Begin with weighted invariants or conditional statements retaining every scale. An invertible relabeling of constraints accompanied by the corresponding transformation of G changes only the orthogonal frame; physical coordinate scales and the actual integer lattice cannot be omitted in this way. Do not mix the F or good spaces of the old `n^(3/4)` framework with those of the new 192√n framework.

4. **If the objective remains arithmetic approximation to e+π, first propose a different approximation family.** A weighted family, different b, or a new combination of indices needs its own complete definition: actual integer coefficients, final common endpoint factor, raw and reduced rational numbers, the complete exponential/arctangent error including the factor 4, nonvanishing, and control over infinitely many indices. First check whether the new object is actually the old `p_n/q_n`. If it differs, document 1999 does not automatically exclude it; earlier spectral, transfer, saddle, or finite-seed results do not automatically establish its new interfaces either. The first sufficient target is the actual `q_n·|e+π−p_n/q_n|→0`, or a nonzero small quantity for the same primitive integer form; a small normalized error alone is insufficient.

This round does not recommend expanding the directory-wide review of the old raw family to seek a shrinking interface already ruled out by document 1999. Ten existing core manuscripts hold within their actual claims. General interface counterexamples and remaining targets serve as boundaries for continuation. Before publishing any genuinely new mathematical result, check its local predecessors and corresponding primary literature.
