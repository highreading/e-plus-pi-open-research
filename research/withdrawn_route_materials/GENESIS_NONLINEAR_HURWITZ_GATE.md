## Explicit global operation and proved scalar bridge

Let F(z)=exp(z)+4 arctan(z), F(0)=1. Consider the nonlinear operation

    N_F(t)=exp(F(exp(t)-1)-1).

Its local Taylor derivatives at t=0 are integers. Indeed the derivatives a_j=F^(j)(0) are integers: a_0=1, a_(2j)=1 for j>=1, and a_(2j+1)=1+4(-1)^j(2j)!. Composition with exp(t)-1 applies the integer Stirling transform. Exponentiating the resulting zero-constant Hurwitz series applies the integer complete Bell polynomials. This argument retains both actual source laws, rather than replacing F by a deformation.

Along the real path t=log 2 the inner argument is 1, with no branch ambiguity, so

    N_F(log 2)=exp(S-1).

For integers a and b>0, positivity and injectivity of the real exponential give the exact equivalence

    S=a/b  <=>  N_F(log 2)^b=e^(a-b).

Thus there is a precise scalar-to-object consequence before any purported contradiction. The hoped-for new tool would have to constrain multiplicative relations between a nonlinear Hurwitz value at log 2 and exp(1). Integer origin jets alone supply no such theorem.
