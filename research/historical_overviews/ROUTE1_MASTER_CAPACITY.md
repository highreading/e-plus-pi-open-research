## 2. What may be added and what may not

There are two different ledgers.

1. The **Builder ledger** contains proved lower bounds for additional
   divisibility or matching mass.  These may be added only after compulsory
   Cartier content and every previously booked factor have been removed.
2. The **Closer ledger** contains proved upper bounds on an unresolved branch.
   Closing a branch can be decisive even though it adds no divisor.

The ledgers are not automatically additive across rows of the tables below.
Several mechanisms use the same prime support or the same denominator-clearing
reservoir.  Until disjointness or an exact overlap formula is proved, the
archive uses the larger safe upper bound and books no sum.

## 7. Admission test for every new mechanism

A candidate receives substantial work only after all seven checks pass.

1. **Actual-family implication.**  It must be forced by the original collision
   or a proved Frobenius/Hensel lift of that collision.
2. **Correct digit.**  Its valuation level must match the gate being closed.
3. **De-overlap.**  Previously booked Cartier content and compulsory factors
   must be divided out before capacity is measured.
4. **Non-thin support.**  If all relevant primes divide an integer of
   polynomial size in $m$, the mechanism is immediately zero-rate.
5. **Raw capacity.**  Its maximum possible normalized mass must be computed
   before a long proof is attempted.
6. **Asymptotic route.**  Infinitely many examples or a larger finite scan are
   insufficient; there must be a path to positive Chebyshev mass or to an
   $o(m)$ closer theorem.
7. **Module novelty.**  A new coordinate must increase actual codimension; a
   Jordan extension of an old relation receives no further jet-by-jet study.
