# Stage 4 handoff and inclusive prime-support clarification

The new author theorem in SMALL_PRIME_NORMALIZATION_STAGE4.md is

 A_lead divides |det G| N_k,
 N_k=2^[k v_2((k-1)!)] (oddpart binom(2k-2,k-1))^k
                         lcm(1,3,...,4k-3)^k.

The notation for the lcm means all positive odd integers at most 4k-3. Accordingly, its prime support is p<=4k-3, INCLUSIVE. The phrase 'support ... below 4k-3' in report Section 7 should be read with this inclusive endpoint, as already specified correctly in report (1) and (4). No formula or proof changes.

For p=2 the additive loss is k v_2((k-1)!). For odd p<=4k-3 it is k[v_p(oddpart binom(2k-2,k-1))+floor(log_p(4k-3))]; for p>4k-3 it is zero. Their weighted sum is log N_k=O(k^2). The small-prime term (det G)_(p<=6k) survives with multiplicity one and its growth is NOT controlled.

The fixed physical clearer delta=b^k[b 2^(3k+2)ell_k ell_(6k)]^k and its final gcd are unchanged. N_k is an auxiliary denominator bound for the same monic polynomial, not a new physical normalization. The proof uses the square-root inverse of J, the complete endpoint terms (including the dyadic cancellation), and a bordered determinant with no G inverse.

The report is an author proof. No independent verification, computational check, prime scan, or irrationality conclusion is claimed in this stage.
