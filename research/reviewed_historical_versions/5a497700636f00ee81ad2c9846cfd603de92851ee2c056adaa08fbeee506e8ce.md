# Exact second-layer low-output jet

## 1. Actual normalization and statement

Let k=3^a, a>=3. Let Q0=sum q_r y^r be the primitive integer MONOMIAL polynomial in the actual full matching kernel. Keep the endpoint in rho_0=(mu-delta_{-1})/2, and K(P)=-f((y+1)P)+4 lambda(P), f(y^r)=(2r)!, lambda(P)=integral_0^1 P(x^2)dx. Thus rho_0(Q0 y^i)=0 for i<k and K(Q0 y^i)=0 for i<k-1. Write Q0=sum alpha_j ell_j, ell_j=4^j P_{2j}(sqrt(y)). For every low index j<k-1 the EXACT lower equation is

 alpha_j=(4j+1) f((y+1)Q0 ell_j)/(4*16^j).

Study the branch 3|q_1 and set x=q_1/3 (an integer). All congruences for rational numbers are in Z_(3). The second-layer map depends only on the three residues (q_0,x,q_2) modulo 3.

**Theorem.** For every low j, the row giving alpha_j/3 modulo 3 is zero unless every ternary digit of j is 0 or 1. In the latter case its row, acting on (q_0,x,q_2), is

 j modulo 9       row modulo 3
 0                (1,2,2)
 1                (1,1,1)
 3                (2,2,2)
 4                (2,1,1).

These are all possible residues for such j. Over the entire range 0<=j<k-1 this matrix has rank exactly 2 and kernel

 q_0=0,   x+q_2=0 modulo 3.

Consequently the following is an exact equivalence for the ACTUAL kernel on the stated branch:

 min_low v3(alpha_j)=1
   iff (q_0, q_1/3+q_2) != (0,0) modulo 3.

If both residues vanish then every low alpha_j is divisible by 9. This is an exact second-layer characterization, not a uniform assertion that its survival premise holds. In particular v3(q_1)=1 alone is not sufficient: q_2 can cancel q_1/3.

## 2. Complete reduction modulo 9 BEFORE division

For an integer polynomial P, the coefficients of its complete factorial functional are

 f((y+1)P)=sum_r ((2r)!+(2r+2)!) [y^r]P.

Modulo 9 the weights at r=0,1,2 are respectively 3,8,6, and every weight for r>=3 is zero: v3(6!)=2 and factorial valuations are nondecreasing. In particular the r=2 term must not be discarded. Thus

 f((y+1)P)=3P_0+8P_1+6P_2 modulo 9.

Let l_r=[y^r]ell_j, with absent coefficients zero. Substitution P=Q0 ell_j yields, on q_1=3x,

 f((y+1)Q0 ell_j)
 =q_0(3l_0+8l_1+6l_2)+3x(8l_0+6l_1)+6q_2 l_0 mod 9.

The exact Legendre coefficient identities are

 l_0=(-1)^j binom(2j,j),
 l_1=-j(2j+1)l_0,
 l_2=j(j-1)(2j+1)(2j+3)l_0/6.

The last identity is an identity of integers despite its displayed denominator; it is zero for j=0,1. The coefficient l_1 is always divisible by 3: if j=0 or 1 modulo 3 its explicit multiplier is divisible by 3; if j=2 modulo 3, binom(2j,j) is divisible by 3 by the units-digit carry. Therefore the numerator is always divisible by 3, for ALL j. Division by 3 after the full modulo-9 calculation gives

 alpha_j/3 = (j+1) {q_0(l_0+2(l_1/3)+2l_2)
                         +2x l_0+2q_2 l_0} mod 3.       (A)

Here 4*16^j is a 3-unit congruent to 1 modulo 3. Equation (A) retains every surviving factorial term and the Legendre correction l_1/3. Reducing ell_j only modulo 3 before this calculation would lose that correction.

When 3|(4j+1), equivalently j=2 modulo 3, (A) is zero. This includes both v3(4j+1)=1 and larger valuations: the numerator has already acquired a factor 3. No division by 4j+1 occurs. The possibly nonzero l_2 in this class has been retained in (A) before multiplication by the vanishing prefactor.

For j=0 or 1 modulo 3, put u=j(2j+1)/3, an integer. Then l_1/3=-u l_0. Also l_2=0 modulo 3: for j=0 the factors j and 2j+3 supply two factors of 3 before the single division by 3; for j=1 use j-1 and 2j+1 instead. The zero initial cases are immediate. Thus (A) simplifies to

 alpha_j/3=(j+1)l_0[(1+u)q_0+2x+2q_2] mod 3.          (B)

This explains why only l_0 modulo 3, not its lift modulo 9, is needed in the final formula: every occurrence requiring it modulo 9 already carries 3 after the branch reduction. No additional Legendre lift terms are omitted.

## 3. All-index classification and rank proof

The elementary base-3 binomial congruence gives l_0=1 modulo 3 when every ternary digit of j is 0 or 1, and l_0=0 otherwise. For clarity: without a carry when doubling j, each digit 1 contributes a factor 2 to binom(2j,j); the sign (-1)^j cancels their product modulo 3. If j contains a digit 2 there is a carry and the central binomial coefficient is divisible by 3.

For j=3h, u=h(6h+1)=h modulo 3. For j=3h+1, u=(3h+1)(2h+1)=2h+1 modulo 3. In the nonzero-l_0 cases h modulo 3 is 0 or 1. Inserting these four possibilities into (B) gives precisely the four displayed rows. All remaining j give zero rows, including j=2 modulo 3 and those with a higher ternary digit 2.

The rows at j=0 and j=1 are (1,2,2) and (1,1,1); their first two columns have determinant -1 modulo 3, so rank is at least 2. Every row is one of these, its double, or zero, so rank is at most 2. Both indices lie in every requested low range. Solving these two equations gives x+q_2=0 and q_0=0. Hence the full range, no matter how large a becomes, supplies no third independent second-layer jet condition. Coordinates q_r for r>=3 are entirely invisible at this layer.

Equivalently, let A0=q_0+2x+2q_2 and A1=q_0+x+q_2 modulo 3. The complete low-output vector consists only of A0,A1,2A1,2A0 and zeros at the positions specified above. Testing alpha_0 and alpha_1 alone suffices to decide second-layer survival, although this is a consequence of the all-index proof rather than an assumption about the other outputs.

## 4. What matching does and does not presently establish

The inherited upper moment argument implies R=(y-1)^3(y+1) divides Q0 modulo 3. Writing Q0=R A modulo 3, its first coefficients satisfy q_0=-A_0, q_1=-A_0-A_1 and q_2=-A_1-A_2. On the branch q_1=0 this imposes A_1=-A_0 but does not fix q_0 or q_2. The lift x=q_1/3 is not determined by these mod-3 equations.

The inherited highest-denominator lower congruences impose q_r=0 modulo 3 on (k+3)/2<=r<=(3k-1)/2. Those indices are above this jet. They do not exclude the second-layer nullspace either. For example the primitive integer polynomial P=y^3(y^3-1)(y+1), of degree 7, has q_0=q_1=q_2=0 and satisfies ALL upper moment congruences modulo 3 (R annihilates the functional), as well as this central coefficient condition for k>=27. Its second-layer output map is zero. This is only a witness that the specified inherited first-layer congruences and primitivity do not exclude the nullspace. P is NOT asserted to satisfy the characteristic-zero matching system, nor all its higher-depth saturated congruences.

The full exact matching matrix has a unique rational kernel under the inherited rank hypotheses. Whether its primitive generator avoids the displayed nullspace uniformly in a remains unresolved. The map computation does not supply that missing fact: lower matching equates these factorial outputs to the corresponding compact coefficients, but does not by itself assert one output survives at depth 1. No inference from real rank or positivity to this normalized modular condition is made.

Here is the precise new actual-kernel premise, expressed without replacing the original normalizations. Let C_m be the signed maximal minors of E_{i,m}=L K(y^i U_m), let d_E=gcd_m |C_m|, t_m=C_m/d_E, Q=sum t_m U_m, and g=content_monomial(Q), so Q0=Q/g. Put D=d_E*g and S_r=sum_m C_m [y^r]U_m. Then q_r=S_r/D. These divisions are exact in Z. On the branch 3|q_1, S_1/(3D) is integral. The nullspace condition is EXACTLY

 S_0/D=0 modulo 3,
 (S_1+3S_2)/(3D)=0 modulo 3.                         (C)

Thus the desired survival premise is that at least one congruence in (C) fails. Equivalently, with v3(0)=infinity, at least one equality holds:

 v3(S_0)=v3(D),
 v3(S_1+3S_2)=v3(D)+1.

Integrality ensures these are the only ways the residues can be nonzero. The original d_E and the distinct monomial content g remain in D. Neither can be replaced by an algorithmic nullspace content or by a guessed power of 3. If both residues vanish, the present theorem proves min_low v3(alpha)>=2 but gives no finite upper bound for that deeper branch.

## 5. Conversion, content, and physical scope

Use the inherited actual conversion bound d_conv=-min_all v3(alpha_j), with 0<=d_conv<=a. Let Delta=Delta_{2k}, theta_L the monomial-to-Legendre conversion index, and Lambda the low gcd of adj(B)coeff(Q) for the ORIGINAL reduced polynomial Q=gQ0. Then exactly

 v3(theta_L)=v3(Delta)-d_conv,
 v3(Lambda)=v3(g)+v3(Delta)+min_low v3(alpha),
 v3(zeta_W)=d_conv+min_low v3(alpha).

Therefore, conditional on the explicit survival premise above,

 v3(zeta_W)=d_conv+1<=a+1,
 v3(Lambda)=v3(g)+v3(Delta)+1.

If the premise fails, v3(zeta_W)>=d_conv+2. The bound d_conv<=a is an inherited author result, not reproved or computationally retested here. The present finite-jet proof does not assert the premise uniformly or infer it from k=27.

Keep M(t)=gcd_m |H_m H_{m+1}t_m| and M_end(t)=gcd(M(t),|sum_m(-1)^m t_m B_m|) as in the source; this stage does not evaluate them or identify them with g. In particular theta_L is NOT theta_W. The exact wide interface remains
