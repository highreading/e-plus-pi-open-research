# Two-channel denominator module: complete return-content criterion

Author proof, not independent review. This bounded stage extends Main Stages16–17 to exactly two nonzero invariant factors of the ACTUAL denominator module. No numerical experiment or claim of uniform occurrence is made.

## 1. Actual normalization and lattice-faithful presentation

Sources read in full: main/FINITE_MODULE_FORCED_CONTENT_STAGE16.md and main/CYCLIC_DENOMINATOR_RETURN_CONTENT_STAGE17.md. Use their canonical m=k>=2, h=y+1, p>6k, O=Z_p, L=O^k, and complete blocks

 M_ij=mu(h^(i+j)), G_ij=mu(h^(k+i+j)), J_ij=nu_k(h^(i+j)),
 C=M-J, B_ij=R_k(h^(i+j)), D_ij=R_k(h^(k+i+j)).

All endpoint and period jets in R_k are retained, especially the low block B. Put

 H=J^{-1}(DG^{-1}C-B), beta/beta_k=det(TI-H),
 beta_k=(-1)^k det J det G.

Keep the exact source clearer delta=b_k^k E_clear^k, b_k=oddpart binom(2k-2,k-1), E_clear=b_k 2^(3k+2) lcm(1,...,k) lcm(1,...,6k). Set I_j=delta beta_j, g=gcd_j I_j, A=|I_k|/g. At p>6k, delta and det J are units. Write a=v_p(det G), n=v_p(A), d=v_p(g)=a-n.

Assume N_H=(L+HL)/L is O/p^t1 plus O/p^t2, with 1<=t1<=t2. Write ell=t1+t2. Integral left/right Smith operations on the rational matrix H give P,H,Q with P,Q in GL_k(O) and PHQ diagonal, exactly two entries of negative valuation -t1,-t2. Absorb their units in the corresponding column vectors. Separating these two entries and transforming back constructs

 H=H0+U diag(p^-t1,p^-t2)V^t,                       (1)

where H0 is integral, U=[u1,u2] consists of two columns of an integral invertible matrix (up to units), and V^t consists of two rows of an integral invertible matrix. In particular U is a split injection O^2 -> L and V^t is a split surjection L -> O^2. This is independent left/right Smith reduction, NOT similarity of H and its diagonal form. Conversely these splitting properties imply that (1) has exactly the stated denominator module: its image modulo L is the image under U of diag(p^-t1,p^-t2)O^2 modulo O^2. Adding H0 does not change it.

Thus the chosen presentation is lattice-faithful, unlike an arbitrary sum of two fractional rank-one matrices. No assumption on the number of singular Smith directions of G is substituted for the hypothesis on N_H. In this presentation H0 is exactly J^{-1}(DG^{-1}C-B)-sum p^-ta ua va^t, so it retains the complete actual moment data. It can be calculated by rational arithmetic and integral Smith transformations; no nonunit inverse is declared integral without this construction.

The inherited content identity is

 rho_p=ell-n, 0<=rho_p<=ell, d=a-ell+rho_p.         (2)

## 2. Finite returns and the genuinely mixed term

Use a reversed variable z, so every coefficient degree is retained without an infinity convention. Define

 f(z)=det(I-zH0)=sum_{h=0}^k c_h z^h, c_0=1,
 r_ab,j=v_a^t H0^j u_b in O, a,b in {1,2},
 R_ab(z)=sum_{j>=0} r_ab,j z^j.

These are returns in the ACTUAL H0, not in DG^{-1}C with B suppressed. Set

 L_a(z)=z f(z) R_aa(z),
 M(z)=z^2 f(z)(R_11(z)R_22(z)-R_12(z)R_21(z)).       (3)

Although defined as series, L_a and M are polynomials of degree at most k. Indeed R=V^t(I-zH0)^{-1}U and the determinant identity over O[[z]] gives

 det(I-zH)=f(z)-p^-t1 L_1(z)-p^-t2 L_2(z)+p^-ell M(z). (4)

Polynomiality of M is not inferred by discarding tails: introduce independent formal variables x1,x2 into det(I-zH0-z x1 u1v1^t-z x2 u2v2^t). By multilinearity it has degree at most one in each x_a. Its x1 x2 coefficient is M(z), and its total z-degree is at most k. This also supplies a finite minor description: M is the sum over distinct column positions i<j of the determinants obtained by replacing column i with -z u1 v1_i and column j with -z u2 v2_j, plus the term with channels exchanged, leaving all other columns equal to those of I-zH0. Terms involving the same column twice are absent. This accounts for signs and all mixed two-channel contributions.

For an alternative minor identity let S=I-zH0 and K_ab=v_a^t adj(S)u_b. Then f M=z^2(K_11 K_22-K_12 K_21). Division by f here is exact in O[z]; f(0)=1 also gives an integral formal-series inverse. M is not det(K) alone and its content need not equal the minimum valuation of the individual products.

Explicit finite generators are as follows. For 1<=l<=k put

 l_a,l=sum_{h=0}^{l-1} c_h r_aa,l-1-h.

For 0<=j<=k-2 put

 w_j=sum_{i=0}^j (r_11,i r_22,j-i-r_12,i r_21,j-i).

For 2<=l<=k put m_l=sum_{h=0}^{l-2} c_h w_{l-2-h}; set m_0=m_1=0 and l_a,0=0. Then L_a=sum l_a,l z^l and M=sum m_l z^l. Only r_ab,j with j<k are needed (mixed terms only require j<=k-2). Cayley–Hamilton for integral H0 controls all later returns without saturating any Krylov module.

The mixed coefficient ideal is exactly (w_0,...,w_{k-2})=(m_2,...,m_k), by a unit triangular transformation with coefficients c_h. This is a finite ideal, but its generators are SUMS of two-by-two cross-time return determinants, not the individual determinants. Cancellation within each w_j is intrinsic to the polynomial. In particular vanishing diagonal returns alone does not kill the mixed term: the cross returns r_12 and r_21 still occur with a minus sign.

## 3. Necessary and sufficient criterion retaining coefficient cancellations

Define the integral polynomial

 P_loc(z)=p^ell f(z)-p^t2 L_1(z)-p^t1 L_2(z)+M(z).

Its coefficient at z^l, for 0<=l<=k, is

 b_l=p^ell c_l-p^t2 l_1,l-p^t1 l_2,l+m_l.           (5)

The constant coefficient is p^ell. Since det(TI-H)=T^k det(I-T^-1 H), reversing coefficients preserves content. The least scalar p-power clearer of this monic characteristic polynomial is p^n by primitive normalization of I_j. Hence

 rho_p=min_{0<=l<=k} v_p(b_l).                     (6)

This minimum is finite and at most ell, because b_0=p^ell. Formula (6) includes complete cancellation, even when several terms of (5) have the same valuation. It is not the minimum of their separate valuations.

For every integer 0<=s<=ell the exact checkable criterion is

 rho_p>=s iff m_l-p^t2 l_1,l-p^t1 l_2,l=0 mod p^s
                    for every 1<=l<=k.             (7)

There are no omitted constant, leading, or intermediate degrees: b_0 is automatically divisible by p^s; all other k coefficients are listed. For s>ell the assertion is impossible; s=0 is automatic. Both mixed-internal cancellation and cancellation between the two linear channels and M are retained in (7).

One may reduce the congruences further without losing cancellation. Multiplication by f(z)^-1 is unit triangular on coefficients through degree k. Since f^-1 L_a=z R_aa and f^-1 M=z^2 det R, (7) is equivalent to the k explicit RETURN congruences

 -p^t2 r_11,0-p^t1 r_22,0=0 mod p^s,
 w_{l-2}-p^t2 r_11,l-1-p^t1 r_22,l-1=0 mod p^s,
                                      2<=l<=k.     (8)

Thus rho_p is the minimum of ell and the valuations of these k combined generators. Only finite returns up to k-1 occur. Passing to this triangularly equivalent ideal is valid even though f^-1 is an infinite series: coefficients through degree k depend only on input coefficients through degree k, and the polynomial P_loc has no higher coefficients to test. This is a rank-two return ideal, not two independently minimized rank-one return ideals.

## 4. An explicit sufficient annihilation condition in actual data

Choose nonnegative integers e1,e2 satisfying

 t2+e1>=s, t1+e2>=s, e1+e2>=s.                    (9)

A sufficient condition for rho_p>=s is the finite actual set

 v_a^t H0^j u_b=0 mod p^ea,
 a,b in {1,2}, 0<=j<k.                             (10)

Indeed diagonal terms in (8) then have valuations at least t2+e1 or t1+e2, and every mixed product in w_j has valuation at least e1+e2. This proves all combined congruences, without assuming that their valuations minimize independently. Equivalently v_a annihilates O[H0]U modulo p^ea. The unsaturated integral Krylov module is essential; replacing it by its saturation strengthens the premise and is not justified.

This condition is in the complete two-channel moment data: construct U,V,H0 from (1) using H=J^{-1}(DG^{-1}C-B), and test (10) with exactly that H0. It neither asserts (10) uniformly nor substitutes a first-return pairing for all k returns. For full cancellation s=ell, the simple choice e1=t1,e2=t2 yields rho_p=ell, n=0 and d=a. For partial s, many weaker choices satisfying (9) are possible. Conditions (9)-(10) are sufficient only; (8) is necessary and sufficient and can hold through cancellations even when (10) fails.

## 5. Boundary of the result

The presentation, mixed finite generators, criterion (8), and sufficient condition (10) apply to the actual canonical branch under its stated denominator-module hypothesis. No identity of the actual moment functionals has been proved that forces these return congruences on an infinite family. No occurrence/distribution of the rank-two branch is proved. At p<=6k no conclusion is claimed, and no changed clearer is used.

The exact primitive leading coefficient remains A=|I_k|/g; d=a-ell+rho_p, with rho_p computed by (6) or (8). Structural content a-ell is not counted again in rho_p. All endpoint terms and the entire B remain in H0; independent Smith transformations are not basis changes preserving the identity pencil by similarity. The older low-Legendre theta and zeta quantities concern another matrix and are not identified with N_H.

This completes the assigned rank-two reduction. The unresolved arithmetic is the congruence of the combined generators in (8), not just separate valuations of diagonal returns or minors. No 3-adic escalation, kernel rerun, degree scan, prime atlas, or independent review was performed.
