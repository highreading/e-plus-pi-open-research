 lambda(P)=mu(h^k P),

The last functional means coefficientwise rational integration: I(y^j)=1/(2j+1). No transcendental constant or transcendental-weight integral is reduced modulo p.

## Limited Stage 15 review

All-exterior-power bound: VALID. Write the exact characteristic matrix as H=XG^(-1)Y+Z with X=J^(-1)D, Y=C, Z=-J^(-1)B. These three matrices are p-integral; B is the complete low rational block. Multilinearity followed by Laplace expansion reduces a j-minor to integral combinations of l-minors of XG^(-1)Y for l<=j. Two applications of Cauchy–Binet and Smith form give valuations at least -Sigma_l>=-Sigma_j. Thus p^Sigma_j wedge^j H is integral. No pairing hypothesis is required.

Weak majorization: VALID without pairing assumptions. Triangularization over an algebraic closure identifies eigenvalues of exterior powers with products of selected eigenvalues, including algebraic multiplicity. Select only negative-valuation roots; if fewer than j exist, apply the corresponding smaller exterior power and use monotonicity of Sigma. This proves the partial-sum bound. The total denominator weight equals v_p(A) by the primitive reversed-polynomial Newton polygon. Zero roots and a reduced degree of the reversed polynomial cause no exception.

Primitive factors and complements: VALID. Gauss's lemma gives primitive integral factors and A=A_F^e A_R in absolute leading coefficients. Applying the primitive denominator-weight identity separately to F and R gives contributions e v_p(A_F) and n-e v_p(A_F). Both are weights of subsets of the actual root multiset, so their upper bounds use Sigma_(ef) and Sigma_(k-ef), respectively. No irreducibility assumption is needed. When ef=k the primitive constant complement is a unit and n=e v_p(A_F), exactly as asserted. Rational-root ceiling and floor bounds are the degree-one instance.

Global divisibility: VALID in the stated prime range. The determinant-divisor quotient T_j(G)=det G/Delta_(k-j)^G is the product of the j largest positive integer Smith invariants, and has local valuation Sigma_j. Consequently the local inequalities give precisely the displayed divisibilities of q_large^e and (A_F)_large^e, and the two-sided prime-product interval. At large primes not dividing det G the primitive leading coefficient and its primitive factor leading coefficients are units. The products therefore include all relevant large primes and say nothing about small primes.

The canonical clearer delta is a p-unit, and A=|I_k|/g with I_j=delta beta_j and the full coefficient gcd g. These hypotheses are essential to n=v_p(det G)-v_p(g). No correction to the assigned Stage 15 implications is required. This review supplies no bound on the actual Smith tails or final gcd.

## Original simultaneous weighted identities

To avoid confusion with the physical low block B, denote the differential coefficients by a(y), b(y), d(y):

 a=4yh^2,
 b=8(k+2)yh+2h^2,
 d=4(k+2)(k+1)y+2(k+2)h-h^2,
 T P=aP''+bP'+dP.

The previous weighted identity, retained here as one component of the joint system, is

 lambda(TP)=2^(k+2)[2P'(1)+(k+1)P(1)].                 (1)

The new rational component is

 omega(TP)=P(0)+8c[P'(1)+(k+1)P(1)]+c I(VP),          (2)
 V=-h^2-2kh+4k(k+1)y.

Proof of (2). Put U=h^(k+2)P and L=4y d^2/dy^2+2 d/dy-1. Then LU=h^k TP, so its divisibility by h^k justifies the high-degree rational formula. The factorial identity F(LU)=-U(0) gives -F(LU)=P(0). For u(x)=U(x^2) and w(x)=(1+x^2)^(-k), integration by parts twice gives

 integral_0^1 (u''-u)w dx=[u'w-uw']_0^1+integral_0^1 u(w''-w)dx.

At zero u'=w'=0. At one the bracket is 2^(-k)[2U'(1)+kU(1)]=8[P'(1)+(k+1)P(1)]. Multiplication of w''-w by U(x^2) gives V(x^2)P(x^2). Every remaining integral is therefore the rational polynomial functional I. This proves (2), including the factorial endpoint at zero and both terms at one.

Subtracting c2^(-k) times (1) eliminates the derivative endpoint and yields the new compatibility identity

 (omega-c2^(-k)lambda)(TP)
   =P(0)+4c(k+1)P(1)+c I(VP).                        (3)

This is an actual functional identity, not a generic matrix rank criterion. Its last term does not vanish merely because P has the form zq with z in the joint kernel.

## Complete finite test system, including additional moments

For z in the joint kernel define

 eta_j=lambda(y^(k+j)z),
 theta_j=omega(y^(k+j)z), j=0,1,

and, for either functional L0=lambda or omega, put

 W_L0(z,q)=L0(a z''q+(2a q'+bq)z').

The product rule is T(zq)=a z''q+(2a q'+bq)z'+zTq. Set

 E(z,q)=2^(k+2)[2z'(1)q(1)+2z(1)q'(1)+(k+1)z(1)q(1)],
 R(z,q)=z(0)q(0)+8c[z'(1)q(1)+z(1)q'(1)+(k+1)z(1)q(1)]+c I(Vzq).

For all tests q of degree below k the exact simultaneous equations are

 W_lambda(z,q)+lambda(zTq)=E(z,q),
 W_omega(z,q)+omega(zTq)=R(z,q).                       (4)

For deg q<=k-3 the second term on each left side is zero by the respective null system, because deg Tq<=deg q+2. For k=2 that smaller test range is empty; equations (4) with the corrections below still apply.

Write

 d_n=4(n+k+2)^2-2(n+k+2)-2.

The top terms of T(y^n) are -y^(n+2)+d_n y^(n+1). Consequently the two top test corrections are exactly

 lambda(zT(y^(k-2)))=-eta_0,
 lambda(zT(y^(k-1)))=-eta_1+d_(k-1)eta_0,
 omega(zT(y^(k-2)))=-theta_0,
 omega(zT(y^(k-1)))=-theta_1+d_(k-1)theta_0.             (5)

Thus neither of the two additional mu moments from the previous report has been discarded. The rational system has its own corresponding two moments. They satisfy, by the valid high-degree formula,

 theta_j=-F(h^k y^(k+j)z)+c I(y^(k+j)z).

There is no identity here expressing these factorial moments solely in terms of eta_0 and eta_1.

Subtracting the two equations of (4) in the proportion used in (3) gives, for every deg q<k,

 W_omega(z,q)-c2^(-k)W_lambda(z,q)
   +omega(zTq)-c2^(-k)lambda(zTq)
 =z(0)q(0)+4c(k+1)z(1)q(1)+c I(Vzq).                 (6)

For the lower tests its correction on the left is zero. For the top two tests it is respectively

 -(theta_0-c2^(-k)eta_0),
 -(theta_1-c2^(-k)eta_1)+d_(k-1)(theta_0-c2^(-k)eta_0).

Equations (4)–(6), together with the original two null systems, are a complete exact joint boundary system. They explicitly show the new compatibility contributed by the rational functional.

## Degree, prime and endpoint checks

For deg z,deg q<k, deg(zq)<=2k-2 and deg T(zq)<=2k. Thus the largest mu or factorial polynomial argument has degree at most 3k. These functionals are integer-valued on integral polynomials; their evaluations are reduced directly, with no division by moments or factorials.

Every I argument in (2)–(6) has degree at most 2k, introducing only odd denominators at most 4k+1. Since k>=2, 4k+1<6k<p. The normalization c and all powers of two are p-units. The two additional rational moments have the same denominator bound. Therefore every displayed reduction is legitimate for p>6k. No inverse of d_n is taken; it may vanish modulo p. No inverse of a(y)=4y(y+1)^2 is taken either. The zeros at y=0,-1 prevent treating it as an everywhere invertible coefficient.

Endpoint values in the formulas mean y=0 and y=1, corresponding to h=1 and h=2. The jet endpoint h=0 has already been handled by divisibility, before the high-degree rational formula is applied. That formula has not been applied to the physical low block B.

## Remaining injectivity premise and stopping point

Adding the rational null conditions does supply the new endpoint identity (6), notably the zero-endpoint value z(0) and a rational compact pairing I(Vzq). It does not make this compact pairing vanish. Even if z(0)=z(1)=z'(1)=0, the right side retains c I(Vzq), while the left side contains derivative moment pairings. Consequently neither a pointwise differential equation nor uniqueness from endpoint data follows.

A precise fixed-dimensional next premise is injectivity of

 N_p -> F_p^3, z -> (z(0),z(1),z'(1)).

If proved for the actual matrices, this would give dim N_p<=3, improving the four-coordinate conditional target previously formulated for the single-functional system. No such injectivity is proved here. In particular the homogeneous null equations and these three zero endpoint values must still be shown to force the additional moments and derivative pairings to produce only z=0. Equations (5)–(6) specify exactly the unsuppressed corrections. The previous single-functional bound of four was itself conditional, not an established bound being improved unconditionally.

An exclusion N_p=0 would require a stronger argument still. This stage establishes neither exclusion nor a uniform numerical bound on dim N_p; it provides the complete joint system and its missing injectivity condition, satisfying the stated stopping condition.

The tall 2k-by-k stack [G;D] in this question is distinct from the historical wide right block. No new arithmetic restriction on its maximal-minor gcd is obtained, so its rank/gcd equivalence is not presented as a result. Joint injectivity would not imply nonsingularity of Z^T C J^(-1)D Z on a filtered subspace: the latter also involves the low-coordinate transformation and restriction of a bilinear pairing.
