## 2. Actual data, common clearer, and all four borders

For r>=0 use exactly

 C_r=D_(2r)-(-1)^r,
 R_r=-(2r)!+4 sum_(a=1)^r (-1)^(r-a)/(2a-1),
 V_r=(-1)^r.

For k>=2 put

 L_k=lcm(1,3,...,6k-5),
 P_k(t)=I_(k,0)+t I_(k,1)=L_k^k det[C_(i+j);R_(i+j)+tV_(i+j)],
 G_k=gcd(I_(k,0),I_(k,1)),

with i=0,...,k-1 in each row block and j=0,...,2k-1. When I_(k,1)!=0 the actual denominator is q_k=|I_(k,1)|/G_k. No other integer pair is substituted for this final primitive normalization.

For this adjacent step set D=L_(k+1) and rho=D/L_k. Every matrix in this section uses D on EACH lower row. Let the core row list be

 Rcal_k=((C,0),...,(C,k-1),(R,0),...,(R,k-1)).

A row (C,i) at column j means C_(i+j); a row (R,i) means D(R_(i+j)+tV_(i+j)). Columns are monomials, not a transformed basis. The core columns are 0,...,2k-1. Its determinant is rho^k P_k(t).

For a=C or R and b=0 or 1 define B_ab(t) to be the determinant with row list

 (Rcal_k,(a,k))

and column list

 (0,1,...,2k-1,2k+b).

These are the four auxiliary (2k+1)-minors, with their row/column orientations fixed. They are actual unshifted bordering minors, not unrelated generic matrices. The largest moment index is 3k+1, so D clears every rational endpoint term. All entries lie in Z[t]. All four B_ab are affine in t, because their response matrices have rank at most one.

The full (2k+2)-matrix with both new rows appended in order C_k,R_k and both new columns appended has determinant (-1)^k P_(k+1)(t): moving C_k across the k old R rows produces precisely that sign.

## 3. Fraction-free adjacent identity and complete coefficient equations

The exact identity is

 B_C0(t) B_R1(t)-B_C1(t) B_R0(t)
   =(-1)^k rho^k P_k(t) P_(k+1)(t).                 (1)

Proof. Apply Desnanot--Jacobi to the full (2k+2)-matrix, deleting the last two rows and columns for its common core. The four singly bordered determinants are exactly those defined above. The identity is polynomial in the entries, so neither a nonzero core determinant nor a division is required.

Write B_ab=x_ab+t y_ab. Define the THREE integers

 N0=x_C0 x_R1-x_C1 x_R0,
 N1=x_C0 y_R1+y_C0 x_R1-x_C1 y_R0-y_C1 x_R0,
 N2=y_C0 y_R1-y_C1 y_R0.

Thus all eight coefficients of the four auxiliary affine minors are retained. Equation (1) is exactly the following three simultaneous equations:

 N0=(-1)^k rho^k I_(k,0) I_(k+1,0),
 N1=(-1)^k rho^k [I_(k,0) I_(k+1,1)+I_(k,1) I_(k+1,0)],
 N2=(-1)^k rho^k I_(k,1) I_(k+1,1).                (2)

Dropping N1 is NOT legitimate for content. Cancellation in N1 is included, and its precise value remains important even when the extreme coefficients have large common divisors. If one solves (2) by dividing by a coefficient of P_k, that chosen coefficient is an additional pivot and may be zero or p-divisible. The fraction-free statement (1)-(2) has no such hidden pivot.

## 4. New adjacent content law and its exact missing premise

Set Gamma_k=gcd(N0,N1,N2), using nonnegative gcd and content(0)=0. Gauss's content lemma applied to (1) gives

 Gamma_k=rho^k G_k G_(k+1).                        (3)

This remains true if a polynomial is identically zero. For valuations and quotients below assume both P_k and P_(k+1) are nonzero. In particular the inherited nonzero-response claim would suffice for k>=24; its analytic proof is not reviewed here.

For every prime p, including 2,

 v_p(G_k)+v_p(G_(k+1))
  =min(v_p(N0),v_p(N1),v_p(N2))-k v_p(rho).        (4)

This is an all-depth restriction coupling adjacent physical contents, not a recurrence of scalar moments. For example, if the minimum on the right before subtraction is less than k v_p(rho)+2a, G_k and G_(k+1) cannot both be divisible by p^a. If that minimum equals k v_p(rho), both contents are p-units. No such minimum is asserted for the actual family without further arithmetic work.

In divisibility form,

 G_k divides Gamma_k/rho^k,
 G_(k+1)=Gamma_k/(rho^k G_k).                      (5)

Since rho is odd, the corresponding odd-part product is exactly

 odd(G_k) odd(G_(k+1))=odd(Gamma_k)/rho^k.

Here rho divides (6k-3)(6k-1)(6k+1), as these are the three new odd integers added to the lcm. Therefore log(rho^k)=O(k log(k+1)). The normalizing loss is explicit and smaller than the growing determinant's k^2 log k scale, but no estimate on Gamma_k has been proved.

Proof of the content step, to avoid a hidden coprimality premise: write each nonzero P as its positive content times a primitive polynomial. The product of two primitive integer polynomials is primitive, because modulo any prime their nonzero reductions have nonzero product. Thus the content of the product is the product of the contents. This includes the full middle coefficient in (2).

The exact open premise is now an upper/lower law for the gcd of the three complete bilinear border combinations N0,N1,N2, not merely unit entries of an individual border matrix. Formula (3) is a useful adjacent-size interface but not itself an asymptotic gain. It would be misleading to claim that it bounds G_k without controlling this boundary.

## 5. Applying the inherited recurrence to an actual column

For reference, the inherited operator is K4=sum_(ell=0)^4 q_ell(r)E^ell. The coefficients are integer polynomials. To specify them without an unexplained placeholder, let s=2r+1 and set

 a(s)=s(s+1), g(s)=a(s)+1,
 P(s)=s^3+4s^2+5s+1,
 J0(s)=s^4+8s^3+23s^2+30s+13,
 T(s)=2s^6+32s^5+215s^4+768s^3+1527s^2+1588s+665,

 h0(s)=-a(s)g(s+2), h1(s)=1-a(s)a(s+2), h2(s)=g(s),
 m0(s)=T(s+2)(s+2),
 m1(s)=-(s+4)[P(s+4)J0(s)(s+6)-P(s)J0(s+4)(s+2)]/2,
 m2(s)=T(s)(s+6).

Then

 q_ell(r)=sum_(u=0)^2 m_u(s) h_(ell-u)(s+2u),

omitting indices outside 0,1,2. In particular the complete forward/backward singular factors are

 q4(r)=T(s)(s+6)g(s+4)>0,
 q0(r)=-T(s+2)(s+2)s(s+1)g(s+2)<0.                (6)

The denominator 2 in m1 cancels in its integer polynomial numerator, as proved in the source. Both endpoint sequences C and R+tV obey this operator.

Let Rcal be either of the singly bordered row lists in Section 2, and let d=k+1 be its number of distinct shift levels 0,...,k. Write w_j(t) for its column j, and define the integer diagonal matrix H to have the row's shift i on that diagonal position. Let E_i be the diagonal 0/1 projector onto the rows with shift i. Thus H=sum iE_i and sum E_i=Id.

The actual vector identity is

 sum_(ell=0)^4 q_ell(j+H)w_(j+ell)(t)=0.           (7)

Its coefficients are diagonal MATRICES, not scalars. Replacing q_ell(j+H) by q_ell(j) is precisely the invalid step in a naive four-column recurrence.

Let U(t) be any fixed ordered list of 2k other columns with this row list. Define actual projected-column bordered minors

 D_i(U;j,t)=det[U(t),E_i w_j(t)],
 D(U;j,t)=det[U(t),w_j(t)]=sum_(i=0)^k D_i(U;j,t).

Project (7) and use determinant linearity in its last column to obtain the fraction-free recurrence

 q4(j+i) D_i(U;j+4,t)
   =-sum_(ell=0)^3 q_ell(j+i)D_i(U;j+ell,t).       (8)

For fixed U this is a closed collection of d scalar order-four DETERMINANT recurrences. It has 4d consecutive states, not four states independent of k.

For a single formula involving the unprojected target put

 Sigma_(j,d)=product_(i=0)^(d-1) q4(j+i).

Then

 Sigma_(j,d) D(U;j+4,t)
  =-sum_(i=0)^(d-1) sum_(ell=0)^3
       [Sigma_(j,d)/q4(j+i)] q_ell(j+i) D_i(U;j+ell,t).       (9)

Every coefficient in (9) is an integer. The 4d auxiliary minors are ALL shown. The forward nonunit factors are exactly the explicit product in (6); they cannot be divided away at their prime divisors. Backward propagation has the identical formula with q0 in place of q4 and ell=1,...,4, using product_i q0(j+i). These are safe unsimplified singular factors; common factors in the inherited operator may allow cancellation, but no minimal singular set is claimed.

For the four borders in (1), use U=columns 0,...,2k-1, and j=2k-4 or 2k-3 to reach appended column 2k or 2k+1. These j are nonnegative for k>=2, and all moments required remain at most 3k+1, so the original adjacent clearer D suffices. There is no unmentioned larger moment or denominator.

## 6. The smallest growing linear weight boundary

Here is the exact closure obstruction proved, with its quantifiers.

Fix one of the bordered row lists, any j>=0, and consider linear column-weight spaces invariant under multiplication by q4(j+H). The smallest such space containing a vector w with nonzero projection E_i w at every level has dimension d=k+1. It is

 span_Q{E_0 w,...,E_(d-1)w}=Q[H]w.               (10)

Proof. The q4(j+i) are pairwise distinct: q4 is the product of positive, strictly increasing polynomials in s>=1 displayed in (6). If sum_(m=0)^(d-1) c_m q4(j+H)^m w=0, projection to level i gives sum_m c_m q4(j+i)^m=0. A polynomial of degree <d cannot vanish at all d distinct numbers unless all c_m vanish. The d orbit vectors are independent and lie in the d-dimensional space on the right of (10). Lagrange interpolation yields every E_i w, proving minimality.

This hypothesis is realized by actual physical columns at t=0 and column index at least 1. At levels i<k there is a C row with C_(i+j)>0. For the C border the extra C_k is positive too. For the R border its only row at level k is D R_(k+j), which is nonzero: for r>=2 the arctangent remainder has absolute value <=4r<(2r)!, so R_r<0. Thus the rank lower bound is not based on arbitrary substituted moment values.

The core (without the extra row) similarly needs k weight coordinates. Adding either physical boundary row introduces exactly one new coordinate: level k. This is the smallest missing growing boundary for direct linear column-weight closure. Equivalently the power basis 1,H,...,H^(d-1) suffices, and no smaller invariant column space suffices under the stated hypothesis.

This is NOT a lower bound on the number of scalars in every conceivable nonlinear recurrence for the evaluated determinants. Fixing a particular cofactor functional may impose extra relations, and updating the core U with k might admit additional special identities. None are proved or disproved here. What is ruled out is the claimed automatic fixed four-state closure obtained merely by applying the scalar recurrence to whole columns and using determinant multilinearity.

### Integer index in the weight coordinates

Writing m_m=det[U,H^m w] and e_i=det[U,E_i w], one has m_m=sum_i i^m e_i. The determinant of this coordinate matrix is

 Delta_weight(d)=product_(0<=i<h<d)(h-i)=product_(h=1)^(d-1) h!.

It is not unimodular. Recovering e_i by interpolation uses

 E_i=product_(h!=i)(H-hId) / [(-1)^(d-1-i)i!(d-1-i)!].

If instead powers of q4(j+H) are used, their exact Vandermonde determinant is product_(i<h)[q4(j+h)-q4(j+i)]. These index factors must be retained if one changes coordinates. Equations (8)-(9) use the actual integer projectors directly, and hence introduce neither Vandermonde denominator. A nonunit coordinate index is not favorable physical common content.

No Legendre basis and no old recurrence-tail basis has been applied to the physical columns in (1). Their old Delta or Xi factors are therefore absent because these operations were not performed, not because the factors were suppressed.

## 7. The projected auxiliary minors are not an affine family

A second caution is essential for tracking both physical outputs. A projected column has response direction E_i u rather than the common response direction u of an unprojected column. Thus D_i(U;j,t) can have degree two in t even when U consists solely of physical columns. Its quadratic coefficients cancel on summing i to recover D(U;j,t); they may not be discarded individually in (8)-(9).

If further core columns are projected while trying to close the recurrence, the required degrees genuinely grow for the ACTUAL moments. This is not just an abstract rank bound. In a bordered row list, take the distinct columns 0,...,2k and project columns 2i,2i+1 to level i for each i<k, and column 2k to level k. Permuting rows produces k two-by-two diagonal blocks and one singleton. The i-th block is

 [[C_(3i), C_(3i+1)],
  [D(R_(3i)+tV_(3i)),D(R_(3i+1)+tV_(3i+1))]].

Its t coefficient is D(-1)^(3i+1)[C_(3i)+C_(3i+1)], which is nonzero, including i=0 since C0=0,C1=2. The singleton is C_(3k) for the C border or D(R_(3k)+tV_(3k)) for the R border. Consequently these actual fully projected determinants have degrees exactly k and k+1, respectively.

This construction stays inside moment range 3k and the same clearer D. It shows why a recurrence state consisting solely of affine physical-output pairs is not stable under unrestricted projector decoration. It does not assert that every possible elimination algorithm must store all these polynomials: special identities or compressed encodings might avoid that, but require a new proof.
