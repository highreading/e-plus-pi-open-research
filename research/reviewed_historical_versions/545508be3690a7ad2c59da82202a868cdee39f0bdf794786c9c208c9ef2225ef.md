## Result

For every s in this family,

 rank_F3(H_b mod3)=3,
 dim_F3 ker(H_b mod3)=s-6.

An explicit unit minor of H_b uses its first three rows (original upper indices 4,5,6) and exterior monomial columns 4,5,6; its determinant is 2 modulo 3. Thus the divided boundary is very far from saturated: its characteristic-zero rank is s-4, but only three pivots are units. This establishes excess modular kernel and is the prescribed stopping point. It does not imply characteristic-zero survival or vanishing of the two output residues.

## 1. Divided row functional and cancellation of all lower reconstruction corrections

Let S(X)=X^4-2X^3-4X-7 and define the integral sequence

 d_n=(c_{n+4}-2c_{n+3}-4c_{n+1}-7c_n)/9.

Integrality follows from the established scalar recurrence modulo 9. Its reduction modulo 3 has period 9:

 d_n mod3 = (2,1,0,0,0,2,1,2,1).                    (1)

For an all-index verification, generate c modulo 27 using e_0=0,
 e_{n+1}=(2n+2)(2n+1)e_n+2n(n+1),
 c_n=e_n+(1-(-1)^n)/2.
The state resets e_27=0 modulo 27 because both terms of its last transition have a factor 27. Coefficients then repeat with period 27, so c has period 54. Substitution over this finite scalar period gives (1) repeated six times. This is extraction of the single divided functional d mod3, NOT a modulo-27 matching-kernel computation or a new-degree test.

Apply S in the UPPER ROW index to the exact A. Since N is independent of that row index,

 (S A)_{r,h}/9 = d_{r+h}+sum_{j in J} d_{r+j} N_{j,h}.

The actual lower pivot structure gives N=0 modulo 3. Therefore

 (S A)_{r,h}/9=d_{r+h} modulo 3.                    (2)

This is why no unknown precision correction from T_J^{-1}, compact denominators, or eliminated central coefficients is lost. They cancel AFTER applying the exact row functional and dividing by 9, rather than being guessed from a modulo-9 inverse. The factorial term and endpoint are still present in the exact A.

## 2. Explicit entries, in the original boundary normalization

Put cbar=(0,1,1,1,0,2) periodically modulo 3 and

 M_{i,l}=cbar_{i+l}, 0<=i,l<4,
 v(h)=(cbar_h,cbar_{h+1},cbar_{h+2},cbar_{h+3})^t.

M is invertible modulo 3. Define, for r>=0 and exterior h in I1,

 F_{r,h}=dbar_{r+h}-(dbar_r,dbar_{r+1},dbar_{r+2},dbar_{r+3}) M^{-1}v(h).  (3)

This is an explicit finite-period formula for a unit row transform of H_b, not a new normalization of its columns. Indeed extend the Schur residual rows by R_0=...=R_3=0. For r>=0,

 (R_{r+4}-2R_{r+3}-4R_{r+1}-7R_r)/9
   =((SA)_{r,I1}-(SA)_{r,I0} A00^{-1}A01)/9.

Using (2) and A00=M, A01=(v(h)) modulo 3 proves F=L H_b modulo 3, where L is the lower triangular unit matrix implementing this recurrence. With boundary row indices r=0,...,s-5, the entries of H_b itself are consequently specified without ambiguity by

 H_{r,h}=F_{r,h}+2H_{r-1,h}+4H_{r-3,h}+7H_{r-4,h} mod3, (4)

with negative-index entries zero. All four previous unit upper pivots are retained in M^{-1}; the factor 9 is exactly the prescribed one.

The matrix F has period 3 in its row index and period 18 in h. For h=0,...,17 its three rows are

 0 0 0 0 0 1 2 1 1 2 1 2 1 2 2 1 2 0

Only h in I1 are used in the actual boundary. To prove the row period rather than infer it from a finite matrix, observe from (1) that dbar_{n+3}-dbar_n=(1,2,2) periodically. This sequence lies in the span of the four shifts of cbar: every sequence annihilated by (X^3-1)(X+1) is reconstructed uniquely from its first four values by M. Its residual under the projection in (3) is therefore zero. Thus F_{r+3,h}=F_{r,h} for all r,h. The column period 18 follows directly from the periods 9 and 6.

## 3. Rank and the precise new rows

The three rows of F at columns h=4,5,6 give

 [[0,1,2],[1,2,1],[0,0,1]],

with determinant 2 modulo 3. These columns are exterior for every s>=27, and there are at least three residual rows. Therefore F has rank at least three. Row period three proves rank at most three. Since L is unit triangular, H_b has the same rank. On its first three rows the transformation involves only those rows and has determinant one, so the SAME columns and first three rows also give a determinant-2 minor of H_b. No computed degree is used in this proof.

Exactly three independent newly divided row constraints occur:

 sum_{h in I1} F_{r,h} t_h=0 mod3, r=0,1,2.          (5)

All other divided rows are their combinations. Here t is the exterior coefficient parameter, not the original reduced cofactor vector. Rationally H_b has rank s-4, so the remaining s-7 pivots are nonunits. In the original full matching matrix, three of the previously 9-divisible pivots have valuation exactly 2; the remaining s-7 have valuation at least 3. This is a statement about the actual monomial matrix after the retained local-unit row scalings, not about the Legendre matrix's Smith pivots.

## 4. What these rows say about the two low residues

The newly divided equations admit a compact intrinsic description. Modulo 3, central coefficients q_J vanish and q_{0..3}=Zt. The previous upper equations are the cbar moment equations; the new equations are the dbar moment equations after those four upper pivots. The sequence dbar is annihilated by (X^3-1)^2=(X-1)^6, since its step-3 difference has period three. The cbar sequence is annihilated by (X-1)^3(X+1). Their combined equations are equivalent, on the exterior polynomial, to divisibility by

 P7(y)=(y-1)^6(y+1).                               (6)

Here is a rank justification of equivalence. Both functionals annihilate every multiple of the monic degree-seven P7. On remainders of degree below seven the four original rows and three divided rows have rank seven: their first seven columns 0,...,6 give the unit 4-by-4 block M and the displayed unit 3-by-3 residual minor. Therefore their common radical on polynomials is precisely the ideal (P7). There are sufficiently many actual rows for these seven tests. Thus actual Q0 must satisfy P7|Q0 modulo 3, strengthening the old multiplicity-three root at 1 to multiplicity six. This still does not fix its constant coefficient or its lifted linear coefficient.

In particular, at this divided precision simultaneous vanishing of the TWO target residues is compatible with primitivity. A concrete witness modulo 3 is

 P(y)=y^2 P7(y)=y^9+y^8+y^6+y^5+y^3+y^2 mod3.

All its support lies in exterior indices for s>=27. It has q0=q1=0, q2=1 and satisfies (6), hence (5). Let tbar be its exterior coordinates in I1; it is nonzero. For any integral lift t, use the exact reconstruction Z from the preceding stage. Then q0=0 modulo 3 and q1=0 modulo 3 still hold. Changing t to t+3u does not change any equation (5), but changes q1/3+q2 modulo 3 by (Z_1 mod3)u. This row is nonzero: at exterior column h=4 its coefficient is 2 (obtained from -M^{-1}v(4)). Thus choose u supported at h=4 to set q1/3+q2 to zero. This preserves primitivity, the exact lower equations and the first four exact upper equations, and H_b t=0 modulo 3.

This witness is NOT asserted to lie in ker H_b over Z_3. It proves precisely that these newly divided rows, even with both residues and primitive normalization enforced, leave excess modular solutions. They cannot settle characteristic-zero survival. Moreover varying the same lift realizes any value of the second residue while leaving all divided equations modulo 3 fixed. The missing conditions are the remaining nonunit directions of H_b and their saturated rows, not another application of (5). We stop rather than divide further or escalate moduli.
