# Cyclic denominator modules and exact return-content cancellation

Author: Main. Status: new author proof, not independently reviewed. This stage refines the coefficient-cancellation remainder of Stage16. It is an exact local interface, not an actual growing-degree content estimate or an irrationality proof.

## 1. Fixed actual normalization

Use the complete canonical diagonal family m=k>=2, h=y+1, indices 0<=i,j<k:

M_ij=mu(h^(i+j)), G_ij=mu(h^(k+i+j)), J_ij=nu_k(h^(i+j)),
C=M-J, B_ij=R_k(h^(i+j)), D_ij=R_k(h^(k+i+j)).

The functional R_k and all endpoint/period jets are exactly those of Stage10. The low block B is not replaced by its high-degree formula. Write

beta(T)=det[[C,G],[B+TJ,D]],
H=J^(-1)(DG^(-1)C-B), beta/beta_k=det(TI-H),
beta_k=(-1)^k det J det G.

With b_k=oddpart binom(2k-2,k-1), L_r=lcm(1,...,r), set E_clear=b_k*2^(3k+2)*L_k*L_(6k), delta=b_k^k*E_clear^k, I_j=delta beta_j, g=gcd_j I_j>0 and A=|I_k|/g. No basis/index factor is suppressed.

Fix p>6k. All blocks are p-integral and delta,det J are p-units. Put O=Z_p, F=Q_p, L=O^k,
a=v_p(det G), d=v_p(g), n=v_p(A)=a-d.

Stage16 gives N_H=(L+HL)/L, ell=len N_H<=a, h_p=a-ell, and

d=h_p+rho_p, rho_p=ell-n>=0.                         (1)

This stage addresses rho_p, which cannot be read from C,G,D alone.

## 2. A cyclic denominator module has a rank-one fractional presentation

Assume N_H is nonzero cyclic, of length t. This is a condition on the actual H, not merely the corank of G modulo p. Then there exist H0 in M_k(O) and primitive u,v in O^k such that

H=H0+p^(-t)u v^T.                                  (2)

Proof: the Smith form UHV over F with U,V in GL_k(O) has exactly one negative depth, -t. Separate that diagonal entry, absorbing its unit in u, and conjugate back by the independent left/right transformations. All other entries are integral. Each distinguished column/row of an invertible integral matrix is primitive. The operation constructs (2); it is not a similarity assertion.

Conversely, (2) with t>=1 and u,v primitive has N_H generated by p^(-t)u+L and of length exactly t: v^T L=O, and p^(-t)u has exact order p^t modulo L. Hence ell=t and h_p=a-t.

The case N_H=0 is already settled by Stage16: n=0,d=a,rho_p=0.

## 3. Return ideal theorem

For a presentation (2), define the integral returns

r_j=v^T H0^j u, 0<=j<k,
w=min_(0<=j<k) v_p(r_j), with v_p(0)=+infinity.

Then the exact identities are

rho_p=min(t,w),
n=t-min(t,w),
d=a-t+min(t,w).                                    (3)

In particular, one unit return forces rho_p=0, whereas divisibility of ALL k returns by p^s, 0<=s<=t, is equivalent to rho_p>=s. Vanishing of only r_0 is not enough. The value min(t,w) is independent of the chosen rank-one presentation, because it equals the intrinsic ell-n; w beyond t need not be intrinsic.

Proof. Set q0(T)=det(TI-H0)=T^k+c1 T^(k-1)+...+ck in O[T]. By the rank-one determinant identity, valid polynomially even at singular T,

q(T):=det(TI-H)=q0(T)-p^(-t)R(T),
R(T)=v^T adj(TI-H0)u.                              (4)

The coefficient of T^(k-1-j) in R, for 0<=j<k, equals

s_j=r_j+c1 r_(j-1)+...+c_j r_0.                    (5)

One proof expands (TI-H0)^(-1)=sum_(j>=0)H0^j T^(-j-1) formally at infinity and multiplies by q0. The first k coefficients give (5). The transformation from (r_0,...,r_(k-1)) to (s_0,...,s_(k-1)) is lower triangular over O with diagonal one, hence has an integral inverse. Therefore the coefficient ideal of R is exactly (r_0,...,r_(k-1)), with minimum valuation w.

If w<t, some coefficient of p^(-t)R has negative valuation w-t, and no integral coefficient of q0 can cancel it. Every coefficient of q has valuation at least w-t, with equality for at least one coefficient. Its least p-power clearer is p^(t-w). If w>=t (including R=0), q is monic integral and its least p-power clearer is 1. The primitive normalization says this least exponent is n. Equations (1) now give (3).

Cayley-Hamilton for the integral H0 implies that all returns r_j for j>=k are O-linear combinations of the first k. Thus the congruence in (3) is equivalent to v^T annihilating the integral Krylov module O[H0]u modulo p^s. This module need not be saturated; replacing it by its saturation would change the condition.

## 4. A sufficient actual Smith branch, with all contraction factors

Suppose G has exactly one positive Smith depth, necessarily a>0. Write

G=U diag(1,...,1,p^a) V, U,V in GL_k(O),
G^(-1)=G0+p^(-a)x y^T,
G0=V^(-1)diag(1,...,1,0)U^(-1),
x=V^(-1)e_k, y^T=e_k^T U^(-1).

Any diagonal units can be absorbed in U,V. Define the actual quantities

H0=J^(-1)(DG0 C-B), u*=J^(-1)Dx, v*^T=y^T C.

They are integral and

H=H0+p^(-a)u* v*^T.                               (6)

Let e_u=min valuation of the entries of u*, e_v=min valuation of the entries of v*. A zero vector has depth infinity. If e_u+e_v>=a then H is integral, n=0 and d=a. Otherwise put

t=a-e_u-e_v>0, u=p^(-e_u)u*, v=p^(-e_v)v*.

These u,v are primitive and (6) becomes (2), with unchanged complete H0. Consequently

d=e_u+e_v+min(t,min_(0<=j<k)v_p(v^T H0^j u)),       (7)
n=t-min(t,min_(0<=j<k)v_p(v^T H0^j u)).

The first two terms in d are the structural contraction; the return term is additional coefficient cancellation. This is a formula for the FINAL local gcd, not a lower bound obtained by discarding B. No occurrence or distribution of this Smith branch for growing k is proved.

## 5. Global certified improvement and its remaining gap

Let C_k be any set of primes p>6k for which nonzero cyclic N_H has been proved, and choose actual presentations (2). Define

Q_return=product_(p in C_k) p^min(t_p,w_p),
Q_struct=product_(p>6k) p^h_p.

Only primes dividing det G occur. Equations (1) and (3) imply

Q_struct Q_return divides g,
A_large divides (det G)_large/(Q_struct Q_return).  (8)

There is no double counting: the return factor consumes rho_p, not h_p. Primes outside C_k retain the Stage16 bound. No claim about p<=6k is made.

For S=e+pi in the inherited nonvanishing range k>=131072, a sufficient condition to contradict a fixed rational denominator q>=1 is

A_small*(det G)_large/(Q_struct Q_return)
       *|beta(S)/beta_k| < q^(-k).                 (9)

This follows from |P(S)|=A|beta(S)/beta_k| and (8). It is not proved on any infinite sequence. Existing complete signed-error bounds remain the analytic input; the new analytic leading-constant report is not needed for this exact statement.

## 6. Limits and next actual target

This is new symbolic author research. No finite computation, prime scan, or independent review was performed. The rank-one determinant identity and integral Cayley-Hamilton argument prove (3) for every actual cyclic denominator branch. The missing arithmetic is a uniform estimate or favorable infinite-family occurrence for the actual return ideal, with all other primes and A_small accounted for.

The condition v^T H0^j u=0 mod p^s for every j<k is explicit but still potentially as hard as coefficient content. It must not be presented as a solved denominator estimate. It offers a different matrix interface in which the complete B acts through an integral propagation operator. A next worthwhile step is to derive an actual moment/jet identity for these returns, not to replace them with arbitrary-matrix examples or repeat a first-layer pairing test.
