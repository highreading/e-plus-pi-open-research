# Actual cross-linking through a finite boundary matrix

Status: original author algebra, not independently reviewed. This stage does not repeat the Stage16 review or either separate injectivity problem. It supplies an all-depth boundary evaluation and an exact Smith test for cross-linking, but no uniform improved bound on its length and no asserted orthogonality theorem.

## Objects

Let k>=2, p>6k, O=Z_p, L=O^k. Retain the canonical h=y+1 matrices G,C=M-J,D and the complete low rational B. Write m_n=mu(h^n), w_n=nu_k(h^n), and r_n=R_k(h^n). Thus G_ij=m_(k+i+j), C_ij=m_(i+j)-w_(i+j), D_ij=r_(k+i+j). The full jet is

w_n=n! 2^n binom(k-1,n)(2k-2n-3)!!/(2k-3)!! for 0<=n<k,
w_n=0 for n>=k.

All entries are p-integral; J is a unit matrix. Define fractional lattices

Lambda_C={x in Q_p^k:Gx,Cx in L},
Lambda_D={y in Q_p^k:Gy,Dy in L}.

Their quotients by L are K_C,K_D. Pairing is x^T G y modulo O. The horizontal and vertical source systems in the supplied records are used as author inputs, not re-reviewed.

## 1. Boundary reconstruction, not inversion of G

Let S e_i=e_(i+1) for i<k-1 and S e_(k-1)=0. Set e=e_(k-1), e0=e_0 and

c=G e0=(m_k,...,m_(2k-1))^T,
t=(0,m_(2k),m_(2k+1),...,m_(3k-2))^T.

Then the following exact identity holds over O:

G-S^T G S^T=c e0^T+e t^T=:E.                         (1)
G=sum_(j=0)^(k-1) (S^T)^j E (S^T)^j.                (2)

To check (1), the shifted matrix at i<k-1,j>=1 has entry G_(i+1,j-1)=G_ij. Its first column and last row vanish. Their remaining entries are exactly c and t, with the corner counted once because t_0=0. Iterating (1) proves (2); the final remainder is zero because S^k=0. This is a finite Stein reconstruction with different left/right shifts, not an assertion that G or the pairing is symmetric under a truncated shift. No nonunit pivot is inverted.

For x,y in the fractional lattices define boundary coordinate vectors of length 2k, indexed by j=0,...,k-1:

alpha_j(x)=c^T S^j x,       beta_j(x)=e^T S^j x,
gamma_j(y)=e0^T(S^T)^j y=y_j,
delta_j(y)=t^T(S^T)^j y.

Equations (1)-(2) give the exact all-depth boundary expression

<x,y>=sum_j [alpha_j(x) gamma_j(y)+beta_j(x) delta_j(y)] mod O. (3)

All G endpoint moments through 3k-2 are explicit. In contrast to a formula involving G^{-1}, (3) evaluates the pairing using shifts and boundary moment rows only. It is not legitimate to discard alpha_j for j>0 on grounds that Gx is integral: those terms test shifted vectors, whose integrality is a separate condition. Nor may one discard j=0 just because alpha_0 is integral: gamma_0 can be fractional.

## 2. Actual simultaneous shift restrictions

For clarity retain the additional endpoint vectors

v_i=m_(2k+i), d_i=r_(2k+i), c=G e0.

The exact Hankel commutators imply for x in Lambda_C and y in Lambda_D

CSx=-c(e^Tx) mod L,
GSx=e(v^Tx)-v(e^Tx) mod L,
DSy=e(d^Ty)-d(e^Ty) mod L,
GSy=e(v^Ty)-v(e^Ty) mod L.                            (4)

For the C equation, c^Tx=e0^TGx is integral, as is S^TCx. The other equations follow identically without assuming any of the displayed scalar boundary values vanish. These are vector congruences for fractional representatives; multiplying them by a fractional row does not preserve their modulus.

The mixed G commutator has the exact scalar identity

x^TG Sy-(Sx)^TG y=(e^Tx)(v^Ty)-(v^Tx)(e^Ty).           (5)

If shifts are not in the fractional lattices, (5) is still an identity of rational scalars but is not a relation between induced endomorphisms of K_C and K_D. Even when they are in those lattices, it determines a difference of pairings, not their absolute initial value. Thus the separate boundary systems do not force (3) to vanish by telescoping.

The added endpoints in v,d reach index 3k-1 and are retained. With N=2k+i their exact values are

v_i=sum_(a=0)^N binom(N,a) D_(2a),
d_i=-sum_(a=0)^N binom(N,a)(2a)!+c_k sum_(a=0)^(k+i) binom(k+i,a)/(2a+1).

D_(2a) here is the derangement number. The latter formula is used only for the high block, where h^k kills the whole period jet. All new rational denominators are at most 4k-1, apart from c_k whose prime support is below p. The low block B is not replaced by this formula.

## 3. An exact extra boundary matrix controlling the cross image

Choose N at least the largest Smith depth of G, so both K modules are killed by p^N. Define full-rank integral solution lattices

U_C={u in L:Gu,Cu in p^N L},
U_D={v in L:Gv,Dv in p^N L}.

Then Lambda_C=p^(-N)U_C and Lambda_D=p^(-N)U_D. Proof: p^N G^{-1}L is contained in L; the conditions then translate in both directions. These lattices contain p^N L and have O-bases, represented as columns of integral invertible-over-Q_p matrices U,V. They can be constructed by Smith/Hermite operations on the full congruence systems. In particular the U system includes every jet weight above, not merely its anti-diagonal.

Form two 2k-by-k boundary matrices

F_C=[ rows c^T S^j U for 0<=j<k ; rows e^T S^j U for 0<=j<k ],
F_D=[ rows e0^T(S^T)^j V for 0<=j<k ; rows t^T(S^T)^j V for 0<=j<k ].

The extra boundary matrix is

Z_N=F_C^T F_D in M_k(O).                              (6)

Formula (2) proves Z_N=U^T G V, but (6) specifies it entirely in terms of the two sets of boundary coordinates. The restricted pairing on these generating lifts is exactly p^(-2N)Z_N modulo O. This is a cross test: separate Smith contents of U_C and U_D alone do not determine it.

Let sigma_1,...,sigma_k be the Smith depths of Z_N. They are finite because U,G,V are nonsingular over Q_p. Then the length s of the linking homomorphism K_D -> Hom(K_C,Q_p/O) is exactly

s=sum_i max(0,2N-sigma_i).                            (7)

Its image exponent is p^a_cross, where

a_cross=max_i max(0,2N-sigma_i).                       (8)

Proof: columns of p^(-N)U generate Lambda_C, so evaluation of a character on these columns embeds Hom(K_C,Q_p/O) into (Q_p/O)^k. The map induced by columns of p^(-N)V is multiplication by p^(-2N)Z_N modulo O. Smith changes on its input and output are O-automorphisms; a diagonal entry p^(sigma_i-2N) has image length max(0,2N-sigma_i). Relations coming from integral representatives are already killed: pairing Lambda_C with L, and L with Lambda_D, is integral because G is symmetric and G Lambda_C,G Lambda_D are integral. Thus no further quotient changes this image length.

Consequently exact orthogonality is equivalent to Z_N in p^(2N)M_k(O). More generally p^q annihilates the cross image if and only if Z_N is entrywise divisible by p^(2N-q), for 0<=q<=2N. This criterion and (7) are independent of the chosen solution-lattice bases and of larger permissible N because they calculate the same intrinsic map.

For a practically smaller certificate, (6) may be accumulated as a sum of k matrices of rank at most two, one for each j. Divisibility must hold for the SUM. Requiring termwise divisibility is sufficient but not necessary; cancellation between boundary terms can occur. This preserves precisely the cancellation lost by an argument based on separate injectivity tests.

## 4. What is and is not settled about orthogonality

Equations (3)-(8) test the proposed actual-family orthogonality at every depth without assuming it. No argument here proves Z_N divisible by p^(2N) for the actual family, and no exact actual-family counterexample has been computed. Therefore orthogonality remains unresolved rather than accepted or refuted. In particular (4) supplies no such divisibility: it leaves fractional terminal coefficients and shifted endpoint moments in (3).

The new exact boundary Smith invariant (7), rather than merely h=c+b-s, is the replacement structural identity. It determines s and its forced annihilator once the actual boundary matrix is controlled. It does not by itself give a genuinely stronger uniform bound than s<=min(c,b). The missing arithmetic statement can be made precise: establish a uniform lower bound on the Smith depths of F_C^TF_D beyond those implied by the separate solution-lattice indices. Termwise or total divisibility to depth 2N would prove orthogonality; weaker depth improvements give the exact annihilator in (8). No occurrence or size estimate is asserted for such improvements.

This bounded branch stops at that all-depth identity. It does not rerun horizontal/vertical injectivity, an old prime atlas, or k=27. No numerical experiment is claimed.

## 5. Primitive normalization and handoff

Retain the canonical beta(T)=det[C,G;B+TJ,D], specified complete p-unit clearer delta, I_j=delta beta_j, g=gcd(I_0,...,I_k), and A=|I_k|/g. Stage16's scoped local algebra gives

d=v_p(g)=c+b-s+rho_p,
rho_p=ell-v_p(A)>=0,
H=J^{-1}(DG^{-1}C-B).

Only s is the target of (7). The complete B stays in H and in the characteristic-polynomial cancellation rho_p, owned by Main. No endpoint boundary formula permits its deletion. The new identities do not estimate rho_p, small-prime contributions, or global primitive content. They are author proofs, not independent verification or an irrationality result.
