# Logarithmic endpoint transfer: a bounded partial advance

Author: Child 3. Author proofs below, not independent verification. Scope is m=k only. The scalar asymptotic and the complete physical identities are inherited. The determinant coefficient remains conditional.

Sources read in full: Main FIXED_REFERENCE_AND_NORMALIZED_CONTENT_STAGE9.md and Child 4 (09f65248) FIXED_REFERENCE_REVIEW_AND_ENDPOINT_CONSTANT_STAGE7.md. Their scalar asymptotic is used, not reproved. The earlier V2 linear-threshold derivation is not repeated.

## 1. Definitions and outcome

On [0,1], gamma(dy)=y^(-1/2)dy, w0=exp(sqrt(y))gamma/2, sigma_k=w0(1+u_k), u_k=c_k exp(-sqrt(y))(1+y)^(-k), c_k=4^k/binom(2k-2,k-1). Put f=log(1+u_k), M=log(1+c_k), Q=sqrt(k)(log k)^(3/2), alpha=2/(3pi sqrt(2)). Thus 0<=f<=M and M=(1/2)log k+O(1).

Let P0 denote the degree-below-k projection in L2(w0), and let

 T0=tr(P0 f P0), E=log[D_k(sigma_k)/D_k(w0)], Delta=E-T0.

Two new estimates are proved here:

 (A) |T0-Tgamma| <= C sqrt(k) M = o(Q),
     Tgamma=integral f(y)K_(gamma,k)(y,y)dgamma(y),

 (B) 0<=Delta <= [(exp(M)-1-M)/M^2] V0,
     V0=||(I-P0)fP0||_HS^2.

C is an explicit absolute constant below. Estimate (A) removes the actual fixed weight from the shrinking-test-function trace problem, with a quantitative error already smaller than the desired main term. Estimate (B) uses the logarithm, not the raw squared bump. Since exp(M)=O(sqrt(k)), a sufficient defect estimate is

 V0=o((log k)^(7/2)).                                      (1)

In particular V0=O((log k)^3) suffices. Neither (1) nor the reference trace asymptotic is claimed proved here. This is a partial advance, not the proposed determinant theorem.

## 2. Quantitative fixed-weight projection transfer

Work first in H=L2(gamma). Let P be the polynomial projection, and let J be multiplication by y in its orthonormal polynomial basis. Set s(y)=exp(sqrt(y)/2); the constant 1/2 in w0 does not affect the following subspace. The unitary map from L2(w0) to H sends its polynomial space onto s times the polynomial space. Let R be the orthogonal projection onto this subspace. Since multiplication operators commute with this unitary map,

 T0=tr(R f),  Tgamma=tr(P f).

Here f is the full k-dependent logarithm; there is no replacement of the bump.

Let S=s(J), and B=P S^2 P on ran(P). Since S>=I, B>=I. The projection onto S ran(P) is

 R=S P B^(-1) P S.

Consequently

 tr((I-P)R)=||(I-P)S P B^(-1/2)||_HS^2
            <=||(I-P)SP||_HS^2.

P and R have equal finite rank, so

 ||R-P||_HS^2=2tr((I-P)R)<=2||(I-P)SP||_HS^2.              (2)

There is an absolute bound for the last norm. Expand s(y) in T_n(2y-1). Its cosine function is phi(t)=exp(cos(t/2)/2), 0<=t<=pi. We have phi'(0)=0, |phi'(pi)|=1/4, and |phi''|<=3sqrt(e)/16. Twice integrating its cosine coefficients gives

 |a_n|<=K_s/n^2,
 K_s=1/(2pi)+3sqrt(e)/8.

For every n the cross block of T_n(2J-I) has rank at most n and operator norm at most 1, because J is tridiagonal with spectrum in [0,1]. Thus

 ||(I-P)SP||_HS <=sum_(n>=1)|a_n|sqrt(n)<=3K_s.             (3)

Uniform convergence of the Chebyshev series and absolute convergence in HS justify the infinite sum. Equations (2)-(3) give ||R-P||_HS<=3sqrt(2)K_s. Its rank is at most 2k, hence

 |T0-Tgamma|<=||R-P||_1 ||f||_infinity
             <=6K_s sqrt(k) M.                            (4)

Because M=O(log k), (4) is o(Q). Unlike weak convergence, it is uniform over all bounded test functions of the given amplitude; shrinking support causes no loss. The trace-transfer constant is C=6K_s.

## 3. Exact logarithmic tilt and the compression defect

For 0<=t<=1 let wt=exp(tf)w0, and let Pt be its degree-below-k polynomial projection. Define

 F(t)=log[D_k(wt)/D_k(w0)].

All derivatives are justified directly by the finite positive Gram matrix and bounded multiplication f. Differentiating log determinant gives

 F'(t)=tr(Pt f Pt),
 F''(t)=tr(Pt f^2 Pt)-tr((Pt f Pt)^2)
       =||(I-Pt)fPt||_HS^2=:Vt>=0.                        (5)

These formulas may equivalently be derived from the finite Andreief ensemble, but no probabilistic limit theorem is needed. Since F(0)=0, F(1)=E and F'(0)=T0,

 Delta=integral_0^1 (1-t)Vt dt.                            (6)

This is an exact nonnegative defect expressed through logarithmic multipliers in tilted polynomial spaces.

A useful comparison avoids estimating the changing kernels pointwise. Fix a w0-orthonormal polynomial basis p_0,...,p_(k-1), and let Bt be its wt Gram. Since 1<=exp(tf)<=exp(tM), Bt>=I. For each p_i approximate f p_i by its w0 polynomial projection, and collect the residuals r_i=(I-P0)fp_i. The wt-best polynomial approximations have no larger residual Gram in Loewner order: for every linear combination, orthogonal projection minimizes its squared residual norm. Calling that best-residual Gram Ct, we therefore have

 0<=Ct<= [<r_i,r_j>_(wt)] <=exp(tM)[<r_i,r_j>_(w0)].

Upon orthonormalizing the polynomial basis in wt, (5) becomes Vt=tr(Bt^(-1)Ct). Positivity and Bt^(-1)<=I give

 Vt<=tr Ct<=exp(tM)V0.                                   (7)

Integrating (7) in (6) proves

 0<=Delta<=V0 integral_0^1(1-t)exp(tM)dt
          =V0 (exp(M)-1-M)/M^2.                           (8)

The expression at M=0 is interpreted as 1/2. In the present application M>0. Formula (8) still loses a factor of order sqrt(k)/(log k)^2, but its input is the variance of f, whose amplitude is logarithmic rather than square-root in k. It is therefore genuinely different from the previously insufficient raw-bump HS estimate.

The exact kernel input required in (8) is

 V0=(1/2) double integral (f(y)-f(z))^2
                |K_(w0,k)(y,z)|^2 dw0(y)dw0(z).            (9)

The normalization 1/2 follows from the projection identity. An O(log^3 k) estimate for (9) would give Delta=O(sqrt(k)log k)=o(Q). No estimate for the raw difference u_k(y)-u_k(z) is used.

## 4. Smaller explicit pair of sufficient remaining estimates

Let omega(dy)=dy/[pi sqrt(y(1-y))], J_k=integral f domega. The inherited scalar theorem states

 k J_k=alpha Q+O(sqrt(k log k)).

By (4) it now suffices to prove the REFERENCE rather than actual-weight trace estimate

 Tgamma=k J_k+o(Q).                                       (10)

For example, if

 dgamma_k=sup_t |integral_[0,t] K_(gamma,k)dgamma/k
                         -omega([0,t])|,

then dgamma_k=o(sqrt(log k/k)) suffices, by integration by parts for the decreasing f. This discrepancy is a target, not an established bound here. Equation (4) proves that the earlier actual-w0 discrepancy hypothesis was stronger than necessary for this particular family of tests.

Independently, (1) suffices for the defect. Under (10) and (1), equations (4), (8) and the inherited scalar theorem give

 E=alpha Q+o(Q).

This implication has two explicit, distinct unproved inputs. The complete proposed coefficient has not been obtained. A practical next step is to bound the logarithmic variance (9) by O(log^3 k), using a local Christoffel--Darboux estimate, while treating (10) using the explicit even-Legendre reference kernel. The present bounded step stops rather than asserting either local estimate without proof.

## 5. Exact arithmetic threshold and a usable finite bracket

Retain the inherited complete physical normalization for m=k>=131072. Let I_j=delta beta_j for a complete integer clearer, g=gcd(I_0,...,I_k)>0, Pphysical(T)=sum (I_j/g)T^j, A=|I_k/g|, and

 kappa_k=[4^(k-1)/binom(2k-2,k-1)]^k,
 exp(-192e^4)<=theta_k<=1.

All upper and lower endpoint jets remain included in the inherited identities. They give exactly

 |Pphysical(S)|=A theta_k D_k(w0) exp(E)/kappa_k,
 Tactual(v)=kappa_k/[v^k theta_k D_k(w0)exp(E)],
 |Pphysical(S)|<v^(-k) iff A<Tactual(v).

Set Z_k(v)=log kappa_k-log D_k(w0)-k log v-log theta_k,
 H_k=6K_s sqrt(k)M,
 R_k=(exp(M)-1-M)V0/M^2.

The proved estimates (4),(8) give the finite bracket

 Z_k(v)-Tgamma-H_k-R_k <=log Tactual(v)
                       <=Z_k(v)-Tgamma+H_k.              (11)

No unknown gcd has been replaced by a partial content. Although (11) still contains the explicit reference integral and the logarithmic variance, it isolates a substantially smaller sufficient analytic interface. Conditional on (10),(1), it becomes

 log Tactual(v)=log kappa_k-log D_k(w0)-k log v
                    -log theta_k-alpha Q+o(Q).

For hypothetical rational S=u/v the inherited complete nonvanishing yields |Pphysical(S)|>=v^(-k); the strict opposite would exclude that denominator.
