# Actual matching elimination modulo 9 and the remaining saturated boundary

Author derivation; not independent verification. This is one elimination attempt for k=s=3^a, a>=3. It does not rerun k=27, scan degrees, or compute modulo 27. The input jet theorem is ../2fff76dd/SECOND_LAYER_LOW_OUTPUT.md: on 3|q1, second-layer survival is equivalent to (q0,q1/3+q2) nonzero modulo 3. We do not repeat that theorem. The new result is an actual unit-pivot elimination of the entire lower block, followed by four upper unit pivots. The remaining characteristic-zero boundary has every coefficient divisible by 9. Its saturation, not the visible equations modulo 9, is the exact missing information.

## 1. Full actual equations over Z_3

Put n=2s, c_r=(D_{2r}-(-1)^r)/2, w_r=(2r)!+(2r+2)!, and

 C_{r,h}=c_{r+h}, 0<=r<s, 0<=h<2s,
 T_{i,h}=3^(a+1)/(2(i+h)+1)-3^(a+1)w_{i+h}/4,
                         0<=i<s-1, 0<=h<2s.

Every entry of T lies in Z_(3), hence Z_3: 2(i+h)+1<=6s-5 and its valuation is at most a+1. T is exactly 3^(a+1)K/4 in monomial tests. Therefore [C;T] has exactly the same characteristic-zero kernel and the same integral kernel over Z_3 as the original full matrix with lower multiplier L. Since v3(L)=a+1, this lower rescaling is a local unit rescaling of the original integer rows; no primitive-kernel content is changed. The signed endpoint term is retained in c_r.

Assume the inherited rational full rank 2s-1. Its Z_3 kernel is a saturated rank-one module generated by the primitive monomial Q0, up to a 3-adic unit. These facts alone do not identify the two residues.

## 2. Actual lower block has s-1 explicit unit pivots

Set m=(3s-1)/2, b=(s-1)/2, d=(5s-1)/2, and extend coefficient indices by zero outside [0,2s-1]. Modulo 9 the full lower equations are

 q_(m-i)+3 q_(b-i)+6 q_(d-i)=0, 0<=i<s-1.       (1)

Proof: the factorial term in T is divisible by 9. Only odd denominators of valuation at least a can survive. They are s,3s,5s, with coefficients 3,1,3/5=6 modulo 9, respectively. All other denominators yield coefficients divisible by 9. This keeps every denominator term surviving at this precision.

Let J={(s+3)/2,...,(3s-1)/2}, ordered as h=m-i, and let I be its complement in {0,...,2s-1}, in increasing order. I has s+1 elements, including 0,1,2,3. The square lower block T_J is identity modulo 3 and hence invertible over Z_3. Moreover T_J is identity modulo 9: the other two indices in (1) are never in J. Define the EXACT integral matrix

 N=-T_J^(-1)T_I.

For exterior coefficient vector z=(q_h)_{h in I}, all lower matching equations are equivalent to q_J=Nz. This is a bijection of integral solution modules and preserves primitiveness, since z is a subset of the original coordinates and Nz is integral.

Formula (1) yields explicit reconstruction modulo 9:

 q_(s+h)=-3q_h for 0<=h<=b,
 q_(h-s)=-6q_h for m+2<=h<=2s-1.

These two disjoint rules cover all central coordinates J. The two exterior indices b+1 and m+1 contribute no central correction modulo 9. This is a boundary relation from the actual compact denominators, not the earlier output nullspace criterion.

## 3. Exact upper Schur matrix and its surprising precision defect

After the lower elimination define

 A=C_I+C_J N, an s by (s+1) matrix over Z_3.

The exact full kernel is now A z=0. The columns of A modulo 9 are

 A_{r,h}=c_{r+h}-3c_{r+s+h},          0<=h<=b,
 A_{r,h}=c_{r+h},                    h=b+1 or h=m+1,
 A_{r,h}=c_{r+h}-6c_{r+h-s},          m+2<=h<=2s-1.   (2)

The actual c moments satisfy the MONIC order-four recurrence modulo 9

 c_(r+4)-2c_(r+3)-4c_(r+1)-7c_r=0 mod 9.          (3)

A direct division-free proof uses e_0=0, e_(r+1)=(2r+2)(2r+1)e_r+2r(r+1), c_r=e_r+(1-(-1)^r)/2. The state resets e_9=0 modulo 9 and its coefficients repeat with period 9, so c has period 18. One period is

 0,1,4,7,0,5,3,1,4,1,0,5,6,1,4,4,0,5.

Substitution verifies (3) at each residue class. Equivalently the divided first-layer recurrence

 (c_(r+4)+c_(r+3)-c_(r+1)-c_r)/3
       =2c_r+c_(r+1)+c_(r+3) modulo 3

holds, with quotients interpreted after the numerator is formed. Its period is (2,0,2). Thus one division of the naive order-four rows by 3 only returns an existing mod-3 row combination; it does not reveal new independent constraints.

Every column in (2) is a fixed combination of shifts of c. Hence every row of A from row 4 onward obeys exactly the same recurrence (3) modulo 9. The first four columns (h=0,1,2,3) and rows (r=0,1,2,3) form a unit block A00: modulo 3 it is the moment Hankel block

 [[0,1,1,1],[1,1,1,0],[1,1,0,2],[1,0,2,0]],

whose determinant is 5, a 3-unit. Consequently A has rank exactly four over Z/9 in the precise sense that unit row/column elimination leaves four identity pivots and a zero remaining block modulo 9.

## 4. Remaining exact boundary and saturation depth

Partition the exterior columns into I0={0,1,2,3} and I1=I\I0, with |I1|=s-3. Partition rows into first four and remaining s-4. Define EXACT matrices over Z_3

 Z=-A00^(-1) A01,
 R=A11-A10 A00^(-1) A01,
 H=R/9.                                             (4)

Section 3 proves H is integral. These definitions use exact rational matrices, not an inversion modulo 9 followed by arbitrary lifts. H has rational rank s-4 by the inherited full rank. For t in Z_3^(s-3), the saturated full matching kernel is exactly

 Ht=0,    z_I1=t,    z_I0=Zt,    q_J=Nz.             (5)

Primitivity is equivalent to t not being zero modulo 3. Both eliminations use unit pivots and integral reconstruction; the division R/9 changes row content, not the characteristic-zero kernel or the original primitive normalization.

This provides s+3 explicit unit pivots in the full matching matrix. The remaining s-4 nonzero Smith pivots are 9 times the Smith pivots of H, up to units. In particular they are all divisible by 9; merely solving the original matrix modulo 9 leaves s-3 free directions, whereas the characteristic-zero kernel has dimension one. This quantifies the unsaturated ambiguity for every s in the prescribed family.

Let h be the largest 3-adic Smith exponent of H (zero is permitted). There is an exact saturation-depth criterion:

 image modulo 9 of {t:Ht=0 exactly}
 = image modulo 9 of {t:Ht=0 modulo 3^(h+2)}.         (6)

Proof: in integral invertible Smith coordinates, equations are 3^e_i t_i=0 at precision h+2, with e_i<=h. They force all constrained coordinates to be zero modulo 9. Conversely the free coordinate lifts to an exact kernel vector. Integral invertible coordinate changes preserve reduction modulo 9. The same precision criterion for R=9H is h+4. This is a theoretical stopping criterion, NOT a calculation at a higher modulus: no modulo-27 or larger experiment is proposed or performed here.

Equivalently one can saturate the rows of H over Z_3 and then work only modulo 9. A saturated row basis Hsat has s-4 rows and rank s-4 modulo 3, and ker(Hsat mod9) is exactly the reduction of the integral characteristic-zero kernel. Knowing H modulo 3, or guessing h=0, is not sufficient without a rank certificate. A proved unit (s-4)-minor of H would give h=0 and make precision 9 sufficient for H. No such unit minor or upper bound on h is established in this stage.

## 5. Exact projection to the two output residues

Use Z_r to denote row r of Z, r=0,1,2,3. Since these are the original exterior indices, q0=Z_0 t, q1=Z_1 t, q2=Z_2 t. Therefore the desired projection of the saturated lattice, restricted to the branch, is exactly

 { (Z_0 t mod3, (Z_1+3Z_2)t/3 mod3):
       Ht=0, Z_1t in 3Z_3 },                         (7)

with primitive actual generators selected by t not zero modulo 3. This map factors through t modulo 9; the displayed division by 3 is performed only on the branch. It is NOT legal to replace Ht=0 by the unsaturated equations [C;T]q=0 modulo 9.

In terms of any genuinely saturated row basis Hsat, both target residues can vanish for a primitive actual generator if and only if there exists t modulo 9 satisfying

 Hsat t=0 modulo 9,
 Z_0t=0 modulo 3,
 (Z_1+3Z_2)t=0 modulo 9,
 t not zero modulo 3.                               (8)

The third congruence already implies the branch Z_1t=0 modulo 3. Exact lifting of ker Hsat ensures this is a criterion for the characteristic-zero primitive generator, not for spurious modular solutions. Since the exact kernel has rank one, its projected image on the assumed primitive branch is either zero or a line in F3^2, and a nonzero line is traversed by primitive multiples only at its two nonzero points.

The precise missing saturated-row information is now Hsat modulo 9 in (8), or a sufficient saturation-depth certificate (6) together with the corresponding boundary reduction. The visible actual rows (2)-(3) yield no further relation after the four unit pivots: R is identically zero modulo 9. Thus this elimination attempt cannot exclude (8). It does not prove that the primitive actual kernel lies in the output nullspace either. Further unproved row divisions must not be treated as available modular equations.
