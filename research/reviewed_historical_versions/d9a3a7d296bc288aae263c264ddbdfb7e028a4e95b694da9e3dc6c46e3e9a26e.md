# Stage 2 scope clarification

This note qualifies a potentially overbroad sentence in Section 8 of BORDERED_DETERMINANT_TRANSPORT_STAGE2.md. The sentence beginning 'There is NOT a fixed finite closed k-evolution for the four B_ab' means only:

**The displayed identities do not furnish a fixed finite closed k-evolution for the four B_ab.**

No impossibility theorem for every actual nonlinear determinant recurrence has been proved. The precise negative theorem is Section 6: the smallest q4(j+H)-invariant linear column-weight space containing an actual column with nonzero support at each row level has dimension k+1 for either singly bordered row list. Section 7 separately proves that unrestricted actual column-projector decorations produce determinants of response degrees k and k+1. Neither result is a lower bound for all compressed nonlinear algorithms.

The exact positive arithmetic result is unchanged: with the four full D=L_(k+1)-cleared affine borders, rho=L_(k+1)/L_k, and N0,N1,N2 the complete coefficients of their two-by-two determinant,

 gcd(N0,N1,N2)=rho^k G_k G_(k+1).

This is an adjacent content identity with a missing boundary estimate, not a uniform content bound. Both endpoint terms, all singular factors, the row-weight coordinate indices, and the final physical gcd remain as in the stage report. The confined k=3 to k=4 computation passed, but is not independent verification. The general existence of a different fixed finite nonlinear closure remains unresolved.
