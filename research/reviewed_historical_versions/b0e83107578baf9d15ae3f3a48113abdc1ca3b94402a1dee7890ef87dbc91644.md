# Diagonal local coefficient content with all nonunit pivots retained

Author: Main. Status: symbolic author derivation, not independently reviewed. This consolidates the recent diagonal arithmetic notes. It supplies exact local reductions, not a uniform valuation bound or an irrationality proof. S denotes the actual e+pi.

## 1. Complete diagonal pencil

Use the canonical polynomial beta_(k,k)(T) from work/session_20261002_codex_continuation/main/GENERAL_POLE_RANK_M_PRIMITIVE_INTERFACE.md, with k>=2. Set h=y+1. In monic row and column bases h^i and h^j, define k-by-k matrices, indexed by 0<=i,j<k,

    M_ij=mu(h^(i+j)),
    G_ij=mu(h^(k+i+j)),
    J_ij=nu_k(h^(i+j)),
    B_ij=R_k(h^(i+j)),
    D_ij=R_k(h^(k+i+j)),
    C=M-J.

Here mu(y^n)=D_(2n), where D_n is the derangement number. The functionals nu_k and R_k are the complete normalized period response and rational remainder in the historical construction. No endpoint contribution is removed.

All these polynomial basis changes are monic triangular and have determinant one. The functional nu_k annihilates every multiple of h^k. Consequently the actual pencil is exactly

    beta(T)=det[[C,G],[B+TJ,D]].                         (1)

The matrix G is symmetric and positive definite over the reals, with

    det G=F*_k=det M_[mu(y+1)^k,k]>0.

The jet matrix is anti-triangular. Its anti-diagonal entries are all

    gamma_k=(k-1)!/(1/2)_(k-1)
           =4^(k-1)/binom(2k-2,k-1).

Thus

    det J=(-1)^binom(k,2) gamma_k^k,
    beta_k=(-1)^k det J det G !=0.                     (2)

These algebraic identities do not require the large analytic threshold. Application to nonzero values at S uses the inherited theorem for k>=131072.

Let delta be the full physical clearer from the historical construction, I_j=delta beta_j, and g=gcd(I_0,...,I_k)>0. The positive primitive leading coefficient is

    A=|I_k|/g.

Equivalently, A is the least positive integer clearing all coefficients of the monic polynomial beta/beta_k. Indeed beta/beta_k is the primitive integer coefficient vector divided by its positive leading coefficient; Bezout for that primitive vector proves minimality. This statement retains the complete coefficient gcd.

## 2. Large-prime hypotheses

Fix a prime p>6k. All entries of the five matrices in (1) belong to Z_p, and J is invertible over Z_p. To check denominators, the inherited complete clearer has prime support at most 6k: its factors are powers of 2, factorial/binomial factors supported below 2k, and least common multiples through 6k. The numerator and denominator of det J likewise have no prime divisor above 2k. Thus delta and det J are p-adic units.

Put

    a=v_p(det G), d=v_p(g).

Equation (2) implies

    v_p(A)=a-d,   0<=d<=a.                             (3)

If a=0, this already gives d=0. Henceforth assume a>0 and let r>=1 be the nullity of G modulo p.

The statements below concern Z_p and formal power series over Z_p. A p-adic unit change of coordinates is not being asserted to be an integer unimodular change globally. Its determinant contributes no p-adic valuation, and its exact factor remains displayed.

## 3. Symmetric reduction retaining the nonunit block

There is W in GL_k(Z_p) such that

    W^T G W=diag(G0,E),

where G0 has size k-r and unit determinant, while E has size r and every entry lies in p Z_p. Both blocks are nonsingular over Q_p. When r=k, G0 is empty and its determinant is one.

For existence, choose a complement to the radical of the symmetric form modulo p. Its restricted form is nondegenerate: a vector in the complement orthogonal to that complement is orthogonal to the radical as well, hence lies in the radical and must be zero. Lift this basis to Z_p. The resulting upper block is invertible; an invertible triangular congruence eliminates the cross block exactly. The remaining block reduces to zero modulo p.

Define

    alpha=min valuation of the entries of E.

Then alpha>=1 and is finite. It equals the smallest valuation among the nonunit Smith invariants of G, because the transformation is invertible over Z_p and G0 is a unit block. Also v_p(det E)=a. No factor p^alpha, and no individual Smith pivot, is divided out.

## 4. Exact reversed-polynomial reduction

Reverse the polynomial by

    chi(z)=z^k beta(1/z).

Multiplying the lower rows in (1) by z and swapping its two row blocks proves

    chi(z)=(-1)^k det(J+zB)
              det[G-z C(J+zB)^(-1)D].                 (4)

This identity holds over Q_p(z) and over Z_p[[z]], since J has unit determinant. Define

    K(z)=W^T C(J+zB)^(-1)D W
        =[[K00(z),K01(z)],[K10(z),K11(z)]],

partitioned according to G0 and E. Set

    Q(z)=K11(z)
          +z K10(z)(G0-zK00(z))^(-1)K01(z).           (5)

The inverse in (5) has p-integral power-series coefficients and unit constant determinant. The Schur complement now gives exactly

    chi(z)=u(z) det[E-zQ(z)],                          (6)

where

    u(z)=(-1)^k(det W)^(-2) det(J+zB)
                              det(G0-zK00(z)).

In particular u is a unit in Z_p[[z]]. For r=k the cross blocks are absent, Q=K, and the empty determinant convention applies.

This is an r-dimensional reduction, but its nonunit matrix E remains present. Replacing E by zero is justified only modulo specified powers of p, as below.

## 5. Exact finite all-depth content formula

Write

    f_j=[z^j]det[E-zQ(z)].

Then

    d=min_(0<=j<=k) v_p(f_j),
    v_p(A)=a-min_(0<=j<=k)v_p(f_j).                    (7)

Use v_p(0)=infinity. The minimum is finite because f_0=det E has valuation a.

Proof: multiplication by u(z) on coefficient vectors of degrees zero through k is a triangular invertible transformation over Z_p, with diagonal entry u(0). It preserves the ideal generated by these coefficients. The reversed polynomial chi has degree at most k, so that ideal is precisely the coefficient ideal of beta. Multiplication by the p-unit delta preserves its valuation, which is d. This proves (7).

Although the determinant in (6) may have an infinite power series, only its first k+1 coefficients are needed. The cutoff is justified by the triangular transformation and the degree of chi, not by discarding a remainder without an argument.

## 6. Reduction through the smallest nonunit pivot depth

Put

    q_j=[z^j]det Q(z).

Because every entry of E is divisible by p^alpha, determinant expansion gives

    det[E-zQ(z)] = (-z)^r det Q(z) mod p^alpha.

Every other term includes at least one entry of E. Combining this congruence with (7) proves

    min(d,alpha)
      =min(alpha, min_(0<=j<=k-r)v_p(q_j)).            (8)

In particular:

- d=0 exactly when one of q_0,...,q_(k-r) is a p-adic unit.
- If their minimum valuation is below alpha, that minimum equals d.
- If they are all divisible by p^alpha, then d>=alpha; the unreduced formula (7) is required for deeper information.

For r=1, alpha=a. Since d<=a, formula (8) determines the full content valuation:

    d=min(a, min_(0<=j<=k-1)v_p(q_j)).                 (9)

The intermediate power series depends on the chosen coordinates. Its truncated content in (7), and the capped value in (8), are invariant because they equal valuations of the physical coefficient vector.

## 7. A first-coefficient survival criterion for arbitrary nullity

Let Z be the final r columns of W. Their reductions form a basis of ker(G mod p). Equation (5) gives

    Q(0)=Z^T C J^(-1)D Z.                             (10)

If det Q(0) is nonzero modulo p, then (8) gives

    v_p(g)=0,  v_p(A)=v_p(F*_k).                      (11)

More explicitly, modulo p the coefficients of chi below degree r vanish, and

    [z^r]chi(z)
      =(-1)^(k+r)(det W)^(-2)
                    det J det G0 det Q(0) mod p.

Thus the actual coefficient beta_(k-r) is a p-adic unit. Changing the kernel basis changes (10) by congruence, so nonsingularity is intrinsic.

If Q(0) is singular, the first potentially surviving coefficient vanishes modulo p. Subsequent coefficients in (8) may still be units. Singularity of Q(0) alone therefore does not prove that p divides the complete coefficient gcd.

As a conditional global consequence,

    log A >= sum_p v_p(F*_k) log p,

where the sum runs only over primes p>6k dividing F*_k for which (10) is nonsingular modulo p. Every large prime dividing the actual content must fail this sufficient survival condition. Neither the size nor the valuation weight of that exceptional set is bounded here.

## 8. Actual high rational block and the remaining arithmetic target

The high rational block has an explicit endpoint formula. Writing

    c_k=4^k/binom(2k-2,k-1), N=k+i+j,

one has

    D_ij=-sum_(s=0)^N binom(N,s)(2s)!
          +c_k sum_(s=0)^(i+j) binom(i+j,s)/(2s+1).    (12)

Indeed h^k annihilates the full period jet, and multiplication of the rational kernel by h^(k+i+j) leaves the polynomial (1+x^2)^(i+j) under the compact integral. The exponential rational remainder is the first sum. Equation (12) therefore retains both rational endpoint contributions. The low block B remains necessary in (4)-(8), even though it does not occur in the first coefficient (10).

The useful new arithmetic question is whether the actual matrices force a unit among the coefficients in (8), or otherwise bound their common valuation. A proof must use the fixed moment and endpoint structure. Generic invertibility of G over Q, real positivity, a unit complement G0, or the displayed formal reduction is insufficient.

This document establishes no such uniform bound. Primes p<=6k require additional accounting for the physical clearer and jet denominators and lie outside its unit hypotheses. No prime scan, finite computation, independent review, or irrationality conclusion is claimed. The exact formulas specify a local interface for the actual final content; they do not replace the global content problem by an already solved one.
