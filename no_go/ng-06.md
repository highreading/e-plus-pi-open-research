Status: **recorded proved exclusion, scoped**. The proof and its historical internal review are available in full. Publication preparation did not rerun or newly certify the mathematics.

## Exact scope

All original admissibility conditions for b=3^(249005515+574312172u), n=2001b, d=b-1, h=2000b+1; complete corrected matrix and actual all-prime g and q.

## Exclusion

The complete primitive error is greater than 1000 sqrt(b) pointwise on the admissible domain. Every unbounded admissible collection fails primitive-error decay.

## Limits

This does not assert new infinitude of a constrained domain and does not exclude the compact mixed pencil, older prime-29 producer or continuing coordinate scratch.
