# NG-04 — Original raw diagonal family: all integer degrees

Status: **recorded proved exclusion, scoped**. The proof and its historical internal review are available in full. Publication preparation did not rerun or newly certify the mathematics.

## Exact scope

The original raw family, actual Z and N=Pe(1)+4Pa(1), full g=gcd(|Z|,|N|), both parities and all sufficiently large integer degrees.

## Exclusion

The actual primitive whole integer error tends to infinity, with an explicit exponential-over-polynomial lower bound. No subsequence of this family yields decay.

## Limits

This excludes this original family only. It does not decide rationality and does not transfer to another auxiliary.
