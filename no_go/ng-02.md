# NG-02 — Fixed sign-changing rational common beta kernels

Status: **recorded proved exclusion, scoped**. The proof and its historical internal review are available in full. Publication preparation did not rerun or newly certify the mathematics.

## Exact scope

A fixed pole-free rational kernel, the same beta weight, nonzero pi moment coefficient on the relevant infinite even set; kernel positivity is not assumed.

## Exclusion

Symmetrization, midpoint asymptotics, exponential coordinate height and complete matching content force primitive divergence.

## Limits

An n-dependent kernel or a different construction is not excluded.
