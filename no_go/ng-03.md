# NG-03 — Degree-one matched Hermite–Padé: all even indices

Status: **recorded proved exclusion, scoped**. The proof and its historical internal review are available in full. Publication preparation did not rerun or newly certify the mathematics.

## Exact scope

The literal (n,1,n) matched endpoint construction, actual completely reduced q_n, nonzero Delta, and all sufficiently large even n.

## Exclusion

Uniform prime-5/13 and dyadic denominator rates beat the complete endpoint error rate; every even subsequence fails primitive-error decay.

## Limits

Odd indices, growing exponential degree and other endpoint constructions are not excluded.
