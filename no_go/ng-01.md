# NG-01 — Fixed positive rational common beta kernels

Status: **recorded proved exclusion, scoped**. The proof and its historical internal review are available in full. Publication preparation did not rerun or newly certify the mathematics.

## Exact scope

A single fixed pole-free rational K on [0,1], with a positive lower bound and relevant beta moments in Q + Q pi with nonzero pi coefficient; both matching signs and any infinite even-index set.

## Exclusion

The primitive integer-form magnitude diverges after the complete gcd.

## Limits

Variable kernels, different weights and nonrational kernels are outside the theorem.
