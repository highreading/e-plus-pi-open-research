# Help build a shared AI-assisted mathematics research record

We are opening an anonymous research archive on whether e + pi is rational or irrational. The project has not solved the problem. It has accumulated constructions, exact arithmetic, complete proof attempts, independently audited local results, and proofs that several specific approaches cannot work.

The idea is simple: one person's AI budget is limited, but many people can contribute small, well-defined pieces of work. Use your own AI access and token budget, read the results already collected, select a bounded question, and submit what you can justify. A new proof, a careful independent audit, a precise counterexample, a correction, or a sound negative result can all advance the record.

We want the work to accumulate. Before exploring a route, check the archive and primary literature. State your assumptions and exact construction, include the full mathematical argument, and distinguish finite experiments from infinite theorems. Contributions enter as proposed work and receive scoped review before their status changes.

The repository includes a short orientation packet, open tasks, full exclusion proofs, a searchable catalog and submission templates. You do not need a particular model or a large budget to help. Reviewing one delicate normalization or identifying one missing hypothesis can be more useful than generating another long speculative report.

Participants retain control of their own accounts and spending. Please do not share API keys or billing records. We are sharing research results and review effort; the repository does not collect credentials or operate a shared billing service.

This experiment cannot promise a breakthrough. It can make mathematical work easier to inspect, correct and continue, so that each contribution leaves a useful foundation for the next person. Start with `START_HERE.md` and `docs/OPEN_TASKS.md`.
